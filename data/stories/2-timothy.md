# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** Paul, in prison in Rome near the end of his life (1:8, 4:6). Many believe it's the last letter he wrote.†

**Who it was for.** Timothy, Paul's young coworker, whom he calls **"my beloved child"** (1:2). Timothy was leading the church in Ephesus. He seems to have been timid and sometimes afraid (1:7).

**What this letter is.** A farewell from a spiritual father to his son. Paul knows he is about to die. He encourages Timothy to be brave, hold on to God's word, and keep preaching.

**How to read it.** Listen for how personal this is. Paul remembers Timothy's tears, his grandmother, and his mother.

## 🗺️ Map of the Letter

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Fan the Flame | 1:1-14 | God gave us power, love, and self-control |
| 2. The Word Cannot Be Chained | 2:8-15 | Even if we are faithless, he remains faithful |
| 3. All Scripture | 3:14-17 | God's word makes us wise for salvation |
| 4. Preach the Word | 4:1-5 | In season and out of season |
| 5. I Have Kept the Faith | 4:6-8, 4:16-18 | The Lord stood by me |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Fan the Flame

Paul writes to Timothy, **"my beloved child"** (1:2). {v: 1:1, 1:2}

He prays for Timothy night and day and remembers his tears. {v: 1:3, 1:4}

**"I am reminded of your sincere faith, which first dwelt in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am convinced is in you as well"** (1:5). {v: 1:5}

**"For this reason I remind you to fan into flame the gift of God"** (1:6). {v: 1:6}

**"For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control"** (1:7). {v: 1:7}

Don't be ashamed of Jesus or of Paul, who is in prison. {v: 1:8}

God **"has saved us and called us to a holy calling, not because of our works, but by His own purpose and by the grace He granted us in Christ Jesus"** (1:9). {v: 1:9}

Jesus **"has abolished death and illuminated the way to life and immortality through the gospel"** (1:10). {v: 1:10, 1:11}

**"For I know whom I have believed, and I am convinced that He is able to guard what I have entrusted to Him for that day"** (1:12). {v: 1:12}

Guard the good news God has given you, with the Holy Spirit's help. {v: 1:13, 1:14}

### Section 2: The Word Cannot Be Chained

**"Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, descended from David"** (2:8). {v: 2:8}

Paul is chained like a criminal. **"But the word of God cannot be chained!"** (2:9). {v: 2:9, 2:10}

Paul quotes a saying the early Christians knew: {v: 2:11}

**"If we died with Him, we will also live with Him; if we endure, we will also reign with Him"** (2:11-12). {v: 2:11, 2:12}

**"If we are faithless, He remains faithful, for He cannot deny Himself"** (2:13). {v: 2:13}

Don't fight over words. Handle God's word carefully and well. {v: 2:14, 2:15}

### Section 3: All Scripture

**"But as for you, continue in the things you have learned and firmly believed"** (3:14). {v: 3:14}

Timothy had known the Scriptures since he was a baby. They **"are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus"** (3:15). {v: 3:15}

**"All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for instruction, for conviction, for correction, and for training in righteousness"** (3:16). {v: 3:16, 3:17}

God-breathed means God is its true author. {v: 3:16}

### Section 4: Preach the Word

Paul gives Timothy a serious charge before God. {v: 4:1}

**"Preach the word; be prepared in season and out of season"** (4:2). {v: 4:2}

"In season and out of season" means when it's easy and when it's hard. {v: 4:2}

A time will come when people only want to hear what they like. {v: 4:3, 4:4}

**"But you, be sober in all things, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry"** (4:5). {v: 4:5}

### Section 5: I Have Kept the Faith

**"For I am already being poured out like a drink offering, and the time of my departure is at hand"** (4:6). {v: 4:6}

A drink offering was wine poured out on the altar. Paul is saying his life is almost poured out for God. {v: 4:6}

**"I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith"** (4:7). {v: 4:7}

A crown is waiting for him, **"and not only to me, but to all who crave His appearing"** (4:8). {v: 4:8}

At his trial, everyone deserted him. Paul forgives them. {v: 4:16}

**"But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me"** (4:17). {v: 4:17}

**"And the Lord will rescue me from every evil action and bring me safely into His heavenly kingdom"** (4:18). {v: 4:18}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

2 Timothy 1 to 4 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice"** (1:5) | Faith is often passed down in families. |
| **"Fan into flame the gift of God"** (1:6) | Use what God gave you. Don't let it go cold. |
| **"A spirit of fear"** (1:7) | God did not give us fear. His Spirit makes us brave and loving. |
| **"I know whom I have believed"** (1:12) | Faith trusts a person: Jesus. |
| **"The word of God cannot be chained"** (2:9) | No prison can stop God's word. |
| **"He remains faithful"** (2:13) | God keeps his promises even when we fail. |
| **"All Scripture is God-breathed"** (3:16) | The Bible is God's word. |
| **"I have kept the faith"** (4:7) | Paul held on to Jesus to the end. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Timothy's father was Greek** and his mother was Jewish (Acts 16:1). His mother and grandmother taught him the Scriptures.

**Paul was likely in a Roman prison** under the emperor Nero. Christian tradition says he was executed there.†

**"The Scriptures"** Timothy knew from childhood were what we call the Old Testament.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does "not a spirit of fear" mean Christians never feel afraid?** No. Timothy seems to have been afraid often. Paul reminds him that God's Spirit gives power and love even when we feel scared.

**If we deny him, he will deny us, but he remains faithful? Which is it?** Paul warns against turning away from Jesus for good. But even when our faith is weak, God stays faithful to his promises.

**Was Paul bragging when he said "I have kept the faith"?** No. He says it's the Lord who stood by him and strengthened him (4:17).

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Your faith is only as strong as you are."*

**What's true:** God calls us to hold on and keep the faith.

**What the passage is mainly about:** It all rests on God's faithfulness. **"If we are faithless, He remains faithful, for He cannot deny Himself"** (2:13). Paul kept the faith because the Lord stood by him.

**Where Jesus is:** Raised from the dead, standing by his people, and coming again.

## ✝️ Good News

Paul was alone in prison, about to die. But he wasn't really alone.

**"But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me"** (4:17).

God saved you by his grace, not your works. He guards what you entrust to him. And he remains faithful, always.

**God gives a spirit of power, love, and self-control. He saved us by grace, and even when we are faithless, he remains faithful and stands by us to the end.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that Scripture is the one sure guide for faith and life, because it is God's own word. He translated the Bible into German so ordinary people, like Timothy's grandmother and mother, could teach it at home.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads through 2 Timothy on four Sundays in October (Lectionary 27 to 30). Lutheran churches often read 2 Timothy 4:6-8 on the Feast of Saints Peter and Paul.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control. Hold on to God's word and keep the faith, because even when we are faithless, he remains faithful.**

# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Peter:** One of Jesus's apostles, traveling to visit the believers.
- **Aeneas:** A man in the town of Lydda, paralyzed for eight years.
- **Tabitha (also called Dorcas):** A follower of Jesus in Joppa, known for helping the poor.
- **The widows:** Women in Joppa whom Tabitha had helped.

**Setting**

- Lydda, a town near the coast, and Joppa, a seaside town nearby.

**When**

- A few years after Jesus rose from the dead.†

## 🧭 Before the Story

**The church was spreading.** It had grown through Judea, Galilee, and Samaria (9:31).

**Saul, who had hurt Christians, had met Jesus.** Now he was preaching about Jesus himself (9:20).

**Peter remembered Jesus raising a girl.** Jesus had said, "Little girl, get up!" (Mark 5:41). Peter had been in the room.

## 🗺️ Map of the Story

| Scene | Verses | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Aeneas | 9:32-35 | Peter heals a paralyzed man |
| 2. Tabitha | 9:36-38 | A kind woman dies, and the believers send for Peter |
| 3. The clothes she made | 9:39 | The widows show what Tabitha did |
| 4. "Tabitha, get up!" | 9:40-43 | Peter prays, and she lives |

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Aeneas

Peter visited the believers in Lydda. There he met Aeneas, who had been paralyzed and stuck in bed for eight years. {v: 9:32, 9:33}

**"Aeneas,"** Peter said, **"Jesus Christ heals you! Get up and put away your mat"** (9:34). {v: 9:34}

Right away, Aeneas got up. Everyone in the area saw him and turned to the Lord. {v: 9:34, 9:35}

### Scene 2: Tabitha

In nearby Joppa lived a disciple named Tabitha. In Greek her name was Dorcas. Both names mean "gazelle." {v: 9:36}

She was **"always occupied with works of kindness and charity"** (9:36). {v: 9:36}

Then she got sick and died. Her friends washed her body and laid it in an upstairs room. {v: 9:37}

They heard Peter was close by, so they sent two men: **"Come to us without delay"** (9:38). {v: 9:38}

### Scene 3: The Clothes She Made

Peter came right away. They took him upstairs. {v: 9:39}

The widows were all there, crying. They showed Peter the tunics and clothes Tabitha had made for them. {v: 9:39}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a room full of grieving women, each holding up a coat or a tunic. "She made this for me." "And this one." The clothes tell the story of her kindness.

### Scene 4: "Tabitha, Get Up!"

Peter sent everyone out of the room. He knelt and prayed. {v: 9:40}

Then he turned to her and said, **"Tabitha, get up!"** (9:40). {v: 9:40}

She opened her eyes. When she saw Peter, she sat up. {v: 9:40}

Peter took her hand and helped her up. Then he called the believers and the widows and showed them she was alive. {v: 9:41}

News spread all over Joppa, and many people believed in the Lord. {v: 9:42}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Acts 9:32-43 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Jesus Christ heals you!"** (9:34) | Peter made it clear the healing came from Jesus. |
| **"Always occupied with works of kindness and charity"** (9:36) | Tabitha served Jesus by helping the poor. |
| **"Showing him the tunics and other clothing"** (9:39) | Her love was visible in what she made. |
| **"He knelt down and prayed"** (9:40) | Peter depended on God, not himself. |
| **"Tabitha, get up!"** (9:40) | Jesus's power to raise the dead worked through Peter. |
| **"Many people believed in the Lord"** (9:42) | The miracle pointed people to Jesus. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Widows were often very poor.** Without a husband, many had no income. Making clothes for them was a big help.†

**Joppa was a port city**, today part of Tel Aviv. Jonah sailed from Joppa (Jonah 1:3), and Peter's vision of the sheet happened there next (10:9).

**Peter's words echo Jesus's.** Jesus said "Talitha koum," "Little girl, get up." Peter said "Tabitha, get up." The names sound alike in Aramaic.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Did Peter have special powers?** No. Peter prayed. Jesus did the healing (9:34).

**Will God raise everyone who dies right away?** Not right away. Tabitha later died again. These miracles are signs. Jesus promises everyone who belongs to him will rise on the last day.

**Is helping people as important as preaching?** Tabitha is called a disciple because of her kindness. Serving people is serving Jesus.

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The widows | Grieving | Their friend had died (9:39) |
| The believers | Urgent | They asked Peter to hurry (9:38) |
| Everyone | Amazed | Tabitha was alive (9:41-42) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Only preachers and leaders really serve God."*

**What's true:** God calls some people to lead and preach.

**What the story is mainly about:** Tabitha served God by sewing clothes for poor widows. The Bible calls her a disciple. Her quiet kindness mattered so much that the whole town knew.

**Where Jesus is:** Working through Peter's prayer, raising Tabitha, just as he once raised Jairus's daughter.

## ✝️ Good News

Tabitha spent her life making things for others. God gave her life back.

**"Tabitha, get up!"** (9:40).

Jesus is stronger than death. One day he will say "Get up!" to everyone who belongs to him.

**Tabitha was always doing kind things. When she died, Peter prayed and said, "Tabitha, get up!" and she lived. Jesus is stronger than death.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that ordinary work done in faith, like sewing clothes for neighbors, is holy work, as pleasing to God as anything done by priests.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads Acts 9:36-43 on the Fourth Sunday of Easter.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Tabitha was always helping the poor. When she died, Peter knelt and prayed and said, "Tabitha, get up!" Jesus is stronger than death.**

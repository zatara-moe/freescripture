# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** Paul wrote this short letter to Titus, a younger coworker he left on the island of Crete to lead the churches there (1:5).

**What was happening.** The churches in Crete were new. Titus needed to appoint leaders and teach people how to live as Christians (1:5, 2:1).†

**What this passage is.** Two short passages that the church reads at Christmas. Each one says God's grace and kindness "appeared." That's what Christmas is: God showing up.

**How to read it.** Look for what God did, and what that means for how we live.

## 🗺️ Map of the Letter

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Grace Has Appeared | 2:11-12 | God's grace brings salvation and teaches us |
| 2. The Blessed Hope | 2:13-14 | We wait for Jesus, who gave himself for us |
| 3. Kindness and Love Appeared | 3:4-5 | God saved us by his mercy |
| 4. Heirs of Hope | 3:6-7 | Justified by grace, we have eternal life |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Grace Has Appeared

**"For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to everyone"** (2:11). {v: 2:11}

Grace is God's undeserved love. It "appeared" when Jesus was born. {v: 2:11}

That grace teaches us to say no to wrong and to live wise, good, and godly lives right now. {v: 2:12}

### Section 2: The Blessed Hope

We wait for **"the blessed hope and glorious appearance of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ"** (2:13). {v: 2:13}

Jesus appeared once as a baby. He will appear again in glory. {v: 2:13}

**"He gave Himself for us to redeem us from all lawlessness and to purify for Himself a people for His own possession, zealous for good deeds"** (2:14). {v: 2:14}

Zealous means eager and full of energy. {v: 2:14}

### Section 3: Kindness and Love Appeared

**"But when the kindness of God our Savior and His love for mankind appeared, He saved us, not by the righteous deeds we had done, but according to His mercy"** (3:4-5). {v: 3:4, 3:5}

God saved us **"through the washing of new birth and renewal by the Holy Spirit"** (3:5). {v: 3:5}

The washing of new birth is baptism. {v: 3:5}

### Section 4: Heirs of Hope

God poured out his Spirit on us generously through Jesus. {v: 3:6}

**"So that, having been justified by His grace, we would become heirs with the hope of eternal life"** (3:7). {v: 3:7}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Titus 2:11-14 and 3:4-7 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"The grace of God has appeared"** (2:11) | In Jesus, God's love came into the world. |
| **"Bringing salvation to everyone"** (2:11) | God's grace is for all people. |
| **"The blessed hope"** (2:13) | Jesus will come again. |
| **"Our great God and Savior Jesus Christ"** (2:13) | Jesus is God. |
| **"Not by the righteous deeds we had done"** (3:5) | We are saved by mercy, not by being good. |
| **"The washing of new birth"** (3:5) | Baptism, where God makes us new. |
| **"Heirs with the hope of eternal life"** (3:7) | Eternal life is ours as God's children. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Crete** is a large island in the Mediterranean Sea, south of Greece.†

**Titus** was a Greek who became a Christian and traveled with Paul (Galatians 2:1-3).

**These verses are read at Christmas** because they say God's grace and kindness "appeared."

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does grace mean we can do whatever we want?** No. Paul says grace teaches us to live godly lives. Grace changes us.

**Is baptism just a symbol?** Lutherans believe baptism is God's work, a washing where he gives new birth and the Holy Spirit, as Titus says.

**What does "justified" mean?** Declared right with God.

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**What does Christmas mean to you?** Tap one.

- Grace appeared :: God's love showed up in a baby, for everyone.
- Mercy :: God saved you, not because of what you did.
- New birth :: In baptism, God washed you and made you new.
- Waiting :: Jesus will appear again. That's our blessed hope.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God saves good people."*

**What's true:** God wants us to do good.

**What the passage is mainly about:** **"He saved us, not by the righteous deeds we had done, but according to His mercy"** (3:5). Christmas is God's kindness appearing for people who didn't deserve it.

**Where Jesus is:** Born at Christmas, given for us, coming again in glory.

## ✝️ Good News

God's grace appeared. It came as a baby in Bethlehem.

**"For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to everyone"** (2:11).

God saved us by his mercy, washed us in baptism, and made us heirs of eternal life.

**The grace of God has appeared in Jesus. He saved us not because of our good deeds, but by his mercy, through the washing of new birth.**

## 📚 From Luther

In the Small Catechism, Luther quotes Titus 3:5-8 to explain how baptism gives new birth. He says it is not the water alone, but God's word with the water, and faith that trusts it.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It reads Titus 2:11-14 on Christmas Eve and Titus 3:4-7 on Christmas Day, in all three years.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**The grace and kindness of God appeared in Jesus. He saved us not by our good deeds but by his mercy, and we wait for his glorious return.**

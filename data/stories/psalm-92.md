# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**What it is.** The only psalm with a heading that links it to a specific day: **"A song for the Sabbath day."** On the Sabbath, the week's work is done and God is praised.

**What it's about.** Singing in the morning and at night. Marveling at God's works. The puzzle of the wicked flourishing — and why they don't last. The righteous growing old like a fruitful tree.

**When the church sings it.** On Sundays in Ordinary Time, especially in the summer.

**How to read it.** Notice the contrast: the wicked flourish like grass (which fades); the righteous flourish like a palm tree (which does not).

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: It Is Good to Praise

**"It is good to praise the LORD, and to sing praises to Your name, O Most High"** (92:1). {v: 92:1}

**"to proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning and Your faithfulness at night"** (92:2). {v: 92:2}

Morning and night — the day is framed with praise. {v: 92:2}

**"with the ten-stringed harp and the melody of the lyre"** (92:3). {v: 92:3}

**"For You, O LORD, have made me glad by Your deeds; I sing for joy at the works of Your hands"** (92:4). {v: 92:4}

### Section 2: How Deep Are Your Thoughts

**"How great are Your works, O LORD, how deep are Your thoughts!"** (92:5). {v: 92:5}

**"A senseless man does not know, and a fool does not understand,"** (92:6) **"that though the wicked sprout like grass, and all evildoers flourish, they will be forever destroyed"** (92:7). {v: 92:6, 92:7}

The wicked look like they are winning. They are not. {v: 92:6, 92:7}

**"But You, O LORD, are exalted forever!"** (92:8). {v: 92:8}

The LORD endures. The wicked do not. {v: 92:8, 92:9, 92:10, 92:11}

### Section 3: Like a Palm Tree

**"The righteous will flourish like a palm tree, and grow like a cedar in Lebanon"** (92:12). {v: 92:12}

Palm trees and cedars live long, grow tall, and do not wilt. {v: 92:12}

**"Planted in the house of the LORD, they will flourish in the courts of our God"** (92:13). {v: 92:13}

**"In old age they will still bear fruit; healthy and green they will remain,"** (92:14) to proclaim, **"The LORD is upright; He is my Rock, and in Him there is no unrighteousness."** (92:15). {v: 92:14, 92:15}

The last word is not silence but proclamation — even in old age. {v: 92:15}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Psalm 92 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"It is good to praise the LORD"** (92:1) | Praise is not a burden; it is healthy and right. |
| **"to proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning and Your faithfulness at night"** (92:2) | The whole day is wrapped in what God is like. |
| **"How deep are Your thoughts!"** (92:5) | God's wisdom runs deeper than our understanding. |
| **"The wicked sprout like grass"** (92:7) | Grass looks green but does not last. |
| **"Like a palm tree"** (92:12) | Long life, deep roots, and continuing fruitfulness. |
| **"In old age they will still bear fruit"** (92:14) | Life with God does not fade into uselessness. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**The Sabbath** was the seventh day, set apart for rest and worship. This psalm was sung in the temple on that day. In Christian tradition, Sunday became the "eighth day" — a new week, a new creation day, resurrection day.

**Palm trees** in the ancient Near East were symbols of victory and long life.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**The wicked flourish — does this psalm mean they always lose?** Not immediately. The psalm is honest: evil people sometimes succeed for a while. But they are like grass — temporary. God endures.

**"Bearing fruit in old age"** — this is not a promise of physical vigor. It is a promise of continued fruitfulness: wisdom, witness, and praise.

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**What does this psalm speak into?** Tap one.

- I need a reason to praise :: It is good to praise — not because everything is easy, but because God is great.
- I see the wicked winning :: They are like grass. God is exalted forever.
- I'm getting older :: In old age they will still bear fruit.
- I feel planted :: In the house of the LORD, they flourish.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"As you get older, you matter less."*

**What's true:** Physical strength declines.

**What the passage is mainly about:** **"In old age they will still bear fruit; healthy and green they will remain"** (92:14). The Sabbath psalm ends with older trees still bearing fruit and still declaring God's faithfulness. Maturity in faith is its own kind of fruitfulness.

**Where Jesus is:** He rose on the first day of the week — the Sabbath is now resurrection day. He is the Rock in whom there is no unrighteousness (92:15).

## ✝️ Good News

It is good to praise the LORD.

**"In old age they will still bear fruit; healthy and green they will remain"** (92:14).

You are planted in the house of the LORD. You will flourish.

**Psalm 92, the Sabbath song, frames the whole day with praise and ends with a promise: the righteous, planted in God's house, bear fruit even in old age.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther said the Sabbath rest points to the eternal rest God gives — and the righteous person's fruitfulness points forward to the fullness of life in God's presence.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It sings Psalm 92 on the Eighth Sunday after Pentecost in Year C and on other Sundays in Ordinary Time.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Psalm 92, the Sabbath song: it is good to praise the LORD morning and evening. The wicked are like grass; the righteous are like palm trees — planted in God's house, bearing fruit even in old age.**

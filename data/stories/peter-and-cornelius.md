# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Cornelius:** A Roman army officer, a centurion, who believes in God and prays. He is not Jewish.
- **Peter:** One of Jesus's first disciples, now a leader of the church.
- **An angel:** A messenger from God.
- **Cornelius's family and friends:** People gathered in his house to listen.
- **Believers from Joppa:** Jewish Christians who travel with Peter.

**Setting**

- Caesarea, a Roman city on the coast, and Joppa, a seaside town about thirty miles south.

**When**

- A few years after Jesus rose from the dead and went up to heaven.†

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus told his followers to go to all nations.** **"You will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth"** (1:8).

**So far, the church was mostly Jewish.** The good news had spread from Jerusalem to Samaria (8:5).

**Jewish people kept apart from non-Jews.** They followed God's laws about clean and unclean foods. Many would not eat with or visit a non-Jew's home.†

**Here's what you know that Peter doesn't:** God is about to open the door wide.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: An Angel Visits Cornelius

In Caesarea lived a man named Cornelius, a Roman centurion. {v: 10:1}

He and his whole household worshiped God. He gave generously to the poor and prayed to God regularly. {v: 10:2}

One afternoon, he saw an angel in a vision. **"Cornelius!"** the angel said (10:3). {v: 10:3}

Cornelius stared at him in fear. The angel said his prayers and gifts had been noticed by God. {v: 10:4}

The angel told him to send for a man named Simon Peter, staying in Joppa. So Cornelius sent two servants and a soldier. {v: 10:5, 10:7, 10:8}

### Scene 2: A Sheet From Heaven

The next day, about noon, Peter went up on the flat roof of the house to pray. He got hungry. {v: 10:9, 10:10}

Then he had a vision. Heaven opened, and something like a big sheet came down, held by its four corners. {v: 10:11}

It was full of all kinds of animals, reptiles, and birds. Many were animals God's law called unclean. {v: 10:12}

A voice said, **"Get up, Peter, kill and eat!"** (10:13). {v: 10:13}

**"No, Lord!"** Peter answered. **"I have never eaten anything impure or unclean"** (10:14). {v: 10:14}

**"Do not call anything impure that God has made clean"** (10:15). {v: 10:15}

This happened three times. Then the sheet went back up to heaven. {v: 10:16}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture Peter on a rooftop by the sea, stomach growling, staring up at a sheet full of creatures he has never eaten in his life. He shakes his head. The voice speaks again.

### Scene 3: Three Men at the Gate

While Peter was puzzling over the vision, Cornelius's men arrived at the gate. {v: 10:17}

The Spirit said to Peter, "Three men are looking for you. Go with them without hesitation, because I have sent them." {v: 10:19, 10:20}

So Peter invited them in as guests. The next day, he went with them. {v: 10:23}

### Scene 4: "I Am Only a Man"

Cornelius had gathered his relatives and close friends. When Peter arrived, Cornelius fell at his feet. {v: 10:24, 10:25}

**"Stand up,"** Peter said, **"I am only a man myself"** (10:26). {v: 10:26}

Peter went in and found many people. He said, "You know it's against our law for a Jew to visit someone from another nation. **But God has shown me that I should not call any man impure or unclean**" (10:28). {v: 10:27, 10:28}

Now Peter understood the vision. It wasn't only about food. It was about people. {v: 10:28}

Cornelius told Peter about the angel. **"Now then, we are all here in the presence of God to listen"** (10:33). {v: 10:30, 10:31, 10:32, 10:33}

### Scene 5: No Favoritism

Peter began to speak. **"I now truly understand that God does not show favoritism, but welcomes those from every nation who fear Him and do what is right"** (10:34-35). {v: 10:34, 10:35}

He told them about Jesus, who is **"Lord of all"** (10:36). {v: 10:36}

God anointed Jesus with the Holy Spirit and power. Jesus went around doing good and healing. {v: 10:38}

They put him to death on a cross, but **"God raised Him up on the third day"** (10:40). {v: 10:39, 10:40}

**"Everyone who believes in Him receives forgiveness of sins through His name"** (10:43). {v: 10:43}

### Scene 6: The Spirit Falls

While Peter was still speaking, the Holy Spirit came on everyone listening. {v: 10:44}

The Jewish believers with Peter were astonished. God had given the Holy Spirit to non-Jews too! {v: 10:45}

They heard them praising God in other languages. {v: 10:46}

Peter said, **"Can anyone withhold the water to baptize these people? They have received the Holy Spirit just as we have!"** (10:47). {v: 10:47}

So they were baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. {v: 10:48}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Acts 10 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Your prayers and gifts to the poor have ascended"** (10:4) | God noticed Cornelius, an outsider. |
| **"Do not call anything impure that God has made clean"** (10:15) | God was changing the rules about who belongs. |
| **"I am only a man myself"** (10:26) | Peter wouldn't let anyone worship him. |
| **"God does not show favoritism"** (10:34) | God welcomes people from every nation. |
| **"Lord of all"** (10:36) | Jesus is Lord of every people, not just one. |
| **"Everyone who believes in Him receives forgiveness"** (10:43) | Forgiveness is for everyone who trusts Jesus. |
| **"Can anyone withhold the water"** (10:47) | Nothing should keep them from being baptized. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Caesarea was the Roman capital of Judea.** It was a big, mostly non-Jewish city built by Herod the Great.†

**God's food laws are in Leviticus 11.** They set Israel apart from other nations.†

**Peter told this story to the church in Jerusalem.** At first, some criticized him for eating with non-Jews. When he explained, they praised God (11:1-18).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Was Cornelius already saved, since he prayed?** He believed in God and prayed. But he needed to hear about Jesus, and God sent Peter to tell him. The angel didn't preach. God sent a person with the message.†

**Do Christians have to follow the food laws?** This vision showed that the food laws no longer divided God's people. Christians may eat any food (Mark 7:19).

**What does "speaking in tongues" mean here?** They praised God in languages they hadn't learned, like on the day of Pentecost (2:4). It showed the Spirit had really come.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cornelius | Afraid | An angel appeared to him (10:4) |
| Peter | Confused | He didn't understand the vision (10:17) |
| Cornelius | Eager | He gathered everyone to listen (10:24, 10:33) |
| The Jewish believers | Astounded | The Spirit came to non-Jews (10:45) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Christianity is for certain kinds of people."*

**What's true:** God chose Israel and worked through one people to bless the world.

**What the story is mainly about:** Through Jesus, the door opened wide. **"God does not show favoritism"** (10:34). Jesus is Lord of all, and the Holy Spirit came to people from every nation.

**Where Jesus is:** Peter preached that Jesus is Lord of all, died, and rose. Through his name, everyone who believes is forgiven.

## ✝️ Good News

A Roman soldier and his family, outsiders, heard the good news. The Holy Spirit came on them, and they were baptized.

**"I now truly understand that God does not show favoritism"** (10:34).

Jesus is Lord of all. His forgiveness is for every nation, every family, and you.

**God showed Peter that no one is outside his welcome. Jesus is Lord of all, and the Holy Spirit came to people of every nation.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther pointed to this story to show that the Holy Spirit comes through the preached word. While Peter was speaking, the Spirit fell on those who heard.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It reads Acts 10:34-43 on Easter Day in all three years and on the Baptism of Our Lord in Year A. In Year B, it reads Acts 10:44-48 on the Sixth Sunday of Easter.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**God sent Peter to the home of Cornelius, a Roman soldier. Peter learned that God shows no favoritism, and the Holy Spirit came on people of every nation.**

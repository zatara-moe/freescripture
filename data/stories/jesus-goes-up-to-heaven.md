# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God. He has risen from the dead.
- **The apostles:** Jesus's closest followers. There are eleven now.
- **Two men in white:** Messengers from God.
- **Mary and the believers:** Jesus's mother, his brothers, and other followers, about 120 people.
- **Matthias:** A follower of Jesus chosen to join the apostles.

**Setting**

- The Mount of Olives, a hill just outside Jerusalem. Then an upstairs room in the city.

**When**

- Forty days after Jesus rose from the dead, about 2,000 years ago.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus rose from the dead on Easter.** He appeared to his disciples many times (Luke 24:36, John 20:19).

**Luke wrote two books.** His Gospel tells what Jesus did on earth. Acts tells what Jesus kept doing through his followers. Acts begins where the Gospel ends.

**One apostle was gone.** Judas Iscariot had betrayed Jesus, and he had died (Matthew 27:5).

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Forty Days

After Jesus suffered and rose, he showed himself to the apostles **"with many convincing proofs that He was alive"** (1:3). {v: 1:1, 1:2, 1:3}

For forty days he appeared to them and taught them about the kingdom of God. {v: 1:3}

He told them, **"Do not leave Jerusalem, but wait for the gift the Father promised"** (1:4). {v: 1:4}

**"For John baptized with water, but in a few days you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit"** (1:5). {v: 1:5}

### Scene 2: "You Will Be My Witnesses"

They asked, **"Lord, will You at this time restore the kingdom to Israel?"** (1:6). {v: 1:6}

Jesus said it wasn't for them to know when. {v: 1:7}

**"But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you, and you will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth"** (1:8). {v: 1:8}

A witness is someone who tells what they have seen. {v: 1:8}

### Scene 3: Taken Up

**"After He had said this, they watched as He was taken up, and a cloud hid Him from their sight"** (1:9). {v: 1:9}

They kept staring up into the sky. {v: 1:10}

Suddenly two men dressed in white stood beside them. {v: 1:10}

**"Men of Galilee,"** they said, **"why do you stand here looking into the sky? This same Jesus, who has been taken from you into heaven, will come back in the same way you have seen Him go into heaven"** (1:11). {v: 1:11}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture eleven men on a hillside, heads tipped back, shading their eyes, still looking at an empty sky. Then two strangers in white clear their throats.

### Scene 4: Waiting and Praying

They walked back to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives. {v: 1:12}

They went to the upstairs room where they were staying. {v: 1:13}

**"With one accord they all continued in prayer, along with the women and Mary the mother of Jesus, and with His brothers"** (1:14). With one accord means together, united. {v: 1:14}

### Scene 5: A New Apostle

About 120 believers were gathered. Peter stood up. {v: 1:15}

Judas, who had been one of the twelve, had betrayed Jesus and died. Someone needed to take his place. {v: 1:16, 1:17}

It had to be someone who had been with Jesus from his baptism to the day he went up, a witness of his resurrection. {v: 1:21, 1:22}

They named two men, Joseph called Barsabbas and Matthias. {v: 1:23}

They prayed, **"Lord, You know everyone's heart. Show us which of these two You have chosen"** (1:24). {v: 1:24}

They cast lots, a way of drawing names, and Matthias was chosen. {v: 1:26}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Acts 1 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Many convincing proofs that He was alive"** (1:3) | Jesus's resurrection was real. |
| **"Wait for the gift the Father promised"** (1:4) | The Holy Spirit was coming. |
| **"You will receive power"** (1:8) | The Spirit would give them strength. |
| **"To the ends of the earth"** (1:8) | The good news is for the whole world. |
| **"A cloud hid Him from their sight"** (1:9) | Jesus went to be with the Father. |
| **"This same Jesus... will come back"** (1:11) | Jesus will return. |
| **"They all continued in prayer"** (1:14) | They waited by praying together. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**A Sabbath day's journey** was about two-thirds of a mile. The Mount of Olives was close to the city.†

**Casting lots** was an old way of letting God decide, like drawing straws. After the Holy Spirit came, we never read of the apostles casting lots again.†

**Ascension Day** is celebrated forty days after Easter, always on a Thursday.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Did Jesus fly up into space?** The Bible says he was taken up and a cloud hid him. It means he went to be with the Father, to rule over all things. Heaven isn't a place in outer space.†

**If Jesus went away, is he gone?** No. He promised, **"I am with you always"** (Matthew 28:20). He is with us by his Spirit, in his word, and in the Lord's Supper.

**When will he come back?** Jesus said no one knows the time (1:7). We can trust that he will.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Jesus left, so now we're on our own."*

**What's true:** Jesus isn't walking on earth the way he did before.

**What the story is mainly about:** Jesus went up to rule over everything. He promised the Holy Spirit, and he promised to come back. We're not alone. We're his witnesses, with his power.

**Where Jesus is:** At the right hand of the Father, ruling over all things, praying for us, and coming back.

## ✝️ Good News

Jesus didn't just disappear. He went up to reign, and he left a promise.

**"This same Jesus, who has been taken from you into heaven, will come back in the same way you have seen Him go into heaven"** (1:11).

Jesus rules now, sends his Spirit, and will come again.

**After forty days, Jesus was taken up into heaven. He promised the Holy Spirit and sent his followers to be his witnesses. He will come back.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that Christ's going up to heaven doesn't mean he is far away. "The right hand of God" is everywhere. Christ is present wherever he has promised to be.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It reads Acts 1:1-11 on the Ascension of Our Lord in all three years. In Year A, it reads Acts 1:6-14 on the Seventh Sunday of Easter. In Year B, it reads Acts 1:15-17 and 21-26 that Sunday.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Forty days after Easter, Jesus was taken up into heaven. He promised the Holy Spirit, sent his followers to be his witnesses, and will come back.**

# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** Daniel was a young man from Jerusalem, taken to Babylon in exile. He served kings there and stayed faithful to God (Daniel 1:1-6).

**What kind of writing this is.** The second half of Daniel is full of visions, dreams with symbols: beasts, horns, and thrones. This kind of writing is called apocalyptic. The word means "unveiling." It pulls back the curtain to show what God is doing behind history.†

**What comes before.** In chapter 7, Daniel sees four terrifying beasts come out of the sea. They stand for cruel empires that crush God's people (7:17).

**How to read it.** Don't get lost in every symbol. Watch who is on the throne, and who receives the kingdom.

## 🗺️ Map of the Message

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Ancient of Days | 7:9-10 | God sits on his throne as judge |
| 2. The Beast Is Destroyed | 7:11-12 | The cruel empires lose their power |
| 3. One Like a Son of Man | 7:13-14 | Someone receives an everlasting kingdom |
| 4. Those Who Sleep Will Awake | 12:1-3 | God's people will be delivered and raised |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: The Ancient of Days

**"As I continued to watch, thrones were set in place, and the Ancient of Days took His seat"** (7:9). {v: 7:9}

The Ancient of Days is God, who has always existed. {v: 7:9}

His clothes were white as snow. His hair was like pure wool. His throne blazed with fire. {v: 7:9}

Thousands upon thousands stood before him. **"The court was convened, and the books were opened"** (7:10). {v: 7:10}

God sits as judge over all the earth. {v: 7:10}

### Section 2: The Beast Is Destroyed

One of the beasts had been boasting with arrogant words. {v: 7:11}

Now it was destroyed. The other beasts lost their power. {v: 7:11, 7:12}

The empires that seemed so strong could not stand before God. {v: 7:12}

### Section 3: One Like a Son of Man

Then Daniel saw something else. {v: 7:13}

**"I saw One like the Son of Man coming with the clouds of heaven. He approached the Ancient of Days and was led into His presence"** (7:13). {v: 7:13}

"Son of Man" means someone who looks like a human being. But he comes with the clouds, like God. {v: 7:13}

**"And He was given dominion, glory, and kingship, that the people of every nation and language should serve Him"** (7:14). Dominion means the right to rule. {v: 7:14}

**"His dominion is an everlasting dominion that will not pass away, and His kingdom is one that will never be destroyed"** (7:14). {v: 7:14}

> 🧵 **Follow the thread**
> "Son of Man" was Jesus's favorite name for himself. At his trial, he told the high priest, **"You will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power and coming with the clouds of heaven"** (Mark 14:62).

### Section 4: Those Who Sleep Will Awake

Near the end of the book, an angel tells Daniel about the last days. {v: 12:1}

There will be great trouble. **"But at that time your people—everyone whose name is found written in the book—will be delivered"** (12:1). {v: 12:1}

**"And many who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake, some to everlasting life"** (12:2). {v: 12:2}

This is one of the clearest promises of the resurrection in the Old Testament. {v: 12:2}

**"Then the wise will shine like the brightness of the heavens, and those who lead many to righteousness will shine like the stars forever and ever"** (12:3). {v: 12:3}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Daniel 7:9-14 and 12:1-3 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"The Ancient of Days took His seat"** (7:9) | God is on his throne, no matter what empires do. |
| **"The books were opened"** (7:10) | God sees everything and will judge justly. |
| **"One like the Son of Man"** (7:13) | A human-like figure who comes with God's glory. |
| **"His kingdom is one that will never be destroyed"** (7:14) | His rule lasts forever. |
| **"Everyone whose name is found written in the book"** (12:1) | God knows his people by name. |
| **"Many who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake"** (12:2) | God will raise the dead. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Daniel lived under Babylon and then Persia.** He saw empires rise and fall.†

**The four beasts** are usually understood as four empires, possibly Babylon, Media, Persia, and Greece.†

**Michael** is named as an archangel who protects God's people (Jude 1:9, Revelation 12:7).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does God have white hair?** This is a vision with symbols. White hair shows God is ancient and wise. White clothes show he is pure.

**Who is the Son of Man?** Daniel doesn't name him. Jesus called himself the Son of Man and said he would come with the clouds (Mark 14:62).

**Is this scary?** The beasts are scary. But the vision is good news: God is on the throne, and his King's kingdom will never end.

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Daniel | Troubled | The beasts were terrifying (7:15) |
| God's people | Afraid | Empires were crushing them (7:17) |
| God's people | Hopeful | They will be delivered and raised (12:1-2) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"The most powerful nations decide what happens in the world."*

**What's true:** Nations and leaders have real power, and their choices matter.

**What the passage is mainly about:** Empires rise and fall like beasts. But the Ancient of Days is on the throne, and he gives an everlasting kingdom to the Son of Man.

**Where Jesus is:** Jesus is the Son of Man. He rose from the dead and ascended with the clouds. He will come again, and his kingdom has no end.

## ✝️ Good News

The beasts looked powerful. They didn't last.

**"His dominion is an everlasting dominion that will not pass away, and His kingdom is one that will never be destroyed"** (7:14).

Jesus, the Son of Man, rules forever. His people's names are written in his book, and he will wake them from the dust.

**God is on the throne. He gives an everlasting kingdom to one like a Son of Man, and he will raise his people to everlasting life.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther spent much time on Daniel's visions. He said they comfort Christians: however powerful the world's empires seem, Christ's kingdom will outlast them all.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Daniel 7:9-10 and 13-14 on Christ the King Sunday, and Daniel 12:1-3 the week before (Lectionary 33).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**The Ancient of Days is on his throne. He gives one like a Son of Man a kingdom that will never be destroyed, and those who sleep in the dust will awake.**

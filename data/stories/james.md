# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** James, most likely the brother of Jesus, who became a leader of the church in Jerusalem (Acts 15:13, Galatians 1:19).† He didn't believe in Jesus at first (John 7:5), but after the resurrection, Jesus appeared to him (1 Corinthians 15:7).

**Who it was for.** Jewish Christians scattered across many lands (1:1). Many were poor and mistreated by the rich.

**What this letter is.** Short, punchy, and practical, a lot like the book of Proverbs, or like Jesus's Sermon on the Mount. James asks: does your faith show up in real life? How you treat the poor, what you say, how you handle trouble.

**How to read it.** This page walks through all five chapters, picking up the passages read in church. Listen for how often James sounds like Jesus.

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Joy in Trials

**"Consider it pure joy, my brothers, when you encounter trials of many kinds, because you know that the testing of your faith develops perseverance"** (1:2-3). {v: 1:1, 1:2, 1:3, 1:4}

Perseverance means not giving up. {v: 1:3}

**"Now if any of you lacks wisdom, he should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault, and it will be given to him"** (1:5). {v: 1:5, 1:6, 1:7, 1:8}

Rich people fade like wildflowers. The poor have a high place with God. {v: 1:9, 1:10, 1:11}

God never tempts anyone to sin. Temptation comes from our own wrong desires. {v: 1:12, 1:13, 1:14, 1:15, 1:16}

**"Every good and perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of the heavenly lights, with whom there is no change or shifting shadow"** (1:17). {v: 1:17}

God gave us new birth through his word. {v: 1:18}

### Section 2: Doers of the Word

**"Everyone should be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to anger"** (1:19). {v: 1:19, 1:20, 1:21}

**"Be doers of the word, and not hearers only"** (1:22). {v: 1:22}

Hearing God's word and not doing it is like looking in a mirror and forgetting your own face. {v: 1:23, 1:24, 1:25}

**"Pure and undefiled religion before our God and Father is this: to care for orphans and widows in their distress, and to keep oneself from being polluted by the world"** (1:27). {v: 1:26, 1:27}

### Section 3: No Favorites

**"My brothers, as you hold out your faith in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ, do not show favoritism"** (2:1). {v: 2:1}

Imagine a rich man in fine clothes and a poor man in rags walking into church. If you give the rich man the best seat and tell the poor man to stand, something is very wrong. {v: 2:2, 2:3, 2:4}

**"Has not God chosen the poor of this world to be rich in faith and to inherit the kingdom He promised those who love Him?"** (2:5). {v: 2:5, 2:6, 2:7}

The royal law is to love your neighbor as yourself. {v: 2:8, 2:9, 2:10, 2:11, 2:12}

**"Mercy triumphs over judgment"** (2:13). {v: 2:13}

### Section 4: Faith That Works

**"What good is it, my brothers, if someone claims to have faith, but has no deeds?"** (2:14). {v: 2:14}

Suppose someone is cold and hungry, and you just say, "Stay warm and eat well!" and walk away. What good is that? {v: 2:15, 2:16}

**"So too, faith by itself, if it does not result in action, is dead"** (2:17). {v: 2:17}

Even demons believe God exists. That isn't saving faith. {v: 2:18, 2:19, 2:20}

Abraham's faith showed in what he did. So did Rahab's. {v: 2:21, 2:22, 2:23, 2:24, 2:25}

**"As the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without deeds is dead"** (2:26). {v: 2:26}

### Section 5: Taming the Tongue

Teachers will be judged more strictly. We all stumble, especially with words. {v: 3:1, 3:2}

A small bit steers a horse. A small rudder steers a ship. {v: 3:3, 3:4}

**"Consider how small a spark sets a great forest ablaze"** (3:5). {v: 3:5, 3:6}

People can tame wild animals, **"but no man can tame the tongue"** (3:8). {v: 3:7, 3:8}

**"Out of the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers, this should not be!"** (3:10). {v: 3:9, 3:10, 3:11, 3:12}

### Section 6: Wisdom From Above

Real wisdom shows itself in humble, good actions. Jealousy and selfish ambition create chaos. {v: 3:13, 3:14, 3:15, 3:16}

**"But the wisdom from above is first of all pure, then peace-loving, gentle, accommodating, full of mercy and good fruit, impartial, and sincere"** (3:17). {v: 3:17, 3:18}

Fights come from wanting what we don't have. **"You do not have, because you do not ask"** (4:2). {v: 4:1, 4:2, 4:3, 4:4, 4:5}

**"God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble"** (4:6). {v: 4:6}

**"Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you"** (4:7-8). {v: 4:7, 4:8, 4:9}

**"Humble yourselves before the Lord, and He will exalt you"** (4:10). {v: 4:10}

### Section 7: If the Lord Wills

Don't judge each other. God is the only Judge. {v: 4:11, 4:12}

Some people plan their year as if they control it. **"You do not even know what will happen tomorrow!"** (4:14). {v: 4:13, 4:14}

**"What is your life? You are a mist that appears for a little while and then vanishes"** (4:14). {v: 4:14}

So say, "If the Lord is willing, we will live and do this or that." {v: 4:15, 4:16, 4:17}

James warns rich people who cheated their workers. God hears the cries of the poor. {v: 5:1, 5:2, 5:3, 5:4, 5:5, 5:6}

### Section 8: Patience and Prayer

**"Be patient, then, brothers, until the Lord's coming"** (5:7). {v: 5:7}

A farmer waits patiently for rain. Be patient too, because the Lord is coming soon. {v: 5:7, 5:8, 5:9}

Remember the prophets and Job, who held on. **"The Lord is full of compassion and mercy"** (5:11). {v: 5:10, 5:11, 5:12}

**"Is any one of you suffering? He should pray. Is anyone cheerful? He should sing praises"** (5:13). {v: 5:13}

If someone is sick, call the church leaders to pray and anoint them with oil. {v: 5:14, 5:15}

**"Therefore confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous man has great power to prevail"** (5:16). {v: 5:16}

Elijah was a regular person, and God answered his prayers. {v: 5:17, 5:18}

If someone wanders away, bring them back. {v: 5:19, 5:20}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

James 1 to 5 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Consider it pure joy"** (1:2) | God can use hard times to make our faith strong. |
| **"Every good and perfect gift is from above"** (1:17) | Every good thing comes from God. |
| **"Be doers of the word"** (1:22) | Live out what you hear. |
| **"Do not show favoritism"** (2:1) | Treat rich and poor the same. |
| **"Faith without deeds is dead"** (2:26) | Living faith always shows itself in love. |
| **"No man can tame the tongue"** (3:8) | Words are powerful and hard to control. |
| **"Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you"** (4:8) | God welcomes anyone who turns to him. |
| **"You are a mist"** (4:14) | Life is short, so trust God with tomorrow. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**James led the church in Jerusalem.** He spoke at the big meeting about whether Gentiles had to keep the law (Acts 15).

**Many early Christians were poor.** Rich landowners sometimes held back workers' pay (5:4).

**Anointing with oil** was used as medicine and as a sign of God's blessing.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does James disagree with Paul?** Paul says we are made right with God by faith, not works (Romans 3:28). James says a "faith" with no love isn't real faith. Paul is talking about how we are saved. James is talking about what saving faith looks like. Both say faith is alive and active.

**Should I be happy when bad things happen?** James doesn't say trials are good. He says God can grow something good in us through them.

**If I pray, will every sick person be healed?** James says God hears prayer and raises up the sick. Sometimes that's healing now, and always it will be on the last day.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"As long as you believe the right things, it doesn't matter how you live."*

**What's true:** We are saved by grace through faith, not by our works.

**What the passage is mainly about:** Real faith is alive. It feeds the hungry, honors the poor, and guards its words. **"Faith by itself, if it does not result in action, is dead"** (2:17).

**Where Jesus is:** He is "our glorious Lord Jesus Christ" (2:1), and his coming is near (5:8).

## ✝️ Good News

James is tough. But he also says God gives more grace.

**"God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble"** (4:6).

Every good gift comes from God. He draws near when we draw near, and the Lord is full of compassion and mercy.

**Living faith shows itself in love. Every good and perfect gift comes from God, who gives grace to the humble and is full of compassion and mercy.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther once called James "an epistle of straw" compared to books like Romans and John, because it says little about Jesus's death and resurrection. But he also praised it for its strong teaching about God's law, and the Lutheran confessions quote it.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads through James on five Sundays in September (Lectionary 22 to 26). In Year A, James 5:7-10 is read on the Third Sunday of Advent.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Be doers of the word, not hearers only. Living faith shows itself in love: honoring the poor, taming the tongue, and praying for each other, trusting God, who gives every good gift and gives grace to the humble.**

# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God. He has just begun his work in Galilee.
- **Simon, Andrew, James, and John:** Four fishermen. Jesus called them to follow him a short time ago.
- **A man with an unclean spirit:** A man in the synagogue who is controlled by an evil spirit.
- **Simon's mother-in-law:** The mother of Simon's wife. She is sick with a fever.
- **The people of Capernaum:** The whole town, who come to the door that evening.

**Setting**

- Capernaum, a fishing town on the north shore of the Sea of Galilee. First the synagogue, then Simon and Andrew's house.

**When**

- One Sabbath day and the next morning, about 2,000 years ago.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus had just started preaching.** He said, **"The kingdom of God is near. Repent and believe in the gospel!"** (1:15). The kingdom of God means God's rule, the way God runs things. To repent means to turn back to God.

**Jesus called four fishermen.** Simon, Andrew, James, and John left their nets to follow him (1:16-20).

**Before that, Jesus was tested in the wilderness.** For forty days he was tempted by Satan, God's enemy (1:12-13).

**Here's what you know that the people in Capernaum don't:** At Jesus's baptism, God said, **"You are My beloved Son"** (1:11). Jesus is God's Son. The people in town are about to see what that means.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: A New Kind of Teacher

Jesus and his four new followers went to Capernaum. {v: 1:21}

On the Sabbath, Jesus went into the synagogue and began to teach. The Sabbath is the seventh day of the week, a day of rest and worship. A synagogue is a place where people met to pray and hear God's word. {v: 1:21}

The people were astonished at his teaching. Astonished means very surprised. {v: 1:22}

Jesus taught **"as one who had authority, and not as the scribes"** (1:22). The scribes were teachers of God's law. They often explained what other teachers had said. Jesus spoke as someone who had the right to say what God means. {v: 1:22}

### Scene 2: A Shout in the Synagogue

Suddenly a man in the synagogue cried out. He had an unclean spirit. An unclean spirit is an evil spirit, a demon. {v: 1:23}

**"What do You want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have You come to destroy us? I know who You are—the Holy One of God!"** (1:24). {v: 1:24}

The spirit knew who Jesus was. It was afraid of him. {v: 1:24}

Jesus spoke sharply to the spirit. **"Be silent!"** he said. **"Come out of him!"** (1:25). {v: 1:25}

The spirit shook the man hard and came out of him with a loud scream. {v: 1:26}

The spirit was gone. The man was free. {v: 1:26}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a quiet room full of people sitting on stone benches. A scream breaks the silence. Then the room is quiet again, and everyone stares.

### Scene 3: "What Is This?"

Everyone was amazed. They asked each other, **"What is this? A new teaching with authority! He commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey Him!"** (1:27). {v: 1:27}

Jesus did not shout or fight. He only spoke, and the spirit had to obey. {v: 1:27}

News about Jesus spread quickly through the whole area of Galilee. {v: 1:28}

### Scene 4: A Fever

Jesus and his followers left the synagogue. They went to the house of Simon and Andrew. James and John came too. {v: 1:29}

Simon's mother-in-law was in bed with a fever. They told Jesus about her right away. {v: 1:30}

Jesus went to her. He took her by the hand and helped her up. {v: 1:31}

The fever left her. Then she began to serve them, taking care of her guests. {v: 1:31}

### Scene 5: The Whole Town at the Door

That evening, after the sun went down, people brought everyone who was sick to Jesus. They also brought people who had demons. {v: 1:32}

The whole town gathered at the door. {v: 1:33}

Jesus healed many people who had all kinds of diseases. He drove out many demons. {v: 1:34}

He did not let the demons speak, because they knew who he was. {v: 1:34}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a narrow street at dusk, crowded with people. Some lean on friends. Some are carried on mats. Oil lamps flicker in the doorways.

### Scene 6: Praying in the Dark

Very early the next morning, while it was still dark, Jesus got up. He went out to a quiet place where he could be alone. There he prayed. {v: 1:35}

Simon and the others went looking for him. {v: 1:36}

When they found him, they said, **"Everyone is looking for You!"** (1:37). {v: 1:37}

But Jesus said, **"Let us go on to the neighboring towns so I can preach there as well, for that is why I have come"** (1:38). {v: 1:38}

So Jesus went all through Galilee. He preached in their synagogues and drove out demons. {v: 1:39}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Mark 1:21-39 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"As one who had authority"** (1:22) | Jesus did not only repeat other teachers. He spoke for God, because he is God's Son. |
| **"I know who You are—the Holy One of God!"** (1:24) | The evil spirit knew who Jesus was before the people did. |
| **"Be silent!... Come out of him!"** (1:25) | Jesus has power over evil. He only had to speak. |
| **"He commands even the unclean spirits"** (1:27) | The people saw that Jesus's words do what they say. |
| **"Took her by the hand, and helped her up"** (1:31) | Jesus healed with a touch. He came close to people who were sick. |
| **"He would not allow the demons to speak"** (1:34) | Jesus did not want evil spirits telling people who he was. He would show them himself. |
| **"For that is why I have come"** (1:38) | Jesus came to preach the good news, not only to heal in one town. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Capernaum was Jesus's home base in Galilee.** It was a fishing town on the north shore of the Sea of Galilee (Easton's Bible Dictionary). Mark later says Jesus was **"at home"** there (2:1).

**The Sabbath ended at sunset.** A Jewish day began and ended at sunset. Many people kept the Sabbath rules by not carrying loads until it ended. That may be why the town waited until after sunset to bring their sick.†

**A fever could be dangerous.** There were no medicines like today's. A high fever could last a long time or even kill.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Is having a demon the same as having a mental illness?** No. Mark tells about sick people and people with demons as two different groups (1:32, 1:34). A mental illness is an illness, like a fever. It is not a demon, and it is not a punishment. Doctors and counselors can help. If you are struggling, please tell a trusted adult. In the U.S., you can call or text 988 any time, day or night. Outside the U.S., findahelpline.com lists free helplines.†

**Why did Jesus tell the demon to be quiet when it was telling the truth?** An evil spirit is not a trustworthy witness. Jesus wanted people to learn who he was from his words and his works, not from his enemies.†

**Did Simon's mother-in-law have to get up and work right after being sick?** Mark shows how completely she was healed. She did not need days to rest. She was strong enough to welcome her guests. Mark uses the same word for "serve" later, when Jesus says he came **"to serve"** (10:45).†

**Why did Jesus need to pray?** Jesus is God's Son, and he is also fully human. He talked with his Father in prayer. Even when crowds were looking for him, he took time to pray.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Jesus was mostly a healer and miracle worker."*

**What's true:** Jesus healed many people. He really did.

**What the story is mainly about:** Jesus's word has authority. The people noticed his teaching first (1:22). When the crowds wanted more miracles, Jesus said he came to preach (1:38). His healings showed that his word is true.

**Where Jesus is:** In the synagogue, at a sickbed, at a crowded door, and alone in the dark, praying. Then on the road to the next town.

## ✝️ Good News

Jesus did not stay away from people who were sick or troubled. He took a sick woman by the hand. He stood at a door crowded with hurting people.

**"For that is why I have come"** (1:38).

He came to bring the good news that God's kingdom is here. Evil has to obey him.

**Jesus has authority over sickness and evil. He comes close and lifts people up.**

## 📚 From Luther

In the Large Catechism, Luther teaches that God's word is not dead letters. It is living and active, and it works where it is spoken. In Capernaum, Jesus spoke, and the spirit had to obey.†

Luther also prayed early in the morning. He wrote morning and evening prayers in the Small Catechism, to begin and end each day with God.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Mark 1:21-28 on the Fourth Sunday after the Epiphany, and Mark 1:29-39 on the Fifth Sunday after the Epiphany.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Jesus teaches with authority. Sickness and evil obey his word, and he comes close to lift people up.**

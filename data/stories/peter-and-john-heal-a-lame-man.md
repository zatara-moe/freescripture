# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Peter and John:** Two of Jesus's apostles, now leaders of the young church.
- **A man lame from birth:** A man who has never walked. He is over forty years old (4:22).
- **The temple leaders:** Priests, the captain of the temple guard, and Sadducees.
- **The council:** The Sanhedrin, the highest Jewish court, including the high priests Annas and Caiaphas.
- **The believers:** The growing community of Jesus's followers.

**Setting**

- The temple in Jerusalem, at a gate called Beautiful. Then before the council.

**When**

- Soon after the Holy Spirit came at Pentecost, about 2,000 years ago.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**The Holy Spirit came at Pentecost.** Peter preached, and about three thousand people were baptized (2:41).

**The believers met together every day.** They prayed, ate together, and shared what they had (2:44-46).

**Here's what you know:** Some of these same leaders had condemned Jesus only weeks before.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: "Look at Us!"

One afternoon, Peter and John went to the temple to pray. {v: 3:1}

A man who had been lame from birth was carried to the temple gate every day to beg. {v: 3:2}

He asked Peter and John for money. {v: 3:3}

Peter looked straight at him. **"Look at us!"** (3:4). {v: 3:4}

The man looked up, hoping for a coin. {v: 3:5}

**"Silver or gold I do not have, but what I have I give you: In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, get up and walk!"** (3:6). {v: 3:6}

Peter took his hand and helped him up. Right away his feet and ankles became strong. {v: 3:7}

He jumped up and walked. He went into the temple **"walking and leaping and praising God"** (3:8). {v: 3:8}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a man who has only ever sat on the ground, taking his first wobbly step, then another, then jumping. People at the gate who passed him every day stop and stare.

### Scene 2: Not by Our Own Power

Everyone recognized him. They were amazed and ran to see. {v: 3:9, 3:10, 3:11}

Peter said, **"Why do you stare at us as if by our own power or godliness we had made this man walk?"** (3:12). {v: 3:12}

It was Jesus. They had rejected him, **"but God raised Him from the dead"** (3:15). {v: 3:13, 3:14, 3:15}

**"By faith in the name of Jesus, this man whom you see and know has been made strong"** (3:16). {v: 3:16}

**"Repent, then, and turn back, so that your sins may be wiped away"** (3:19). {v: 3:17, 3:18, 3:19}

### Scene 3: Arrested

The temple leaders were upset that Peter and John were teaching about Jesus rising from the dead. They arrested them and kept them overnight. {v: 4:1, 4:2, 4:3}

But many people believed. The number of believers grew to about five thousand men. {v: 4:4}

The next day, the council asked, **"By what power or what name did you do this?"** (4:7). {v: 4:5, 4:6, 4:7}

Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said it was by the name of Jesus, whom they crucified and God raised. {v: 4:8, 4:9, 4:10}

**"Salvation exists in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to men by which we must be saved"** (4:12). {v: 4:11, 4:12}

### Scene 4: "We Cannot Stop Speaking"

The council saw how bold Peter and John were. They were **"unschooled, ordinary men"** (4:13). The leaders could tell **"that these men had been with Jesus"** (4:13). {v: 4:13}

The healed man was standing right there. No one could deny it. {v: 4:14, 4:16}

So they ordered Peter and John never to speak in Jesus's name again. {v: 4:17, 4:18}

Peter and John answered, **"For we cannot stop speaking about what we have seen and heard"** (4:20). {v: 4:19, 4:20}

The council threatened them and let them go. {v: 4:21}

### Scene 5: The Place Shook

Peter and John went back to the believers and told them everything. {v: 4:23}

Together they prayed. They didn't ask God to take away the threats. They asked for boldness. {v: 4:24, 4:29}

**"After they had prayed, their meeting place was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke the word of God boldly"** (4:31). {v: 4:31}

The believers were of one heart and mind. They shared everything they had. {v: 4:32}

**"There were no needy ones among them"** (4:34). People who had land or houses sold them and gave the money to help others. {v: 4:34, 4:35}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Acts 3:1 to 4:35 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Silver or gold I do not have"** (3:6) | Peter gave something better than money. |
| **"In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth"** (3:6) | The healing came from Jesus, not Peter. |
| **"Walking and leaping and praising God"** (3:8) | The man's joy overflowed. |
| **"No other name under heaven"** (4:12) | Jesus is the one who saves. |
| **"These men had been with Jesus"** (4:13) | Ordinary people were changed by Jesus. |
| **"We cannot stop speaking"** (4:20) | They had to tell what they had seen. |
| **"There were no needy ones among them"** (4:34) | The believers took care of each other. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**The ninth hour was about 3 p.m.** It was a regular time of prayer at the temple.†

**People with disabilities often had to beg.** There were few other ways to live.†

**Solomon's Colonnade** was a long covered porch on the east side of the temple courts. Jesus had taught there too (John 10:23).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Is "in the name of Jesus" a magic phrase?** No. It means Jesus himself did the healing. Peter said so: "Not by our own power."

**Should people with disabilities just pray to be healed?** God sometimes heals, and sometimes doesn't in this life. People with disabilities are fully loved by God. In God's new creation, all will be made whole.†

**Do Christians have to sell everything?** The first believers chose to share freely. Christians today share in many ways. The point is that no one in the church should be left in need.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"You need to be educated or important to talk about God."*

**What's true:** Learning about God is good.

**What the story is mainly about:** Peter and John were "unschooled, ordinary men." What made them bold was that they had been with Jesus. God uses ordinary people.

**Where Jesus is:** Risen and working through his followers. The healing happened in his name, and his Spirit filled the believers.

## ✝️ Good News

Peter couldn't heal anyone himself. Jesus could.

**"Salvation exists in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to men by which we must be saved"** (4:12).

The risen Jesus still works through ordinary people, giving gifts better than silver or gold.

**In Jesus's name, Peter told a lame man to walk. Jesus is the name that saves, and he makes ordinary people bold.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that God's power works through weak and ordinary people. The church's treasure isn't silver or gold. It is the gospel of Christ.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Acts 4:32-35 on the Second Sunday of Easter, Acts 3:12-19 on the Third, and Acts 4:5-12 on the Fourth.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Peter had no silver or gold, but in Jesus's name a lame man walked. Arrested and threatened, the apostles said, "We cannot stop speaking about what we have seen and heard."**

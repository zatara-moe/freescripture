# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Joshua:** The leader who took Moses's place. He led Israel into the promised land. Now he is old.
- **The tribes of Israel:** All of God's people, with their leaders, elders, and judges.
- **The LORD:** God. LORD in capital letters stands for God's personal name. Jewish readers traditionally don't say it aloud, out of respect.

**Setting**

- Shechem, a city in the hills of the promised land. Abraham once built an altar there (Genesis 12:6-7).

**When**

- Near the end of Joshua's life, more than 3,000 years ago.†

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Joshua led God's people into the land.** They crossed the Jordan River on dry ground and saw the walls of Jericho fall (3:17, 6:20).

**Each tribe received its land.** God kept his promise to Abraham (21:43-45).

**But the people were surrounded by other gods.** Some still had idols of their own.†

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Remember

Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel at Shechem. {v: 24:1}

He told them what God says. Long ago, Abraham's family worshiped other gods, beyond the Euphrates River. {v: 24:2}

But God called Abraham and gave him Isaac. Isaac had Jacob. Jacob's family went down to Egypt. {v: 24:3, 24:4}

God sent Moses, brought them out of Egypt, and saved them at the sea. {v: 24:5, 24:6, 24:7}

God led them through the desert and gave them victory. {v: 24:8, 24:11}

**"So I gave you a land on which you did not toil and cities that you did not build"** (24:13). Everything they had was a gift. {v: 24:13}

### Scene 2: Choose This Day

**"Now, therefore, fear the LORD and serve Him in sincerity and truth"** (24:14). {v: 24:14}

Joshua told them to throw away the false gods. {v: 24:14}

**"Choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve"** (24:15). {v: 24:15}

The old gods of their ancestors? The gods of the people around them? {v: 24:15}

**"As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD!"** (24:15). {v: 24:15}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture an old man standing in front of a huge crowd. He has led them for years. His voice is strong as he says the words his family will live by.

### Scene 3: "We Will Serve the LORD"

The people answered, **"Far be it from us to forsake the LORD to serve other gods!"** (24:16). {v: 24:16}

God had rescued them and protected them. **"We too will serve the LORD, because He is our God!"** (24:18). {v: 24:17, 24:18}

Joshua warned them: God is holy. Serving him is serious. {v: 24:19, 24:20}

**"No!"** replied the people. **"We will serve the LORD!"** (24:21). {v: 24:21}

**"Get rid of the foreign gods among you and incline your hearts to the LORD"** (24:23). {v: 24:22, 24:23, 24:24}

### Scene 4: A Stone of Witness

That day, Joshua made a covenant with the people. {v: 24:25}

He wrote it down and set up a large stone under an oak tree. {v: 24:26}

The stone would be a reminder of the promise they made. {v: 24:27}

Then Joshua sent everyone home. {v: 24:28}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Joshua 24:1-28 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Cities that you did not build"** (24:13) | Everything they had was God's gift. |
| **"Serve Him in sincerity and truth"** (24:14) | Serve God honestly, with your whole heart. |
| **"Choose for yourselves this day"** (24:15) | Joshua asked them to decide. |
| **"As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD!"** (24:15) | Joshua's family had decided. |
| **"Because He is our God"** (24:18) | They chose God because of what he had done. |
| **"Incline your hearts to the LORD"** (24:23) | Turn your heart toward God. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Shechem was an important place.** God first promised the land to Abraham there (Genesis 12:6-7).†

**Covenants were often marked with stones.** A stone stayed as a witness long after the people were gone.†

**Many families hang "As for me and my house" on their walls**, as a sign of their faith.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Did the people keep their promise?** Not always. The book of Judges tells how they turned to other gods again and again. God kept calling them back.

**Joshua said, "You are not able to serve the LORD." What?** Joshua warned them that serving God isn't easy. No one does it perfectly. That's why we need God's forgiveness.†

**Can I choose God?** Lutherans teach that faith is God's gift. He works in us to believe. Like the Israelites, we respond to what God has already done.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |

| Joshua | Determined | He had made his choice (24:15) |
| The people | Grateful | They remembered all God did (24:17) |
| The people | Committed | They promised to serve the LORD (24:24) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Faith is something you inherit. You don't really choose."*

**What's true:** Faith is often passed down in families.

**What the story is mainly about:** Joshua told the story of everything God had done. Then he said, "Choose this day." Faith responds to God's gifts. Every person, and every family, faces that choice.

**Where Jesus is:** Jesus's name in Hebrew is the same as Joshua's: "The LORD saves." Jesus leads his people into a better promised land.†

## ✝️ Good News

Everything the people had was a gift from God.

**"As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD!"** (24:15).

God chose us first. In Jesus, he gives us everything. We serve him not to earn his love, but because he is our God.

**Joshua reminded the people of everything God had given them, then said, "Choose this day whom you will serve. As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD!"**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther wanted every home to be a little church, where parents teach their children God's word and pray together. He wrote the Small Catechism for families.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Joshua 24:1-2a and 14-18 in late August (Lectionary 21), next to Peter saying, "Lord, to whom would we go?" In Year A, it is read in November (Lectionary 32).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Joshua reminded Israel of all God had done and told them to choose whom they would serve. "As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD!"**

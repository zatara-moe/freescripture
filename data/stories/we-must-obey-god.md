# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Peter and the apostles:** Jesus's chosen messengers, leading the church in Jerusalem.
- **The high priest:** The leader of the temple and the council.
- **An angel of the Lord:** A messenger from God.
- **Gamaliel:** A respected Pharisee and teacher of God's law. Paul later studied under him (22:3).

**Setting**

- The temple courts in Jerusalem, a city jail, and the council chamber.

**When**

- A few months after Jesus rose from the dead.†

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Peter and John healed a lame man.** The council ordered them never to speak about Jesus again (4:18).

**They said they couldn't stop.** **"We cannot stop speaking about what we have seen and heard"** (4:20).

**The church kept growing.** The believers shared everything, and no one among them was in need (4:34).

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Signs and Wonders

The apostles did many signs and wonders. The believers met together in the temple courts. {v: 5:12}

More and more people believed in the Lord, men and women. {v: 5:14}

People carried sick friends into the streets on mats, hoping even Peter's shadow would fall on them as he passed. {v: 5:15}

Crowds came from nearby towns, and all of them were healed. {v: 5:16}

### Scene 2: An Open Jail

The high priest and his group were jealous. They arrested the apostles and put them in the public jail. {v: 5:17, 5:18}

**"But during the night an angel of the Lord opened the doors of the jail and brought them out"** (5:19). {v: 5:19}

The angel said, **"Go, stand in the temple courts and tell the people the full message of this new life"** (5:20). {v: 5:20}

So at sunrise, they went right back to the temple and started teaching. {v: 5:21}

The council sent for them. The officers came back confused. The jail was locked, the guards were at the doors, but no one was inside! {v: 5:21, 5:22, 5:23}

Then someone said, "The men you put in jail are standing in the temple, teaching!" {v: 5:25}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture guards standing stiffly at a locked door, then opening it to find an empty cell. They look at each other. Nobody wants to tell the high priest.

### Scene 3: "We Must Obey God"

The officers brought the apostles to the council, carefully, because they were afraid of the crowd. {v: 5:26, 5:27}

The high priest said, **"We gave you strict orders not to teach in this name"** (5:28). {v: 5:28}

Peter and the apostles answered, **"We must obey God rather than men"** (5:29). {v: 5:29}

God raised Jesus, whom they had killed. God lifted him up **"as Prince and Savior, in order to grant repentance and forgiveness of sins to Israel"** (5:31). {v: 5:30, 5:31}

**"We are witnesses of these things, and so is the Holy Spirit"** (5:32). {v: 5:32}

### Scene 4: Gamaliel's Advice

The council was furious. They wanted to kill the apostles. {v: 5:33}

But Gamaliel, a respected teacher, stood up. {v: 5:34}

He reminded them of other movements that had fizzled out when their leaders died. {v: 5:35, 5:36, 5:37}

**"For if their purpose or endeavor is of human origin, it will fail. But if it is from God, you will not be able to stop them. You may even find yourselves fighting against God"** (5:38-39). {v: 5:38, 5:39}

The council listened. But they had the apostles beaten, ordered them again not to speak about Jesus, and let them go. {v: 5:40}

The apostles left **"rejoicing that they had been counted worthy of suffering disgrace for the Name"** (5:41). {v: 5:41}

Every day, at the temple and in homes, they kept teaching that Jesus is the Christ. {v: 5:42}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Acts 5:12-42 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Tell the people the full message of this new life"** (5:20) | The angel sent them right back to preach. |
| **"We must obey God rather than men"** (5:29) | God's command comes first. |
| **"Prince and Savior"** (5:31) | Jesus is King and the one who saves. |
| **"Forgiveness of sins"** (5:31) | This was the heart of their message. |
| **"You may even find yourselves fighting against God"** (5:39) | No one can stop God's work. |
| **"Rejoicing that they had been counted worthy"** (5:41) | They were glad to suffer for Jesus. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**The Sanhedrin** was the highest Jewish court, with about seventy members.†

**Theudas and Judas the Galilean** led uprisings that failed. The historian Josephus also wrote about them.†

**Flogging** meant beating with a whip. Jewish law limited it to forty lashes (Deuteronomy 25:3).†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does "obey God rather than men" mean I don't have to obey parents or teachers?** No. God tells us to honor parents and respect leaders. This is about when someone orders you to deny God or do something wrong.

**Were the apostles happy to be beaten?** Not about the pain. They were glad to belong to Jesus, even when it cost them.

**Do angels still open jails?** God can do anything. More often, he works in ordinary ways. Either way, his word can't be locked up.

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**What does this story say to you?** Tap one.

- Courage :: The apostles kept going. God can give you courage too.
- God's work can't be stopped :: Gamaliel was right. You can trust that.
- Being teased for faith :: The apostles rejoiced. Jesus is with you.
- Forgiveness :: That was the message: forgiveness through Jesus.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Following Jesus means keeping quiet so no one gets upset."*

**What's true:** Christians should be kind and respectful.

**What the story is mainly about:** The apostles were kind, but they would not stop telling people about Jesus. **"We must obey God rather than men"** (5:29). And God's work can't be stopped.

**Where Jesus is:** Raised to God's right hand as Prince and Savior, giving forgiveness, and keeping his messengers going.

## ✝️ Good News

The leaders locked the apostles up. God opened the door.

**"God exalted Him to His right hand as Prince and Savior, in order to grant repentance and forgiveness of sins"** (5:31).

Jesus rules, and no one can stop his good news of forgiveness.

**An angel opened the jail, and the apostles said, "We must obey God rather than men." Jesus is Prince and Savior, and his good news can't be stopped.**

## 📚 From Luther

At the Diet of Worms in 1521, Luther refused to take back his writings unless he was shown wrong from Scripture. His conscience was bound to God's word. Like the apostles, he obeyed God rather than men.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads Acts 5:27-32 on the Second Sunday of Easter.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**An angel opened the jail, and the apostles went right back to preaching. "We must obey God rather than men." No one can stop God's work.**

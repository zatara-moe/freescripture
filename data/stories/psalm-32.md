# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** David (32:1).

**What it is.** A psalm about confession. David tried to hide his sin, and it made him sick inside. When he finally told God, he found relief and forgiveness.

**Why it matters.** It's one of seven "penitential psalms," psalms of sorrow for sin. Paul quotes it to show that God forgives as a gift (Romans 4:7-8).

**How to read it.** Notice the before and after: silence and groaning, then confession and joy.

## 🗺️ Map of the Psalm

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Blessed Is the Forgiven | 32:1-2 | Happy are those whose sins are covered |
| 2. When I Kept Silent | 32:3-5 | Hiding sin hurt, and confessing it healed |
| 3. My Hiding Place | 32:6-7 | God protects the forgiven |
| 4. I Will Teach You | 32:8-11 | Trust God and rejoice |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Blessed Is the Forgiven

**"Blessed is he whose transgressions are forgiven, whose sins are covered"** (32:1). {v: 32:1}

**"Blessed is the man whose iniquity the LORD does not count against him"** (32:2). {v: 32:2}

Transgressions, sins, and iniquity: three words for doing wrong. God forgives them all. {v: 32:1, 32:2}

### Section 2: When I Kept Silent

**"When I kept silent, my bones became brittle from my groaning all day long"** (32:3). {v: 32:3}

David felt God's hand heavy on him, day and night. He was drained, like a plant in summer heat. {v: 32:4}

Then he stopped hiding. {v: 32:5}

**"I said, "I will confess my transgressions to the LORD," and You forgave the guilt of my sin"** (32:5). {v: 32:5}

### Section 3: My Hiding Place

David urges everyone to pray while God may be found. {v: 32:6}

**"You are my hiding place. You protect me from trouble; You surround me with songs of deliverance"** (32:7). {v: 32:7}

Before, David hid from God. Now he hides in God. {v: 32:7}

### Section 4: I Will Teach You

God speaks: **"I will instruct you and teach you the way you should go; I will give you counsel and watch over you"** (32:8). {v: 32:8}

Don't be stubborn like a mule that has to be dragged. {v: 32:9}

**"Loving devotion surrounds him who trusts in the LORD"** (32:10). {v: 32:10}

**"Be glad in the LORD and rejoice, O righteous ones"** (32:11). {v: 32:11}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Psalm 32 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Whose sins are covered"** (32:1) | God covers our sins so they no longer count against us. |
| **"When I kept silent"** (32:3) | Hiding sin makes us miserable. |
| **"I will confess my transgressions"** (32:5) | Telling God the truth about our sin. |
| **"You forgave the guilt of my sin"** (32:5) | Forgiveness comes right away. |
| **"You are my hiding place"** (32:7) | God is a safe place. |
| **"Like the horse or mule"** (32:9) | Don't be stubborn with God. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**"Selah"** appears three times in this psalm. It may have been a musical pause.†

**Many people connect this psalm** with David's sin with Bathsheba, when he hid his wrong for months (2 Samuel 11-12).†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does God forgive only if I feel bad enough?** No. Forgiveness is God's gift. David just stopped hiding and told the truth.

**Do I need to confess to a pastor?** You can confess directly to God. Lutherans also value private confession, where a pastor speaks God's forgiveness to you personally.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"If I don't think about my sins, they won't bother me."*

**What's true:** Dwelling on guilt can crush us.

**What the passage is mainly about:** David tried ignoring his sin, and it made him sick. **"Then I acknowledged my sin to You... and You forgave the guilt of my sin"** (32:5). Freedom came through confession.

**Where Jesus is:** His death covers our sins, so God no longer counts them against us.

## ✝️ Good News

Forgiveness is waiting.

**"Blessed is he whose transgressions are forgiven, whose sins are covered"** (32:1).

The moment David told the truth, God forgave. The same is true for you.

**Hiding sin makes us miserable. When we confess, God forgives the guilt of our sin, and he becomes our hiding place.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther counted Psalm 32 among the "Pauline psalms," because it teaches that forgiveness is a gift of grace. He said it was one of his favorite psalms.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It sings Psalm 32 on the First Sunday in Lent in Year A and the Fourth Sunday in Lent in Year C, and on Sundays in summer and fall.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Blessed are those whose sins are forgiven. When we stop hiding and confess, God forgives, and he becomes our hiding place.**

# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** Paul, writing from prison to Christians in Ephesus and the area around it (4:1, 6:20).

**What came before.** In chapters 1 to 3, Paul praised God for everything he has done: chosen us, saved us by grace, and made Jews and Gentiles one family in Christ.

**What these chapters are.** The second half of the letter. Paul shows how that family lives together: united, kind, truthful, filled with the Spirit, and standing strong in God's armor.

**How to read it.** Watch for the word "walk." Paul uses it for how we live every day.

## 🗺️ Map of the Letter

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. One Body, One Spirit | 4:1-16 | Keep the unity, and grow up together in Christ |
| 2. Put On the New Self | 4:17 to 5:2 | Be kind, and forgive as God forgave you |
| 3. Children of Light | 5:3-20 | Wake up, be wise, be filled with the Spirit |
| 4. At Home and at Work | 5:21 to 6:9 | Serve one another out of reverence for Christ |
| 5. The Armor of God | 6:10-24 | Be strong in the Lord |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: One Body, One Spirit

**"As a prisoner in the Lord, then, I urge you to walk in a manner worthy of the calling you have received"** (4:1). {v: 4:1}

Be humble, gentle, and patient. Bear with each other in love. Keep the unity the Spirit gives. {v: 4:2, 4:3}

**"There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were called to one hope when you were called; one Lord, one faith, one baptism; one God and Father of all"** (4:4-6). {v: 4:4, 4:5, 4:6}

Christ gives each person grace and gifts. {v: 4:7, 4:8, 4:9, 4:10}

He gave apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, and teachers **"to equip the saints for works of ministry and to build up the body of Christ"** (4:12). {v: 4:11, 4:12, 4:13}

Then we won't be like babies, tossed around by every new idea. {v: 4:14}

**"Instead, speaking the truth in love, we will in all things grow up into Christ Himself, who is the head"** (4:15). {v: 4:15, 4:16}

### Section 2: Put On the New Self

Don't live the old way, with darkened minds and hard hearts. {v: 4:17, 4:18, 4:19, 4:20, 4:21}

Take off the old self like dirty clothes. Put on the new self, made to be like God. {v: 4:22, 4:23, 4:24}

So tell the truth, because we belong to each other. {v: 4:25}

Paul quotes a psalm: **"Be angry, yet do not sin"** (4:26). {v: 4:26}

**"Do not let the sun set upon your anger, and do not give the devil a foothold"** (4:26-27). {v: 4:26, 4:27}

Instead of stealing, work, so you have something to share. {v: 4:28}

**"Let no unwholesome talk come out of your mouths, but only what is helpful for building up the one in need"** (4:29). {v: 4:29, 4:30, 4:31}

**"Be kind and tenderhearted to one another, forgiving each other just as in Christ God forgave you"** (4:32). {v: 4:32}

**"Be imitators of God, therefore, as beloved children, and walk in love, just as Christ loved us and gave Himself up for us"** (5:1-2). {v: 5:1, 5:2}

### Section 3: Children of Light

Paul warns against sexual sin, greed, and crude talk. Give thanks instead. {v: 5:3, 5:4, 5:5, 5:6, 5:7}

**"For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light"** (5:8). {v: 5:8, 5:9, 5:10}

Light shows things as they really are. {v: 5:11, 5:12, 5:13}

**"Wake up, O sleeper, rise up from the dead, and Christ will shine on you"** (5:14). {v: 5:14}

This may be a line from an early baptism hymn.† {v: 5:14}

**"Pay careful attention, then, to how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, redeeming the time, because the days are evil"** (5:15-16). {v: 5:15, 5:16, 5:17}

Don't get drunk on wine. **"Instead, be filled with the Spirit"** (5:18). {v: 5:18}

Sing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, and always give thanks. {v: 5:19, 5:20}

### Section 4: At Home and at Work

**"Submit to one another out of reverence for Christ"** (5:21). {v: 5:21}

Paul applies this to the households of his day. {v: 5:21}

He tells wives to submit to their husbands, and husbands to love their wives **"just as Christ loved the church and gave Himself up for her"** (5:25). {v: 5:22, 5:23, 5:24, 5:25}

Christ makes his church clean and holy. {v: 5:26, 5:27}

Husbands should love their wives as their own bodies. {v: 5:28, 5:29, 5:30}

Marriage is a picture of Christ and the church. {v: 5:31, 5:32, 5:33}

Children should obey their parents. Fathers should not make their children angry, but raise them in the Lord. {v: 6:1, 6:2, 6:3, 6:4}

Slaves should serve as if serving Christ, and masters must stop threatening, because both have the same Master in heaven. {v: 6:5, 6:6, 6:7, 6:8, 6:9}

### Section 5: The Armor of God

**"Finally, be strong in the Lord and in His mighty power"** (6:10). {v: 6:10}

**"Put on the full armor of God, so that you can make your stand against the devil's schemes"** (6:11). {v: 6:11}

**"For our struggle is not against flesh and blood"** (6:12). Our real enemy is the devil and the powers of evil. {v: 6:12, 6:13}

Paul pictures a Roman soldier getting dressed for battle. {v: 6:14}

Put on the belt of truth and the breastplate of righteousness. For shoes, wear the readiness that comes from the good news of peace. {v: 6:14, 6:15}

Take up the shield of faith, which puts out all the devil's flaming arrows. {v: 6:16}

**"And take the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God"** (6:17). {v: 6:17}

And pray at all times. {v: 6:18}

Paul asks them to pray for him, an **"ambassador in chains"** (6:20), so he can tell the good news boldly. {v: 6:19, 6:20}

He ends with peace, love, and grace. {v: 6:21, 6:22, 6:23, 6:24}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Ephesians 4 to 6 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"One Lord, one faith, one baptism"** (4:5) | All Christians share the same Lord. |
| **"Speaking the truth in love"** (4:15) | Be honest and kind at the same time. |
| **"Do not let the sun set upon your anger"** (4:26) | Don't let anger sit overnight. |
| **"Forgiving each other just as in Christ God forgave you"** (4:32) | We forgive because we've been forgiven. |
| **"Walk as children of light"** (5:8) | Live openly and honestly. |
| **"Be filled with the Spirit"** (5:18) | Let God's Spirit, not anything else, shape you. |
| **"Submit to one another"** (5:21) | Put others first, out of love for Christ. |
| **"The full armor of God"** (6:11) | God gives everything we need to stand against evil. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Ephesus** was famous for the temple of the goddess Artemis and for magic practices (Acts 19:19, 19:27).

**Paul was chained to Roman soldiers** while in prison (Acts 28:16, 28:20). He saw their armor every day.†

**Households** in Paul's time included husbands, wives, children, and often slaves. Paul's instructions were addressed to each person in that setting.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does Paul approve of slavery?** Paul spoke to people inside a system he didn't create. He told masters that they and their slaves have the same Master and that God plays no favorites (6:9). Christians later drew on teaching like this to oppose slavery.

**Does "submit" mean a wife must accept abuse?** No. Paul begins with "submit to one another" (5:21) and tells husbands to love like Christ, who gave himself up. Christians have understood this passage in different ways, but no reading allows abuse.

**Is the armor of God real armor?** It's a picture. The "armor" is truth, righteousness, the good news, faith, salvation, God's word, and prayer.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Being a Christian means following a list of rules."*

**What's true:** God shows us how to live, and Paul gives lots of practical advice here.

**What the passage is mainly about:** It all flows from what God has done. **"Forgiving each other just as in Christ God forgave you"** (4:32). **"Walk in love, just as Christ loved us"** (5:2). God's love comes first.

**Where Jesus is:** The head of the body, the one who loved the church and gave himself up for her.

## ✝️ Good News

You don't fight alone, and you don't fight in your own strength.

**"Finally, be strong in the Lord and in His mighty power"** (6:10).

God forgives you in Christ, fills you with his Spirit, and gives you his own armor to stand.

**There is one body, one Spirit, and one Lord. Be kind and forgive as God forgave you in Christ, and be strong in the Lord, wearing his full armor.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther's hymn "A Mighty Fortress Is Our God" echoes Ephesians 6. The devil is strong, but "one little word" from God can fell him, and God's Word stands firm forever.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads through Ephesians 4 to 6 on five Sundays in August (Lectionary 18 to 22). In Year A, Ephesians 5:8-14 is read on the Fourth Sunday in Lent.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**There is one body, one Spirit, one Lord, one faith, and one baptism. As God's children of light, we forgive as God forgave us in Christ, and we stand strong in the Lord, wearing the full armor of God.**

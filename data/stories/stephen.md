# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Stephen:** One of seven men chosen to serve the church. He is full of faith and the Holy Spirit.
- **The apostles:** The church's leaders.
- **The council:** The Sanhedrin, the highest Jewish court.
- **Saul:** A young man who watches. Later he will become the apostle Paul.

**Setting**

- Jerusalem: the believers' meeting place, the council chamber, and outside the city walls.

**When**

- A few years after Jesus rose from the dead.†

## 🧭 Before the Story

**The church was growing fast.** Thousands believed in Jesus (4:4).

**The apostles kept preaching**, even after being arrested and beaten (5:40-42).

**The believers took care of widows.** Every day they shared food with widows who had no one to provide for them.†

## 🗺️ Map of the Story

| Scene | Verses | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Seven helpers | 6:1-7 | The church chooses seven men to serve |
| 2. Full of grace | 6:8-15 | Stephen is falsely accused |
| 3. Heaven open | 7:51-56 | Stephen sees Jesus |
| 4. "Do not hold this sin against them" | 7:57-60 | Stephen dies forgiving |

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Seven Helpers

As the church grew, some widows were being overlooked when food was shared. {v: 6:1}

The apostles said they needed to keep praying and preaching. So the church should choose seven men full of the Spirit and wisdom to take care of the food. {v: 6:2, 6:3, 6:4}

Everyone agreed. They chose Stephen, **"a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit"** (6:5), and six others. {v: 6:5}

The apostles prayed and laid their hands on them. The church kept growing. {v: 6:6, 6:7}

### Scene 2: Full of Grace

Stephen was **"full of grace and power"** (6:8). He did wonders and signs among the people. {v: 6:8}

Some men argued with him, but they couldn't win against his wisdom and the Spirit. {v: 6:9, 6:10}

So they got people to lie about him. They said he spoke against God and Moses. {v: 6:11}

They dragged him before the council with false witnesses. {v: 6:12, 6:13}

**"All who were sitting in the Sanhedrin looked intently at Stephen, and they saw that his face was like the face of an angel"** (6:15). {v: 6:15}

### Scene 3: Heaven Open

Stephen gave a long speech about God's faithfulness and his people's stubbornness. Then he told the council they were resisting the Holy Spirit, as their ancestors had. {v: 7:51, 7:52, 7:53}

The council was furious. {v: 7:54}

**"But Stephen, full of the Holy Spirit, looked intently into heaven and saw the glory of God and Jesus standing at the right hand of God"** (7:55). {v: 7:55}

**"Look,"** he said, **"I see heaven open and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God"** (7:56). {v: 7:56}

### Scene 4: "Do Not Hold This Sin Against Them"

They covered their ears and rushed at him. They dragged him out of the city and threw stones at him. {v: 7:57, 7:58}

The witnesses laid their coats at the feet of a young man named Saul. {v: 7:58}

While they were stoning him, Stephen prayed, **"Lord Jesus, receive my spirit"** (7:59). {v: 7:59}

He fell to his knees and cried out, **"Lord, do not hold this sin against them"** (7:60). {v: 7:60}

**"And when he had said this, he fell asleep"** (7:60). {v: 7:60}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a young man standing to one side, a pile of coats at his feet, watching. He will never forget Stephen's face, or Stephen's prayer.

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Acts 6 and 7:51-60 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Full of faith and of the Holy Spirit"** (6:5) | Stephen was chosen to serve because of his faith. |
| **"His face was like the face of an angel"** (6:15) | God's peace shone on Stephen, even when falsely accused. |
| **"Jesus standing at the right hand of God"** (7:55) | Jesus stood to welcome his faithful witness. |
| **"Lord Jesus, receive my spirit"** (7:59) | Stephen trusted Jesus with his life. |
| **"Lord, do not hold this sin against them"** (7:60) | Stephen forgave the people killing him, like Jesus. |
| **"He fell asleep"** (7:60) | Death is like sleep for those who belong to Jesus. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Stephen was the first Christian martyr.** A martyr is someone who dies because they won't give up their faith. The word means "witness."†

**The seven are often seen as the first deacons.** A deacon is someone who serves, especially caring for people in need.†

**Saul became Paul.** Soon after, Jesus met Saul on the road to Damascus (9:3-6).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Why did God let Stephen die?** The Bible doesn't fully explain. Stephen's faithfulness helped spread the gospel, and it may have stayed with Saul. And for Stephen, death meant being with Jesus.†

**Do I have to forgive people who hurt me badly?** Forgiveness is a gift Jesus helps us give over time. Forgiving doesn't mean staying near someone who hurts you. If someone is hurting you, tell a trusted adult.

**Is Jesus sitting or standing?** Usually the Bible says Jesus sits at God's right hand. Here he stands, perhaps to welcome Stephen home.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The widows | Overlooked | They weren't getting food (6:1) |
| The council | Enraged | They gnashed their teeth (7:54) |
| Stephen | Peaceful | He saw Jesus and prayed for his killers (7:55, 7:60) |

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**What does Stephen's story say to you?** Tap one.

- Serving :: Stephen started by serving food to widows. Serving matters.
- Being falsely accused :: God saw the truth about Stephen.
- Forgiving :: Stephen forgave like Jesus did. Jesus can help you too.
- Afraid of dying :: For Stephen, death was falling asleep and waking with Jesus.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Faithful Christians never suffer."*

**What's true:** God is with his people always.

**What the story is mainly about:** Stephen was faithful, and he died for it. But he saw Jesus waiting for him, and he died forgiving, just like his Lord.

**Where Jesus is:** Standing at the right hand of God, welcoming Stephen home.

## ✝️ Good News

Stephen saw heaven open, and Jesus was there.

**"Look,"** he said, **"I see heaven open and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God"** (7:56).

Death couldn't separate Stephen from Jesus. It can't separate you either. Jesus receives everyone who belongs to him.

**Stephen saw Jesus at God's right hand. He forgave the people who killed him and fell asleep in the Lord.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther pointed to Stephen's last prayers as a model for Christians facing death: "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit." He said a Christian can fall asleep trusting Christ, like a child going to bed.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year A, it reads Acts 7:55-60 on the Fifth Sunday of Easter. Many churches also remember Stephen on December 26.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Stephen served the church, was falsely accused, and saw Jesus standing at God's right hand. He died forgiving those who killed him.**

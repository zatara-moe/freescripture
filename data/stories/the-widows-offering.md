# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God. It is a few days before he dies.
- **The scribes:** Teachers of God's law. Some of them like to be noticed.
- **Rich people:** People giving large gifts at the temple.
- **A poor widow:** A woman whose husband has died. She has almost nothing.
- **The disciples:** Jesus's closest followers.

**Setting**

- The temple courts in Jerusalem, near the boxes where people gave offerings.

**When**

- Holy Week, about 2,000 years ago, a few days before Jesus was crucified.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus was teaching in the temple.** A scribe had asked him which commandment is the most important. Jesus said: love God, and love your neighbor (12:28-31).

**God's law told his people to care for widows.** A widow often had no one to provide for her (Deuteronomy 24:19).†

**Here's what you know:** In a few days, Jesus will give everything he has, his own life.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Watch Out

Jesus was teaching in the temple. **"Watch out for the scribes,"** he said (12:38). {v: 12:38}

Some of them liked to walk around in long robes and be greeted with respect in the marketplace. {v: 12:38}

They liked the best seats in the synagogue and the places of honor at dinners. {v: 12:39}

**"They defraud widows of their houses, and for a show make lengthy prayers"** (12:40). To defraud means to cheat. {v: 12:40}

They looked holy, but they took advantage of the weakest people. {v: 12:40}

### Scene 2: The Offering Boxes

Then Jesus sat down across from the place where people put in their offerings. He watched. {v: 12:41}

Many rich people came and put in large amounts. {v: 12:41}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture heavy bags of coins clinking and clattering into the metal offering boxes. People turn their heads to see who is giving so much.

### Scene 3: Two Small Coins

Then a poor widow came. {v: 12:42}

She put in two small copper coins. Together they were worth almost nothing. {v: 12:42}

Hardly anyone would have noticed her. But Jesus did. {v: 12:42}

### Scene 4: More Than All the Others

Jesus called his disciples over. {v: 12:43}

**"Truly I tell you, this poor widow has put more than all the others into the treasury"** (12:43). {v: 12:43}

**"For they all contributed out of their surplus, but she out of her poverty has put in all she had to live on"** (12:44). {v: 12:44}

Surplus means extra. The rich gave what they had left over. She gave everything. {v: 12:44}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Mark 12:38-44 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Watch out for the scribes"** (12:38) | Don't be fooled by people who just look holy. |
| **"For a show make lengthy prayers"** (12:40) | Prayer can be turned into showing off. God isn't fooled. |
| **"He watched the crowd"** (12:41) | Jesus notices what people do, and why. |
| **"Two small copper coins"** (12:42) | Her gift was tiny to everyone else. |
| **"More than all the others"** (12:43) | Jesus measures a gift by the heart and the cost, not the amount. |
| **"All she had to live on"** (12:44) | She trusted God with everything. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**The temple had thirteen offering boxes shaped like trumpets.** People dropped coins in, and you could hear them fall.†

**The widow's coins were called *lepta*.** A lepton was the smallest coin. It was worth a tiny part of a day's pay.†

**Luke tells this story too.** Luke 21:1-4.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Should people give away all their money?** Jesus didn't tell everyone to do what the widow did. He praised her trust. God cares about the heart behind a gift, not the size of it.

**Did Jesus approve of her being poor?** No. Right before this, Jesus spoke against people who cheat widows. He cares about people who are poor.†

**Are all scribes bad?** No. Just before this, Jesus told a scribe he was not far from the kingdom of God (12:34). Jesus is warning about showing off and taking advantage of people.

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Some scribes | Proud | They liked the best seats and attention (12:38-39) |
| The rich | Comfortable | They gave out of their extra (12:44) |
| The widow | Trusting | She gave all she had (12:44) |
| The disciples | Surprised | Jesus said she gave the most (12:43) |

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**Do you ever feel like what you have to give is too small?** Tap one.

- Yes :: Jesus saw two tiny coins. He sees your small gifts too.
- I want to be noticed :: Jesus warned about showing off. He sees what's done quietly.
- I have a lot :: You can give generously. Jesus cares about the heart behind it.
- Thankful :: God gave everything for you. You can give with a glad heart.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Only big gifts make a difference."*

**What's true:** Big gifts can do a lot of good.

**What the story is mainly about:** Jesus noticed a gift no one else noticed. He said the widow's two coins were more than everyone else's. God sees the heart, and he sees people the world overlooks.

**Where Jesus is:** Sitting across from the offering boxes, watching, a few days before he gave everything.

## ✝️ Good News

The widow gave everything she had. A few days later, Jesus gave everything he had: his life, for us.

**"She out of her poverty has put in all she had to live on"** (12:44).

Jesus sees people no one else notices. He sees you. And he held nothing back to save you.

**Jesus notices the people the world overlooks. He gave everything for us, and he sees what we give in love.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that God doesn't need our good works, but our neighbor does. Gifts given in faith, however small, are precious to God.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Mark 12:38-44 in November (Lectionary 32).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**A poor widow gave two small coins, all she had. Jesus said it was more than everyone else's. He sees the heart.**

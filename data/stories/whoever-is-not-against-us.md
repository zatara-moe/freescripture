# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God.
- **John:** One of the twelve disciples, a brother of James.
- **The disciples:** Jesus's closest followers.
- **A stranger:** Someone driving out demons in Jesus's name. He isn't one of the twelve.

**Setting**

- A house in Capernaum, a town in Galilee.

**When**

- About 2,000 years ago, in the weeks before Jesus went to Jerusalem.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**The disciples had just argued about who was the greatest.** Jesus told them the first must be the servant of all (9:34-35).

**Jesus took a little child in his arms.** He said whoever welcomes a child like this welcomes him (9:36-37).

**Here's what you know:** The disciples are still thinking about who is in and who is out. Jesus is about to surprise them again.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: "He's Not One of Us"

John said to Jesus, **"Teacher, we saw someone else driving out demons in Your name, and we tried to stop him, because he does not accompany us"** (9:38). {v: 9:38}

The man was doing good, in Jesus's name. But he wasn't part of their group. {v: 9:38}

**"Do not stop him,"** Jesus replied (9:39). {v: 9:39}

No one who does a miracle in Jesus's name will quickly turn around and speak against him. {v: 9:39}

**"For whoever is not against us is for us"** (9:40). {v: 9:40}

Jesus said even someone who gives you a cup of water because you belong to him will not lose their reward. {v: 9:41}

### Scene 2: The Little Ones

Then Jesus gave a serious warning. {v: 9:42}

Some people cause one of Jesus's little ones to stumble. To stumble means to fall away from trusting him. {v: 9:42}

Jesus said it would be better for that person to be thrown into the sea with a heavy stone around his neck. {v: 9:42}

These are strong words. Jesus takes it very seriously when anyone hurts or misleads one of his little ones. {v: 9:42}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture the little child Jesus had held a few minutes before, still standing nearby. Jesus's voice is quiet, but very serious.

### Scene 3: Strong Words

Then Jesus spoke about sin in our own lives. {v: 9:43}

If your hand, your foot, or your eye causes you to sin, get rid of it. It would be better to enter life without it than to be thrown into hell. {v: 9:43, 9:45, 9:47}

This is a comparison. Jesus is not telling anyone to hurt their body. He is using a shocking picture to say: sin is deadly serious. Turn away from whatever pulls you into it. {v: 9:43, 9:47}

### Scene 4: Salt and Peace

**"Salt is good, but if the salt loses its saltiness, with what will you season it?"** (9:50). {v: 9:50}

Salt gives food flavor and keeps it from going bad. {v: 9:50}

**"Have salt among yourselves, and be at peace with one another"** (9:50). {v: 9:50}

The disciples had been arguing. Jesus told them to be at peace. {v: 9:50}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Mark 9:38-50 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"He does not accompany us"** (9:38) | The disciples wanted to keep good work inside their own group. |
| **"Do not stop him"** (9:39) | God works through people outside our group too. |
| **"Whoever is not against us is for us"** (9:40) | Don't treat people as enemies just because they're different. |
| **"Even a cup of water"** (9:41) | Small kindnesses matter to Jesus. |
| **"Causes one of these little ones... to stumble"** (9:42) | Jesus takes it seriously when anyone hurts or misleads a believer. |
| **"If your hand causes you to sin, cut it off"** (9:43) | A comparison: get rid of what pulls you into sin. |
| **"Be at peace with one another"** (9:50) | Jesus's followers stop competing and make peace. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**A millstone was a huge round stone for grinding grain.** The biggest ones were turned by a donkey.†

**Salt was precious.** It was used to flavor and preserve food, and it was added to offerings at the temple (Leviticus 2:13).†

**Moses once had a similar moment.** Two men prophesied outside the camp, and Joshua wanted to stop them. Moses said he wished all God's people were prophets (Numbers 11:26-29).†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Should someone really cut off a hand or foot?** No. Jesus never wants you to hurt your body. He is using a strong picture to show how serious sin is. If you ever feel like hurting yourself, please tell a trusted adult right away. You are precious to God.

**What does it mean to cause a little one to stumble?** It means hurting a child or a young believer, or leading them away from Jesus. If anyone is hurting you, it is never your fault. Please tell a trusted adult.

**Are all Christians on the same team?** Jesus said not to stop someone doing good in his name. Christians belong to different churches, but Jesus calls us to be at peace with one another.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |

| John | Protective | He tried to stop someone outside the group (9:38) |
| The disciples | Surprised | Jesus said not to stop him (9:39) |
| Jesus | Serious | He warned about hurting little ones (9:42) |

# Why It Matters

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Only people in my group are really on God's side."*

**What's true:** Truth matters. Jesus's followers care about getting the message right.

**What the story is mainly about:** The disciples wanted to keep Jesus's work inside their own circle. Jesus said, **"Whoever is not against us is for us"** (9:40). He cares about every small kindness and every little one.

**Where Jesus is:** In a house, with a child nearby, telling his followers to stop competing and be at peace.

## ✝️ Good News

Jesus protects his little ones. He sees every cup of water given in his name.

**"Have salt among yourselves, and be at peace with one another"** (9:50).

Jesus takes sin seriously because he loves us. He went to the cross to deal with sin, so we could have life.

**Jesus cares about every little one and every small kindness. He calls his followers to be at peace.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that God's church is wider than any one group of people. Wherever the gospel is preached and the sacraments are given, God's people are there.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Mark 9:38-50 in late September (Lectionary 26).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Whoever is not against us is for us. Jesus protects his little ones, takes sin seriously, and calls his followers to be at peace.**

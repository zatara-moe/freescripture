# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** David (25:1).

**What it is.** A prayer for guidance and forgiveness. David is lonely and in trouble, and he remembers his own sins. He asks God to teach him the way and to remember him with love.

**How it's built.** In Hebrew, each verse begins with the next letter of the alphabet, A to Z. It's an ABC of trust.

**When the church sings it.** On the First Sunday of Advent, at the start of the church year.

## 🗺️ Map of the Psalm

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. I Lift Up My Soul | 25:1-3 | I trust you, God |
| 2. Show Me Your Ways | 25:4-10 | Teach me, and remember me in love |
| 3. Forgive My Iniquity | 25:11-15 | God guides the humble |
| 4. I Am Lonely | 25:16-22 | Turn to me and be gracious |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: I Lift Up My Soul

**"To You, O LORD, I lift up my soul; in You, my God, I trust"** (25:1-2). {v: 25:1, 25:2}

**"Surely none who wait for You will be put to shame"** (25:3). {v: 25:3}

### Section 2: Show Me Your Ways

**"Show me Your ways, O LORD; teach me Your paths"** (25:4). {v: 25:4}

**"Guide me in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my salvation; all day long I wait for You"** (25:5). {v: 25:5}

David asks God to remember two things and forget one. {v: 25:6, 25:7}

Remember your compassion and love. **"Remember not the sins of my youth"** (25:7). {v: 25:6, 25:7}

**"Good and upright is the LORD; therefore He shows sinners the way"** (25:8). {v: 25:8, 25:9, 25:10}

### Section 3: Forgive My Iniquity

**"For the sake of Your name, O LORD, forgive my iniquity, for it is great"** (25:11). {v: 25:11}

God teaches those who honor him. He shares his friendship with them. {v: 25:12, 25:13, 25:14}

**"My eyes are always on the LORD, for He will free my feet from the mesh"** (25:15). {v: 25:15}

The mesh is a net, a trap for catching animals. {v: 25:15}

### Section 4: I Am Lonely

**"Turn to me and be gracious, for I am lonely and afflicted"** (25:16). {v: 25:16}

His troubles keep growing. He has enemies. {v: 25:17, 25:18, 25:19}

**"Guard my soul and deliver me; let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in You"** (25:20). {v: 25:20, 25:21}

David ends by praying for all of God's people. {v: 25:22}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Psalm 25 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"I lift up my soul"** (25:1) | I bring my whole self to God. |
| **"Teach me Your paths"** (25:4) | Show me how to live. |
| **"Remember not the sins of my youth"** (25:7) | Please forget my past wrongs. |
| **"He shows sinners the way"** (25:8) | God guides people who've gone wrong. |
| **"Forgive my iniquity, for it is great"** (25:11) | My sin is big, but your name is bigger. |
| **"I am lonely and afflicted"** (25:16) | Honest words about how I feel. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Acrostic psalms** like this one may have helped people memorize them.†

**"For the sake of Your name"** means "because of who you are." David's hope rests on God's character, not his own record.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does God forget sins?** God doesn't lose his memory. But he chooses not to hold our sins against us (Jeremiah 31:34).

**Why read this at Advent?** Advent is a season of waiting. David says three times that he waits for the LORD (25:3, 25:5, 25:21).

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| David | Lonely | He says so (25:16) |
| David | Guilty | He remembers the sins of his youth (25:7) |
| David | Trusting | He lifts his soul to God (25:1) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God only guides good people."*

**What's true:** God guides those who humbly follow him.

**What the passage is mainly about:** **"Good and upright is the LORD; therefore He shows sinners the way"** (25:8). God guides because he is good, not because we are.

**Where Jesus is:** He said, "I am the way" (John 14:6), and he came to seek and save the lost.

## ✝️ Good News

God remembers you, not your sins.

**"Remember me according to Your loving devotion, because of Your goodness, O LORD"** (25:7).

He teaches sinners the way and turns to the lonely.

**David lifted his soul to God and asked to be taught and forgiven. God is good, so he shows sinners the way and remembers us with love.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther said that Christians should pray like David: not trusting in their own goodness, but in God's name and his mercy alone.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It sings Psalm 25 on the First Sunday of Advent in Year C, the First Sunday in Lent in Year B, and on Sundays in the fall.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**"To You, O LORD, I lift up my soul." David asked God to teach him the way, forgive his sins, and turn to him in his loneliness. Because God is good, he shows sinners the way.**

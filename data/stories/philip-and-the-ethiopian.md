# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Philip:** One of the seven men chosen to serve the church (6:5). He has been preaching in Samaria.
- **An Ethiopian official:** A powerful man in charge of the treasury of the queen of Ethiopia. He is a eunuch, a man who could not have children. Royal officials were often eunuchs.†
- **An angel of the Lord:** A messenger from God.

**Setting**

- A desert road going south from Jerusalem toward Gaza.

**When**

- A few years after Jesus rose from the dead.†

## 🧭 Before the Story

**After Stephen died, the believers were scattered.** They told the good news everywhere they went (8:1, 8:4).

**Philip preached in Samaria.** Many people believed and were baptized (8:5, 8:12).

**Jesus had said the good news would go to the ends of the earth** (1:8). For people in Jerusalem, Ethiopia was about as far as you could imagine.†

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: A Desert Road

An angel of the Lord told Philip, **"Get up and go south to the desert road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza"** (8:26). {v: 8:26}

So Philip went. On the road, he met an official from Ethiopia, in charge of the queen's treasury. {v: 8:27}

The man had gone to Jerusalem to worship God. Now he was riding home in his chariot, reading the prophet Isaiah. {v: 8:27, 8:28}

### Scene 2: "Do You Understand?"

The Spirit said to Philip, "Go over to that chariot." {v: 8:29}

Philip ran up and heard the man reading out loud. {v: 8:30}

**"Do you understand what you are reading?"** Philip asked (8:30). {v: 8:30}

**"How can I,"** he said, **"unless someone guides me?"** (8:31). He invited Philip to sit with him. {v: 8:31}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a fine chariot rolling slowly down a dusty road. A man in rich clothes squints at a scroll. Then a stranger jogs up beside him, out of breath.

### Scene 3: Like a Lamb

He was reading Isaiah: **"He was led like a sheep to the slaughter, and as a lamb before the shearer is silent, so He did not open His mouth"** (8:32). {v: 8:32, 8:33}

**"Tell me,"** he asked, **"who is the prophet talking about, himself or someone else?"** (8:34). {v: 8:34}

**"Then Philip began with this very Scripture and told him the good news about Jesus"** (8:35). {v: 8:35}

### Scene 4: "Here Is Water!"

They came to some water. **"Look, here is water! What is there to prevent me from being baptized?"** (8:36). {v: 8:36}

He stopped the chariot. Philip and the official went down into the water, and Philip baptized him. {v: 8:38}

When they came up, the Spirit carried Philip away. The official didn't see him again. {v: 8:39}

He **"went on his way rejoicing"** (8:39). {v: 8:39}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Acts 8:26-40 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Go south to the desert road"** (8:26) | God sent Philip to one person. |
| **"Do you understand what you are reading?"** (8:30) | Philip started with the man's own question. |
| **"Unless someone guides me"** (8:31) | It's good to learn the Bible with others. |
| **"Like a sheep to the slaughter"** (8:32) | Isaiah 53 points to Jesus's death. |
| **"Told him the good news about Jesus"** (8:35) | Jesus is the key to understanding Scripture. |
| **"What is there to prevent me from being baptized?"** (8:36) | Nothing kept him from God's family. |
| **"Went on his way rejoicing"** (8:39) | Baptism brings joy. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**"Ethiopia" in the Bible** usually means the kingdom of Kush, south of Egypt, in today's Sudan. "Candace" was a title for its queens.†

**Eunuchs were kept out of some parts of temple worship** (Deuteronomy 23:1). But Isaiah promised that God would welcome eunuchs who loved him (Isaiah 56:3-5).†

**Isaiah 53** describes God's servant who suffers for others. Christians see it fulfilled in Jesus.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Did Philip really disappear?** The Bible says the Spirit carried him away and he showed up in Azotus. God moved Philip on to his next job.†

**Why is verse 37 missing?** Some later copies of Acts add a verse where the man says he believes. The oldest copies don't have it, so many Bibles leave it out or put it in a note.†

**Can anyone be baptized?** Baptism is God's gift for everyone, from every nation. It's usually done with a pastor in a church.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"You have to understand everything before you can believe."*

**What's true:** God wants us to learn and understand.

**What the story is mainly about:** The official didn't understand, and he said so. God sent someone to explain. Then God welcomed him into his family through baptism, right there by the road.

**Where Jesus is:** In the very words of Isaiah the man was reading, the lamb who was led to the slaughter for us.

## ✝️ Good News

A man from far away was reading about a lamb. Philip told him the lamb's name is Jesus.

**"Then Philip began with this very Scripture and told him the good news about Jesus"** (8:35).

Nothing could keep him from being baptized. Nothing keeps you from God's love either.

**God sent Philip to a desert road to explain Isaiah to an Ethiopian official. He believed, was baptized, and went home rejoicing.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that Christ is the center of all Scripture. Read the Old Testament the way Philip did, and you find Jesus everywhere.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Acts 8:26-40 on the Fifth Sunday of Easter.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**An angel sent Philip to a desert road, where an Ethiopian official was reading Isaiah. Philip told him about Jesus, baptized him, and he went home rejoicing.**

# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jeremiah:** A young man from a family of priests in the village of Anathoth (Jeremiah 1:1).
- **The LORD:** God. LORD in capital letters stands for God's personal name. Jewish readers traditionally don't say it aloud, out of respect.

**Setting**

- Anathoth, a village near Jerusalem.

**When**

- About 627 BC, in the thirteenth year of King Josiah (Jeremiah 1:2).

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Judah was in danger.** The people kept turning to other gods. Powerful empires were rising.†

**God had called prophets before.** He called Samuel as a boy (1 Samuel 3:10). He called Isaiah with a burning coal (Isaiah 6:8).

**Here's what you know:** Jeremiah will warn Judah for forty years. It will be a hard job.

## 🗺️ Map of the Story

| Scene | Verses | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Before you were born | 1:4-5 | God says he knew Jeremiah before he was born |
| 2. "I am only a child" | 1:6-8 | Jeremiah objects, and God answers |
| 3. Words in your mouth | 1:9-10 | God touches Jeremiah's mouth |

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Before You Were Born

The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah. {v: 1:4}

**"Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, and before you were born I set you apart and appointed you as a prophet to the nations"** (1:5). {v: 1:5}

Set apart means chosen for a special purpose. {v: 1:5}

God knew Jeremiah before he was a baby. He had a plan for him all along. {v: 1:5}

### Scene 2: "I Am Only a Child"

Jeremiah was scared. **"Ah, Lord GOD,"** he said. **"I surely do not know how to speak, for I am only a child!"** (1:6). {v: 1:6}

He was young, maybe a teenager.† {v: 1:6}

**"Do not say, 'I am only a child,'"** God said (1:7). Jeremiah must go wherever God sends him and say whatever God tells him. {v: 1:7}

**"Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you to deliver you"** (1:8). {v: 1:8}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a young man shaking his head, hands up, backing away. "Not me. I can't." And then a voice: "I am with you."

### Scene 3: Words in Your Mouth

Then the LORD reached out his hand and touched Jeremiah's mouth. **"Behold, I have put My words in your mouth"** (1:9). {v: 1:9}

Jeremiah didn't have to come up with the words himself. God gave them. {v: 1:9}

God appointed him over nations and kingdoms, **"to uproot and tear down, to destroy and overthrow, to build and plant"** (1:10). {v: 1:10}

His message would bring hard news. But it would end with building and planting. {v: 1:10}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Jeremiah 1:4-10 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Before I formed you in the womb I knew you"** (1:5) | God knew Jeremiah before he was born. |
| **"I set you apart"** (1:5) | God chose him for a purpose. |
| **"I am only a child!"** (1:6) | Jeremiah felt too young and unready. |
| **"Do not be afraid of them, for I am with you"** (1:8) | God's presence was the answer to his fear. |
| **"I have put My words in your mouth"** (1:9) | God gives what he asks for. |
| **"To build and plant"** (1:10) | After hard times, God would bring new life. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Jeremiah had a hard life.** People mocked him, beat him, and threw him into a muddy pit (20:2, 38:6).

**He was called "the weeping prophet."** He cried over his people (9:1).†

**His message came true.** Babylon destroyed Jerusalem in 586 BC. But Jeremiah also promised a new covenant and a return home (31:31).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does God know me before I was born?** Jeremiah's words are about his own call. Psalm 139 says something similar about everyone: God saw us before we were born (Psalm 139:16).†

**Am I too young to serve God?** God told Jeremiah, "Do not say, 'I am only a child.'" Young people can serve God in many ways.

**Did God make Jeremiah's life easy?** No. God promised to be with him, not that it would be easy.

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jeremiah | Amazed | God knew him before birth (1:5) |
| Jeremiah | Afraid | He felt too young (1:6) |
| Jeremiah | Equipped | God put words in his mouth (1:9) |

# Why It Matters

- I'm too young :: God told Jeremiah not to say that. He can use you now.
- I don't know what to say :: God gave Jeremiah the words.
- I'm afraid :: "I am with you to deliver you."
- Does God know me? :: He knew you before you were born.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"You need to be older, smarter, or better before God can use you."*

**What's true:** God gives people time to grow and learn.

**What the story is mainly about:** Jeremiah felt too young and unprepared. God didn't argue about his age. He promised to be with him and to give him the words.

**Where Jesus is:** Jesus promised his followers, **"Do not worry about how to respond or what to say. In that hour you will be given what to say"** (Matthew 10:19).

## ✝️ Good News

Jeremiah said, "I can't." God said, "I am with you."

**"Before I formed you in the womb I knew you"** (1:5).

God knows you completely. He doesn't wait for you to be ready. He makes you ready, and he goes with you.

**God knew Jeremiah before he was born. When Jeremiah said, "I am only a child," God said, "Do not be afraid, I am with you," and put his words in his mouth.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that every Christian has a calling from God. God gives each person work to do in the world, and the gifts to do it.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads Jeremiah 1:4-10 on the Fourth Sunday after the Epiphany, next to Jesus being rejected at Nazareth.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**God knew Jeremiah before he was born and called him to be a prophet. "I am only a child," Jeremiah said. God answered, "Do not be afraid, for I am with you."**

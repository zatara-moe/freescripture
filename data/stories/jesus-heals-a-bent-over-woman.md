# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God.
- **A woman:** She has been bent over for eighteen years. She can't stand up straight.
- **The synagogue leader:** The man in charge of the synagogue. He is upset.
- **The crowd:** People at the synagogue on the Sabbath.

**Setting**

- A synagogue, a meeting place where Jewish people gathered to pray and hear Scripture.

**When**

- One Sabbath, about 2,000 years ago, while Jesus was traveling toward Jerusalem.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**The Sabbath was God's day of rest.** On the seventh day, no one was to work (Exodus 20:8-10).

**Jesus had healed on the Sabbath before.** He healed a man with a withered hand, and leaders started plotting against him (6:6-11).

**Jesus had just told a parable about a fig tree.** The owner wanted to cut it down, but the gardener asked for one more year (13:6-9).

## 🗺️ Map of the Story

| Scene | Verses | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Eighteen years | 13:10-11 | A woman can't stand up straight |
| 2. Set free | 13:12-13 | Jesus heals her |
| 3. "Come on the other days" | 13:14 | The synagogue leader is angry |
| 4. A daughter of Abraham | 13:15-17 | Jesus answers, and the crowd rejoices |

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Eighteen Years

One Sabbath, Jesus was teaching in a synagogue. {v: 13:10}

A woman there had been bent over for eighteen years. She couldn't stand up straight at all. {v: 13:11}

Luke says a spirit had crippled her. {v: 13:11}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a woman at the back of the room. For eighteen years she has seen mostly the floor, people's feet, the dirt road. She can't look up to see who is teaching.

### Scene 2: Set Free

When Jesus saw her, he called her over. {v: 13:12}

**"Woman, you are set free from your disability"** (13:12). {v: 13:12}

He put his hands on her. Right away, she stood up straight. {v: 13:13}

She began to praise God. {v: 13:13}

### Scene 3: "Come on the Other Days"

But the synagogue leader was angry, because Jesus had healed on the Sabbath. {v: 13:14}

He didn't speak to Jesus. He spoke to the crowd. {v: 13:14}

**"There are six days for work,"** he told the crowd. **"So come and be healed on those days and not on the Sabbath"** (13:14). {v: 13:14}

### Scene 4: A Daughter of Abraham

**"You hypocrites!"** the Lord replied (13:15). A hypocrite is someone who says one thing and does another. {v: 13:15}

Jesus reminded them that they untie their ox or donkey on the Sabbath and lead it to water. {v: 13:15}

**"Then should not this daughter of Abraham, whom Satan has kept bound for eighteen long years, be released from her bondage on the Sabbath day?"** (13:16). {v: 13:16}

"Daughter of Abraham" means she belonged to God's people, an heir of God's promise. {v: 13:16}

Jesus's opponents were ashamed. **"And the whole crowd rejoiced at all the glorious things He was doing"** (13:17). {v: 13:17}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Luke 13:10-17 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Eighteen years"** (13:11) | She had suffered a very long time. |
| **"When Jesus saw her, He called her over"** (13:12) | She didn't ask. Jesus noticed her. |
| **"You are set free"** (13:12) | Jesus brought freedom, not just healing. |
| **"Come and be healed on those days"** (13:14) | The leader cared more about the rule than the woman. |
| **"This daughter of Abraham"** (13:16) | She was precious, a full member of God's people. |
| **"The whole crowd rejoiced"** (13:17) | Ordinary people saw how good this was. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**God's law allowed caring for animals on the Sabbath.** Teachers agreed that animals could be untied and led to water.†

**"Daughter of Abraham" was a rare title.** "Son of Abraham" was common. Calling a woman this honored her.†

**Women often stood apart in synagogues.** Jesus called her into the middle, where everyone could see.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Is every illness caused by an evil spirit?** No. Luke was a doctor (Colossians 4:14), and he tells many healing stories with no spirit involved. Most illness has ordinary causes. Doctors and medicine are good gifts.†

**If someone has a disability, did they do something wrong?** No. Jesus never blamed this woman. He called her a daughter of Abraham.

**Should we work on Sundays?** Christians have different ideas about this. Jesus's point is that doing good, like helping someone in need, is always right.

| The woman | Thankful | She praised God (13:13) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God only notices important people."*

**What's true:** God knows everyone, including leaders and important people.

**What the story is mainly about:** Jesus noticed a woman bent over at the back of the synagogue. He called her forward, set her free, and called her a daughter of Abraham in front of everyone.

**Where Jesus is:** In a synagogue on the day of rest, setting someone free who had waited eighteen years.

## ✝️ Good News

For eighteen years, she couldn't look up. Then Jesus called her name.

**"Woman, you are set free from your disability"** (13:12).

Jesus sees people the world overlooks. He sets people free. The day of rest became a day of freedom.

**Jesus saw a woman no one noticed and set her free. He calls you a beloved child of God.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that Christ came to free us from everything that binds us: sin, death, and the devil. In the Small Catechism, he wrote that Jesus redeemed us, freeing us so that we may belong to him.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads Luke 13:10-17 in late August (Lectionary 21).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Jesus saw a woman bent over for eighteen years, called her over, and set her free. He sees people the world overlooks.**

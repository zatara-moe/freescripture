# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God. It is the night before he dies.
- **Simon Peter:** One of Jesus's closest followers. He often speaks first.
- **Judas Iscariot:** One of the twelve disciples. He is about to betray Jesus.
- **The disciple whom Jesus loved:** A disciple who sits next to Jesus at the meal. John's Gospel does not give his name.
- **The other disciples:** Jesus's closest followers, at supper with him.

**Setting**

- A room in Jerusalem where Jesus and his disciples share a meal.

**When**

- Thursday evening, just before the Passover feast, about 2,000 years ago. The next day, Jesus will be crucified.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus had come to Jerusalem for the last time.** Crowds waved palm branches and shouted, **"Hosanna!"** (12:13).

**Jesus knew his hour had come.** He had said, **"Unless a kernel of wheat falls to the ground and dies, it remains only a seed"** (12:24). He was talking about his death.

**Washing feet was a servant's job.** People walked dusty roads in sandals. When guests arrived, a servant usually washed their feet.

**Here's what you know that the disciples don't:** Jesus knows that Judas will betray him tonight (13:11).

## 🗺️ Map of the Story

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Loved to the End

It was just before the Passover feast. Jesus knew that his hour had come to leave this world and go back to the Father. {v: 13:1}

**"Having loved His own who were in the world, He loved them to the very end"** (13:1). {v: 13:1}

They were eating supper. The devil had already put the idea into Judas's heart to betray Jesus. To betray means to turn against someone who trusts you. {v: 13:2}

Jesus knew that the Father had put all things in his hands. He knew he had come from God and was going back to God. {v: 13:3}

### Scene 2: A Towel and a Basin

So Jesus got up from the meal. He took off his outer clothes and wrapped a towel around his waist. {v: 13:4}

He poured water into a basin, a large bowl. Then he began to wash his disciples' feet and dry them with the towel. {v: 13:5}

The Lord was doing a servant's job. {v: 13:5}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture the room going quiet as Jesus kneels on the floor. Water splashes in the bowl. One by one, he takes each dusty foot in his hands.

### Scene 3: "Never!"

Jesus came to Simon Peter. **"Lord, are You going to wash my feet?"** Peter asked (13:6). {v: 13:6}

Jesus said, **"You do not realize now what I am doing, but later you will understand"** (13:7). {v: 13:7}

**"Never shall You wash my feet!"** Peter said. {v: 13:8}

Jesus answered, **"Unless I wash you, you have no part with Me"** (13:8). {v: 13:8}

Peter changed his mind fast. **"Then, Lord,"** he said, **"not only my feet, but my hands and my head as well!"** (13:9). {v: 13:9}

Jesus said that someone who has already bathed only needs his feet washed. **"And you are clean, though not all of you"** (13:10). He knew who would betray him. {v: 13:10, 13:11}

### Scene 4: An Example

When Jesus had finished, he put his clothes back on and sat down with them again. {v: 13:12}

**"Do you know what I have done for you?"** he asked (13:12). {v: 13:12}

He said, "You call me Teacher and Lord, and you are right, because I am." {v: 13:13}

**"So if I, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also should wash one another's feet"** (13:14). {v: 13:14}

**"I have set you an example so that you should do as I have done for you"** (13:15). {v: 13:15}

A servant is not greater than his master. If they know these things, Jesus said, they will be blessed if they do them. {v: 13:16, 13:17}

### Scene 5: Into the Night

Then Jesus was troubled. **"Truly, truly, I tell you, one of you will betray Me"** (13:21). {v: 13:21}

The disciples looked at one another. They had no idea who he meant. {v: 13:22}

The disciple whom Jesus loved was sitting right next to him. Peter signaled to him to ask who it was. {v: 13:23, 13:24}

**"Lord, who is it?"** he asked (13:25). {v: 13:25}

Jesus said it was the one he would give a piece of bread to, after dipping it in the dish. Then he dipped the bread and gave it to Judas. {v: 13:26}

Jesus said to Judas, **"What you are about to do, do quickly"** (13:27). {v: 13:27}

No one else understood. Judas kept the money bag, so some thought Jesus was sending him to buy something or give to the poor. {v: 13:28, 13:29}

Judas took the bread. Then **"Judas went out into the night"** (13:30). {v: 13:30}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture the door opening onto a dark street. Judas steps out. The door closes. Inside, the lamps still burn.

### Scene 6: A New Commandment

When Judas had gone, Jesus spoke to the others. {v: 13:31}

He said the time had come for God to be glorified. Glorified means shown in all his greatness. {v: 13:31, 13:32}

**"Little children, I am with you only a little while longer"** (13:33). {v: 13:33}

Then he gave them a new commandment. **"Love one another. As I have loved you, so you also must love one another"** (13:34). {v: 13:34}

**"By this everyone will know that you are My disciples, if you love one another"** (13:35). {v: 13:35}

Later that night, Peter said he would die for Jesus. Jesus told him that before the rooster crowed, Peter would deny him three times (13:37-38). {v: 13:37, 13:38}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

John 13:1-35 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"He loved them to the very end"** (13:1) | Jesus loved his disciples all the way, to the cross. |
| **"Wrapped a towel around His waist"** (13:4) | The Lord took the place of a servant. |
| **"Unless I wash you, you have no part with Me"** (13:8) | We need Jesus to make us clean. We can't do it ourselves. |
| **"You are clean, though not all of you"** (13:10) | Jesus knew Judas would betray him. He washed Judas's feet too. |
| **"You also should wash one another's feet"** (13:14) | Jesus's followers serve each other, even in humble ways. |
| **"A new commandment I give you: Love one another"** (13:34) | What is new is the measure: love one another as Jesus loved us. |
| **"By this everyone will know"** (13:35) | Love among Christians shows the world who Jesus is. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**People ate lying on low couches.** They reclined on their side around a low table, with their feet stretched out behind them. That's how the disciple whom Jesus loved could lean back against Jesus (13:25).†

**Feet got very dirty.** Roads were dusty, and people wore sandals or went barefoot. Foot washing was usually done by the lowest servant in the house.†

**Matthew, Mark, and Luke tell about the bread and the cup at this meal.** John tells about the foot washing instead. Together they show what Jesus did that night.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Do Christians have to wash each other's feet?** Some churches wash feet on Maundy Thursday, the Thursday before Easter, to remember this night. Jesus's main point was to serve each other humbly, in many ways.

**Why was Peter so upset?** It felt wrong to Peter for his Lord to do a servant's job. He wanted to protect Jesus's honor. Jesus wanted Peter to receive what he was giving.

**Did Jesus wash Judas's feet?** John says Jesus washed the disciples' feet, and Judas was there (13:2, 13:5). John doesn't say Judas was skipped. Many readers believe Jesus washed his feet too.†

**"Satan entered into him." Did Judas have no choice?** John says the devil put the idea in Judas's heart (13:2). Judas still chose. The Bible holds both truths together.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Peter | Shocked | His Lord was washing his feet (13:6, 13:8) |
| Peter | Eager | He wanted to be washed all over (13:9) |
| Jesus | Troubled | He knew one of them would betray him (13:21) |
| The disciples | Confused | They didn't know who Jesus meant (13:22) |

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**Which is harder for you?** Tap one.

- Serving others :: Jesus knelt and washed feet, even Judas's. He gives the love we need to serve.
- Letting others help me :: That was Peter's problem. Jesus said, "Unless I wash you, you have no part with Me." It's okay to receive.
- Loving someone who hurt me :: Jesus washed the feet of the man who betrayed him. You can ask him for help, one step at a time. You don't have to stay near someone who is hurting you.
- I don't know yet :: Peter didn't understand right away either. Jesus said, "Later you will understand."

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Being a Christian mostly means being nice to people."*

**What's true:** Jesus told his followers to love one another and serve each other.

**What the story is mainly about:** First, Jesus serves us. He washed his disciples, then went to the cross to wash away sin. Our love for each other comes from his love for us: **"As I have loved you"** (13:34).

**Where Jesus is:** On his knees with a towel. The Lord of everything, serving his friends, on the night before he died for them.

## ✝️ Good News

Peter said, "Never!" But Jesus said, "Unless I wash you, you have no part with Me."

**"Having loved His own who were in the world, He loved them to the very end"** (13:1).

The next day, Jesus gave his life on the cross. He washes us clean from every sin. That is the love he gives us to share.

**Jesus served his friends and loved them to the end. He washes us clean, and his love flows through us to others.**

## 📚 From Luther

In "The Freedom of a Christian" (1520), Luther wrote that a Christian is a perfectly free lord of all, subject to none, and a perfectly dutiful servant of all, subject to all. We are free because Christ has saved us. And because we are free, we serve our neighbor, as Christ served us.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches read John 13:1-17, 31b-35 every year on Maundy Thursday, the Thursday before Easter. The Revised Common Lectionary also reads John 13:31-35 on the Fifth Sunday of Easter in Year C.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Jesus washed his disciples' feet and loved them to the end. Loved by him, we love and serve one another.**

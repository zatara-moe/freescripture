# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Where we are.** It is the night before Jesus dies. He has washed his disciples' feet and told them he is going away (13:33). He has promised them the Holy Spirit and his peace (14:26-27).

**What this chapter is.** This is part of Jesus's last long talk with his disciples, called the Farewell Discourse (John 14 to 16). Jesus uses one picture all through this section: a grapevine.

**Why a vine.** Grapevines grew all over the land of Israel. In the Old Testament, God called his people a vine he had planted (Psalm 80:8). Now Jesus says he is the true vine.

**How to read it.** Watch for one word that keeps coming back: "remain." It means to stay, to live in, to keep holding on. In the BSB, it comes twelve times in these seventeen verses.

## 🗺️ Map of the Passage

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The True Vine | 15:1-3 | Jesus is the vine. The Father takes care of it |
| 2. Remain in Me | 15:4-8 | Branches bear fruit only by staying on the vine |
| 3. Remain in My Love | 15:9-11 | Jesus loves them as the Father loves him |
| 4. Friends | 15:12-17 | Love one another, as Jesus loves his friends |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: The True Vine

**"I am the true vine, and My Father is the keeper of the vineyard"** (15:1). {v: 15:1}

This is a comparison. Jesus is not a plant. He is saying that he is the source of life for his people, the way a vine gives life to its branches. {v: 15:1}

The keeper of a vineyard is the gardener who takes care of the vines. That is the Father. {v: 15:1}

The gardener cuts off branches that bear no fruit. He prunes the ones that do bear fruit, so they bear even more. To prune means to cut back a plant so it grows better. {v: 15:2}

**"You are already clean because of the word I have spoken to you"** (15:3). {v: 15:3}

### Section 2: Remain in Me

**"Remain in Me, and I will remain in you"** (15:4). {v: 15:4}

A branch can't bear fruit on its own. It has to stay connected to the vine. {v: 15:4}

**"I am the vine and you are the branches. The one who remains in Me, and I in him, will bear much fruit. For apart from Me you can do nothing"** (15:5). {v: 15:5}

Fruit means the good things that grow in a life connected to Jesus, like love, kindness, and faith. {v: 15:5}

A branch that is cut off dries up. People gather dry branches and burn them. {v: 15:6}

But if they remain in Jesus and his words remain in them, they can ask what they wish, and it will be done. {v: 15:7}

When they bear much fruit, the Father is honored. {v: 15:8}

> 🧵 **Follow the thread**
> The apostle Paul lists the fruit of the Spirit: **"love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control"** (Galatians 5:22-23).

### Section 3: Remain in My Love

**"As the Father has loved Me, so have I loved you. Remain in My love"** (15:9). {v: 15:9}

Jesus loves his disciples with the same love the Father has for him. {v: 15:9}

They remain in his love by keeping his commandments, just as Jesus kept his Father's commandments. {v: 15:10}

Jesus told them all this **"so that My joy may be in you and your joy may be complete"** (15:11). Complete means full, with nothing missing. {v: 15:11}

### Section 4: Friends

**"This is My commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you"** (15:12). {v: 15:12}

**"Greater love has no one than this, that he lay down his life for his friends"** (15:13). The next day, Jesus would do exactly that. {v: 15:13}

Jesus said they were his friends. A servant doesn't know what his master is doing. But Jesus had told them everything he learned from his Father. {v: 15:14, 15:15}

**"You did not choose Me, but I chose you"** (15:16). Jesus chose them to go and bear fruit that lasts. {v: 15:16}

**"This is My command to you: Love one another"** (15:17). {v: 15:17}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

John 15:1-17 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"I am the true vine"** (15:1) | Jesus is the source of real life for his people. |
| **"He prunes to make it even more fruitful"** (15:2) | God works in our lives, sometimes through hard times, to help us grow. |
| **"Remain in Me"** (15:4) | Stay connected to Jesus, through his word and his gifts. |
| **"Apart from Me you can do nothing"** (15:5) | We can't make ourselves good on our own. Life comes from Jesus. |
| **"Remain in My love"** (15:9) | Jesus's love is a place to live, not a prize to win. |
| **"Greater love has no one than this"** (15:13) | Jesus laid down his life for his friends. |
| **"You did not choose Me, but I chose you"** (15:16) | Jesus starts the friendship. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Vineyards were everywhere.** Grapes were one of the main crops in Israel. People ate them fresh, dried them into raisins, and made wine.†

**Pruning was done every year.** Gardeners cut vines back hard so they would grow more grapes. Cut-off branches dried quickly and were burned.†

**God's people were called a vine.** **"You uprooted a vine from Egypt"** (Psalm 80:8). Jesus says he is the true vine.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Am I a plant?** No. This is a comparison. A branch lives because it's joined to the vine. You live with God because you are joined to Jesus.

**Does the part about burning branches mean God is waiting to throw me away?** These are serious words. But they come in a talk meant to comfort. Jesus says, **"You are already clean"** (15:3), and **"I chose you"** (15:16). Jesus is holding on to his people. If you're worried, that worry shows you want to stay close to him. Stay close. He wants you there.

**How do I "remain" in Jesus?** Not by trying harder. Jesus says his words remain in you (15:7). You remain in him by hearing his word, through Baptism, and in the Lord's Supper, where he gives himself to you.†

**Does Jesus give us anything we ask?** Jesus is talking about people whose lives are joined to his and full of his words (15:7). Their prayers grow to match what he wants. God always hears.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |

| Jesus | Loving | He loves them as the Father loves him (15:9) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"If you try hard enough, you'll become a better person."*

**What's true:** Jesus wants his followers to bear good fruit and love one another.

**What the passage is mainly about:** A branch doesn't make fruit by trying hard. It stays on the vine, and the fruit grows. **"Apart from Me you can do nothing"** (15:5). The good things in a Christian's life come from Jesus.

**Where Jesus is:** He is the vine, holding up every branch and giving it life. And he is the friend who lays down his life.

## ✝️ Good News

You didn't pick yourself to be part of the vine. Jesus chose you.

**"As the Father has loved Me, so have I loved you. Remain in My love"** (15:9).

Jesus laid down his life for his friends. He calls you his friend. His love is where you live.

**Jesus is the vine, and you are a branch. He chose you, loves you, and gives you life.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that good works don't make a person good. A good tree bears good fruit. It is because Christ has made us his own that good works grow in our lives.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads John 15:1-8 on the Fifth Sunday of Easter, and John 15:9-17 on the Sixth Sunday of Easter.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Jesus is the vine, and we are the branches. He chose us, loves us, and gives us life, and in him we bear fruit.**

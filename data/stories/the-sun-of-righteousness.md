# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who he was.** Malachi, whose name means "my messenger." He spoke to God's people around 450 BC, after they had come home from exile and rebuilt the temple (1:1).†

**What was happening.** The people were tired and doubtful. Worship had become careless. Priests were cutting corners. People asked, "Where is the God of justice?" (2:17).

**What this passage is.** God's answer. A messenger is coming to prepare the way, and then the Lord himself. Malachi is the last book of the Old Testament, and these are its final words.

**How to read it.** Watch for the "day" that is coming. For some it will burn. For others, the sun will rise with healing.

## 🗺️ Map of the Message

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. My Messenger | 3:1 | God will send someone to prepare the way |
| 2. A Refiner's Fire | 3:2-4 | The Lord will purify his people |
| 3. Healing in Its Wings | 4:1-3 | A day of burning, and a day of sunrise |
| 4. Elijah Is Coming | 4:4-6 | Hearts turned toward each other |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: My Messenger

**"Behold, I will send My messenger, who will prepare the way before Me"** (3:1). {v: 3:1}

**"Then the Lord whom you seek will suddenly come to His temple"** (3:1). {v: 3:1}

The people asked where God was. God says: I'm coming. {v: 3:1}

### Section 2: A Refiner's Fire

**"But who can endure the day of His coming? And who can stand when He appears?"** (3:2). {v: 3:2}

**"For He will be like a refiner's fire, like a launderer's soap"** (3:2). {v: 3:2}

A refiner heats silver until the impurities burn away. A launderer scrubs clothes clean. {v: 3:2, 3:3}

God won't destroy his people. He will make them pure, so their worship pleases him again. {v: 3:3, 3:4}

### Section 3: Healing in Its Wings

**"For behold, the day is coming, burning like a furnace"** (4:1). {v: 4:1}

For the proud and evil, that day will be like fire burning straw. {v: 4:1}

**"But for you who fear My name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings"** (4:2). {v: 4:2}

Its "wings" are its rays of light, spreading out like a bird's wings. {v: 4:2}

**"And you will go out and leap like calves from the stall"** (4:2). {v: 4:2, 4:3}

Picture young calves let out of the barn in spring, jumping for joy. {v: 4:2}

### Section 4: Elijah Is Coming

Remember the law God gave through Moses. {v: 4:4}

**"Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and awesome Day of the LORD"** (4:5). {v: 4:5}

**"And he will turn the hearts of the fathers to their children, and the hearts of the children to their fathers"** (4:6). {v: 4:6}

These are the last words of the Old Testament. Then there was silence for about 400 years. {v: 4:6}

> 🧵 **Follow the thread**
> Jesus said John the Baptist was the messenger and the "Elijah" Malachi promised (Matthew 11:10-14). An angel told John's father that John would turn "the hearts of the fathers to their children" (Luke 1:17).

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Malachi 3:1-4; 4:1-6 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"I will send My messenger"** (3:1) | Someone will come first to get people ready. |
| **"Suddenly come to His temple"** (3:1) | God himself will come to his people. |
| **"Like a refiner's fire"** (3:2) | God makes us pure, burning away what's wrong. |
| **"The sun of righteousness will rise"** (4:2) | God's saving light is coming. |
| **"With healing in its wings"** (4:2) | It brings healing and new life. |
| **"Elijah the prophet"** (4:5) | A prophet like Elijah will prepare the way. |
| **"Turn the hearts of the fathers to their children"** (4:6) | Broken families will be restored. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Refining silver** meant melting it in a hot fire and skimming off the dross, the impurities that floated to the top.†

**Elijah** never died. He was taken up to heaven in a whirlwind (2 Kings 2:11). So people expected him to return.

**In the Christian Old Testament,** Malachi comes last, right before Matthew. The Hebrew Bible orders the books differently.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Was John the Baptist really Elijah?** Not Elijah come back to life. Jesus meant John came "in the spirit and power of Elijah" (Luke 1:17), fulfilling Malachi's promise.

**Is the sun of righteousness Jesus?** Christians have long heard it that way. Charles Wesley's Christmas hymn "Hark! The Herald Angels Sing" says: "Hail the Sun of Righteousness! Light and life to all he brings."

**Should we be afraid of the Day of the Lord?** It is serious. But for those who fear God's name, it's a sunrise.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God went silent and isn't coming back."*

**What's true:** After Malachi, no prophet spoke for about 400 years.

**What the passage is mainly about:** The silence ended with a promise. **"Behold, I will send My messenger"** (3:1). John the Baptist came, and then Jesus.

**Where Jesus is:** He is the Lord who came to his temple, the Sun of Righteousness with healing in his wings.

## ✝️ Good News

The sun is rising.

**"But for you who fear My name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings"** (4:2).

God sent his messenger, and then he came himself, in Jesus, to heal and to make us clean.

**God promised to send his messenger and then come himself. He refines his people like silver, and for those who fear him, the sun of righteousness rises with healing in its wings.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther saw John the Baptist as a model preacher. Like a finger pointing, John directed people away from himself to Jesus: "Behold the Lamb of God."†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It reads Malachi 3:1-4 on the Second Sunday of Advent in Year C and on the Presentation of Our Lord. Malachi 4:1-2a is read in November in Year C (Lectionary 33).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**God promised to send his messenger to prepare the way, and then to come himself. He refines his people like silver, and the sun of righteousness rises with healing in its wings.**

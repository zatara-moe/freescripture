# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God. He is on his way to Jerusalem to die.
- **The twelve disciples:** Jesus's closest followers. They keep arguing about who is most important.
- **James and John:** Two brothers among the twelve, sons of Zebedee.
- **A little child:** A child Jesus takes in his arms.

**Setting**

- Galilee and Capernaum, then the road toward Jerusalem.

**When**

- About 2,000 years ago, in the weeks before Jesus died.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus had told his disciples that he would die.** He said he would suffer, be killed, and rise again (8:31).

**Peter, James, and John saw Jesus shine on a mountain.** God's voice said, **"This is My beloved Son. Listen to Him!"** (9:7).

**Here's what you know that the disciples keep missing:** Jesus is going to Jerusalem to give his life. They are thinking about who will be most important when he is king.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: A Second Warning

Jesus and his disciples traveled through Galilee. Jesus didn't want anyone to know, because he was teaching his disciples. {v: 9:30, 9:31}

He told them, **"The Son of Man will be delivered into the hands of men. They will kill Him, and after three days He will rise"** (9:31). "Son of Man" is a name Jesus used for himself. {v: 9:31}

They didn't understand. And they were afraid to ask him. {v: 9:32}

### Scene 2: An Argument on the Road

They came to Capernaum. In the house, Jesus asked them, **"What were you discussing on the way?"** (9:33). {v: 9:33}

They were silent. On the road, they had been arguing about which of them was the greatest. {v: 9:34}

Jesus sat down and called the twelve. **"If anyone wants to be first, he must be the last of all and the servant of all"** (9:35). {v: 9:35}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture twelve grown men suddenly staring at the floor, nobody wanting to answer. Jesus waits.

### Scene 3: A Child in His Arms

Jesus had a little child stand among them. He took the child in his arms. {v: 9:36}

**"Whoever welcomes one of these little children in My name welcomes Me, and whoever welcomes Me welcomes not only Me, but the One who sent Me"** (9:37). {v: 9:37}

Back then, children had no power or importance. Jesus said welcoming a child is welcoming him. {v: 9:37}

### Scene 4: A Big Request

Later, on the road to Jerusalem, Jesus told them a third time that he would be killed and rise again (10:33-34). {v: 10:35}

Then James and John came to him. **"Teacher, we want You to do for us whatever we ask"** (10:35). {v: 10:35}

**"What do you want Me to do for you?"** Jesus asked (10:36). {v: 10:36}

They asked to sit at his right and left in his glory, the two most important seats. {v: 10:37}

**"You do not know what you are asking,"** Jesus said (10:38). He asked if they could drink the cup he would drink. The cup meant the suffering ahead of him. {v: 10:38}

**"We can,"** they said (10:39). Jesus said they would share in his suffering. But the seats at his right and left were not his to give. {v: 10:39, 10:40}

### Scene 5: The Servant of All

When the other ten heard about this, they were angry with James and John. {v: 10:41}

So Jesus called them all together. He said that rulers in the world like to boss people around. {v: 10:42}

**"But it shall not be this way among you. Instead, whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant"** (10:43). {v: 10:43}

**"And whoever wants to be first must be the slave of all"** (10:44). {v: 10:44}

**"For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many"** (10:45). {v: 10:45}

A ransom is a price paid to set someone free. {v: 10:45}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Mark 9:30-37 and 10:35-45 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"They were silent"** (9:34) | They knew their argument didn't fit with what Jesus had just said. |
| **"The servant of all"** (9:35) | In God's kingdom, greatness looks like serving. |
| **"Whoever welcomes one of these little children"** (9:37) | People the world overlooks matter to Jesus. |
| **"You do not know what you are asking"** (10:38) | James and John wanted glory without understanding the cross. |
| **"It shall not be this way among you"** (10:43) | Jesus's followers don't use power to boss people around. |
| **"Not... to be served, but to serve"** (10:45) | Jesus is the greatest, and he became the servant of all. |
| **"A ransom for many"** (10:45) | Jesus gave his life to set us free. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Seats at a king's right and left were the places of honor.** The people closest to the king were the most powerful.†

**Children had little status.** They could not own property or make decisions. They were not seen as important.†

**Jesus's question about the "cup" came true.** James was killed for following Jesus (Acts 12:2). John lived long and suffered too.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Is it wrong to want to be good at something?** No. Jesus isn't against doing your best. He's against wanting to be over others. In his kingdom, the best gifts are used to serve.

**Does serving mean letting people treat you badly?** No. Serving is a choice to help out of love. It doesn't mean staying where someone hurts you. If someone is hurting you, please tell a trusted adult.

**Who sat at Jesus's right and left?** When Jesus was crucified, two criminals were crucified with him, one on his right and one on his left (15:27). That was where Jesus's "glory" began.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The disciples | Afraid | They didn't understand and were afraid to ask (9:32) |
| The disciples | Embarrassed | They had been arguing about who was greatest (9:34) |
| James and John | Ambitious | They wanted the best seats (10:37) |
| The other ten | Angry | James and John asked first (10:41) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Important people deserve to be served."*

**What's true:** God gives some people leadership, and it is right to respect them.

**What the story is mainly about:** Jesus is the most important person who ever lived, and he came to serve. **"Did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many"** (10:45). In his kingdom, greatness is measured by serving.

**Where Jesus is:** With a child in his arms. And on the road to Jerusalem, where he would give his life as a ransom.

## ✝️ Good News

The disciples were arguing about who was greatest. Jesus was on his way to die for them.

**"For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many"** (10:45).

Jesus didn't wait until his friends stopped being selfish. He served them anyway, all the way to the cross.

**Jesus is the greatest, and he became the servant of all. He gave his life as a ransom to set us free.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that a Christian is free, because Christ has saved us. And that very freedom makes us glad to serve our neighbor, the way Christ served us.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Mark 9:30-37 in September (Lectionary 25), and Mark 10:35-45 in October (Lectionary 29).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**In Jesus's kingdom, the greatest is the servant of all. Jesus came not to be served, but to serve, and to give his life for us.**

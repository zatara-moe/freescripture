# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who is speaking.** Moses, near the end of his life. The book of Deuteronomy is his long farewell to God's people.

**Where they are.** On the plains of Moab, by the Jordan River. Forty years in the desert are over. They're about to enter the promised land, but Moses won't go with them (Deuteronomy 34:4).

**What this passage is.** The Shema, the most important prayer for Jewish people, still prayed every morning and evening. Shema is the Hebrew word for "hear."

**Why it matters.** When asked for the greatest commandment, Jesus quoted these words (Mark 12:29-30).

## 🗺️ Map of the Message

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. So You May Live | 6:1-3 | God's commands are for life in the land |
| 2. Hear, O Israel | 6:4-5 | The LORD is One. Love him with everything |
| 3. On Your Heart | 6:6-9 | Talk about God's words everywhere |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: So You May Live

Moses says these commandments are for living in the new land God is giving them. {v: 6:1}

They're for **"you and your children and grandchildren"** (6:2). {v: 6:2}

God's commands are meant to bring good life. {v: 6:3}

### Section 2: Hear, O Israel

**"Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is One"** (6:4). {v: 6:4}

There is only one true God. Not many gods like the nations around them believed in. {v: 6:4}

**"And you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength"** (6:5). {v: 6:5}

All your heart: your feelings and choices. All your soul: your whole life. All your strength: everything you have. {v: 6:5}

### Section 3: On Your Heart

**"These words I am commanding you today are to be upon your hearts"** (6:6). {v: 6:6}

**"And you shall teach them diligently to your children"** (6:7). Diligently means carefully, again and again. {v: 6:7}

Talk about them **"when you sit at home and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up"** (6:7). {v: 6:7}

In other words, all the time. {v: 6:7}

Tie them on your hands and forehead. Write them on your doorposts and gates. {v: 6:8, 6:9}

> 🧵 **Follow the thread**
> When a scribe asked Jesus which commandment is most important, Jesus answered with this very passage (Mark 12:29-30).

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Deuteronomy 6:1-9 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Hear, O Israel"** (6:4) | Listen carefully. This matters most. |
| **"The LORD is One"** (6:4) | There is one true God. |
| **"Love the LORD your God with all your heart"** (6:5) | God wants all of you, not just part. |
| **"Upon your hearts"** (6:6) | God's word should live inside us. |
| **"Teach them diligently to your children"** (6:7) | Faith is passed on at home. |
| **"When you sit at home and when you walk along the road"** (6:7) | Talk about God in everyday life. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Some Jewish people still wear tefillin.** These small leather boxes hold Scripture and are tied to the arm and forehead during prayer.†

**Many Jewish homes have a mezuzah.** It's a small case on the doorpost with these words inside.†

**Luther's Small Catechism was made for homes.** Luther wrote it so "the head of the family" could teach it to the household.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Do Christians have to write Bible verses on doors?** Not as a rule. The point is keeping God's word close in everyday life. Some Christians hang a cross or a verse on the wall.

**Can anyone love God with everything?** No one does perfectly. Jesus did, for us. Then he helps us grow in love.

**Is this only for Jewish people?** These words were given to Israel. Jesus called it the greatest commandment for everyone.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Faith is just for Sundays."*

**What's true:** Gathering for worship on Sundays matters.

**What the passage is mainly about:** God's words belong in every part of life: at home, on the road, morning and night. Faith is something families live and talk about every day.

**Where Jesus is:** Jesus called this the greatest commandment. Then he kept it perfectly, loving God with all his heart, all the way to the cross.

## ✝️ Good News

God gave his people a simple center: love the one true God with everything.

**"Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is One"** (6:4).

We don't love God perfectly. But God loves us perfectly. In Jesus, he loved us with all his heart, soul, and strength.

**There is one true God. Love him with all your heart, soul, and strength, and talk about his words everywhere you go.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther's Small Catechism has prayers for morning and evening, and blessings for meals. He wanted God's word in every part of family life, just like Deuteronomy 6.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Deuteronomy 6:1-9 in late October or early November (Lectionary 31), next to Jesus naming the greatest commandment.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**"Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is One." Love God with all your heart, soul, and strength, and keep his words with you everywhere.**

# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** Paul wrote this letter around AD 54 to the church he started in Corinth (Acts 18:1-11).

**What was happening.** The church in Corinth was proud of its spiritual gifts. Some people thought their gifts, like speaking in tongues, made them more important than others. The church was competing instead of caring.†

**What this chapter is.** Paul says all gifts come from the same Spirit, for the good of everyone. Then he paints a picture: the church is a body, and every part matters.

**What comes next.** Chapter 13, the "love chapter," shows the most excellent way to use every gift.

## 🗺️ Map of the Letter

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Jesus Is Lord | 12:1-3 | Only the Spirit leads us to say it |
| 2. Different Gifts, Same Spirit | 12:4-11 | God gives gifts for the common good |
| 3. One Body, Many Parts | 12:12-20 | Every part belongs |
| 4. I Need You | 12:21-26 | The weaker parts matter most |
| 5. You Are the Body of Christ | 12:27-31 | Each of you is a member |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Jesus Is Lord

**"Now about spiritual gifts, brothers, I do not want you to be uninformed"** (12:1). {v: 12:1}

Before, many of them worshiped idols that couldn't speak. {v: 12:2}

Paul says no one can truly say **"Jesus is Lord"** except by the Holy Spirit (12:3). Faith itself is the Spirit's gift. {v: 12:3}

### Section 2: Different Gifts, Same Spirit

**"There are different gifts, but the same Spirit. There are different ministries, but the same Lord"** (12:4-5). {v: 12:4, 12:5, 12:6}

**"Now to each one the manifestation of the Spirit is given for the common good"** (12:7). {v: 12:7}

Manifestation means a way of showing. The common good means everyone, not just me. {v: 12:7}

Paul lists gifts: wisdom, knowledge, faith, healing, miracles, prophecy, telling spirits apart, tongues, and explaining tongues. {v: 12:8, 12:9, 12:10}

The Spirit decides who gets what. {v: 12:11}

### Section 3: One Body, Many Parts

**"The body is a unit, though it is composed of many parts. And although its parts are many, they all form one body. So it is with Christ"** (12:12). {v: 12:12}

**"For in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, slave or free"** (12:13). {v: 12:13}

Paul imagines a foot saying, "I'm not a hand, so I don't belong." That doesn't make it any less part of the body. {v: 12:14, 12:15, 12:16}

If the whole body were an eye, how would it hear? {v: 12:17}

**"But in fact, God has arranged the members of the body, every one of them, according to His design"** (12:18). {v: 12:18, 12:19, 12:20}

### Section 4: I Need You

The eye can't tell the hand, "I don't need you." {v: 12:21}

**"On the contrary, the parts of the body that seem to be weaker are indispensable"** (12:22). {v: 12:22}

Indispensable means you can't do without it. {v: 12:22}

God gives special honor to the parts that seem to lack it, so the body cares for itself. {v: 12:23, 12:24, 12:25}

**"If one part suffers, every part suffers with it; if one part is honored, every part rejoices with it"** (12:26). {v: 12:26}

### Section 5: You Are the Body of Christ

**"Now you are the body of Christ, and each of you is a member of it"** (12:27). {v: 12:27}

God gave the church apostles, prophets, teachers, helpers, leaders, and more. {v: 12:28}

Not everyone has the same gift. {v: 12:29, 12:30}

**"But eagerly desire the greater gifts. And now I will show you the most excellent way"** (12:31). {v: 12:31}

> 🧵 **Follow the thread**
> The "most excellent way" is love. Paul describes it in the next chapter, 1 Corinthians 13.

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

1 Corinthians 12 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Jesus is Lord"** (12:3) | Faith in Jesus is the Holy Spirit's work. |
| **"The same Spirit"** (12:4) | Every gift has the same giver. |
| **"For the common good"** (12:7) | Gifts are for serving others. |
| **"Baptized into one body"** (12:13) | Baptism makes us part of one church. |
| **"The parts of the body that seem to be weaker are indispensable"** (12:22) | The church needs the people it might overlook. |
| **"If one part suffers, every part suffers with it"** (12:26) | We share each other's pain and joy. |
| **"You are the body of Christ"** (12:27) | Together, the church is Jesus's body in the world. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Corinth's church** had rich and poor, Jews and Greeks, slaves and free people. They didn't naturally get along.†

**Comparing a group to a body** was a common picture in Greek and Roman speeches. Usually it told the lower classes to stay in their place. Paul flips it: the weaker parts get greater honor.†

**Speaking in tongues** means praying or praising in a language the speaker hasn't learned (see Acts 2:4).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Do I have to have a dramatic gift to be a real Christian?** No. Paul's whole point is that no gift makes anyone more important. Helping is on the list too (12:28).

**What if I can't do much?** The parts that seem weaker are the ones the body can't do without (12:22).

**Is "the body of Christ" just a metaphor?** It's a picture, and it's real. In baptism and the Lord's Supper, Christ joins his people to himself and to each other.

# Why It Matters

- Left out :: You belong. God placed you in the body on purpose.
- Not very gifted :: The parts that seem weaker are indispensable.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"I can be a Christian on my own. I don't need church."*

**What's true:** Faith is personal. Each person trusts Jesus for themselves.

**What the passage is mainly about:** **"Now you are the body of Christ, and each of you is a member of it"** (12:27). A hand on its own can't live. We need each other, and the church needs you.

**Where Jesus is:** The church is his body. He holds every part together.

## ✝️ Good News

You might feel like a foot among hands. You still belong.

**"Now you are the body of Christ, and each of you is a member of it"** (12:27).

The Spirit gave you gifts. God put you in the body on purpose, and the body needs you.

**There are many gifts but one Spirit. We are one body in Christ, and every part, especially the weakest, belongs and is needed.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught the "priesthood of all believers." Every baptized Christian has a calling from God, whether a pastor, a farmer, a parent, or a servant. No calling is more holy than another.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads 1 Corinthians 12:1-11 on the Second Sunday after the Epiphany and 12:12-31a on the Third. In Year A, 12:3b-13 is read on the Day of Pentecost.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**There are different gifts but the same Spirit. We are the body of Christ: many parts, one body, and every part belongs.**

# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**What it is.** A song of victory and thanks. Someone who was surrounded, pushed, and nearly killed has been rescued by God. Now he marches up to the temple gates to give thanks.

**Who wrote it.** It doesn't say. It was sung at Passover, and may be the hymn Jesus and his disciples sang after the Last Supper (Matthew 26:30).†

**When the church sings it.** On Palm Sunday and on Easter Day. The crowds on Palm Sunday shouted words from this psalm.

**How to read it.** Listen for the line that keeps repeating: "His loving devotion endures forever."

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: His Love Endures Forever

**"Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever"** (118:1). {v: 118:1}

Israel, the priests, and everyone who fears God are called to say it together. {v: 118:2, 118:3, 118:4}

### Section 2: The LORD Is on My Side

**"In my distress I called to the LORD, and He answered and set me free"** (118:5). {v: 118:5}

**"The LORD is on my side; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?"** (118:6). {v: 118:6, 118:7}

**"It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in man"** (118:8). {v: 118:8, 118:9}

Enemies surrounded him like a swarm of bees. {v: 118:10, 118:11, 118:12}

**"I was pushed so hard I was falling, but the LORD helped me"** (118:13). {v: 118:13}

**"The LORD is my strength and my song, and He has become my salvation"** (118:14). {v: 118:14}

### Section 3: I Will Not Die

Shouts of joy fill the tents of God's people. **"The right hand of the LORD performs with valor!"** (118:15). {v: 118:15, 118:16}

**"I will not die, but I will live and proclaim what the LORD has done"** (118:17). {v: 118:17, 118:18}

He comes to the temple gates. **"Open to me the gates of righteousness"** (118:19). {v: 118:19, 118:20, 118:21}

### Section 4: The Rejected Stone

**"The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone"** (118:22). {v: 118:22}

The stone that seemed useless became the most important one in the building. {v: 118:22}

**"This is from the LORD, and it is marvelous in our eyes"** (118:23). {v: 118:23}

**"This is the day that the LORD has made; we will rejoice and be glad in it"** (118:24). {v: 118:24}

**"O LORD, save us, we pray"** (118:25). In Hebrew, "save us" is *Hosanna*. {v: 118:25}

**"Blessed is he who comes in the name of the LORD"** (118:26). {v: 118:26, 118:27, 118:28}

**"Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever"** (118:29). {v: 118:29}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Psalm 118 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"His loving devotion endures forever"** (118:1) | God's faithful love never ends. |
| **"The LORD is on my side"** (118:6) | With God, I don't need to be afraid. |
| **"My strength and my song"** (118:14) | God gives power and joy. |
| **"I will not die, but I will live"** (118:17) | God rescued him from death. |
| **"The stone the builders rejected"** (118:22) | The one people threw away, God made most important. |
| **"This is the day that the LORD has made"** (118:24) | A day of God's rescue, worth celebrating. |
| **"Blessed is he who comes in the name of the LORD"** (118:26) | A welcome for God's chosen one. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Pilgrims sang this psalm** as they came up to the temple for the great festivals.†

**A cornerstone** was the first and most important stone in a building. Everything else lined up with it.

**On Palm Sunday,** the crowds shouted, "Hosanna! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!" (Mark 11:9).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Who is the rejected stone?** Jesus said it was him (Matthew 21:42). Peter said so too (Acts 4:11).

**Is "this is the day" about every day?** It's often used that way. In the psalm, it means the day of God's great rescue. Christians sing it on Easter, the day of Jesus's resurrection.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God only uses impressive people."*

**What's true:** God gives gifts and strength.

**What the passage is mainly about:** **"The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone"** (118:22). God loves to choose what others throw away.

**Where Jesus is:** He is the rejected stone, crucified and raised. Easter is the day the LORD has made.

## ✝️ Good News

What people threw away, God raised up.

**"The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone. This is from the LORD, and it is marvelous in our eyes"** (118:22-23).

Jesus was rejected and killed, and God raised him. His love endures forever.

**Give thanks to the LORD, for his love endures forever. The rejected stone has become the cornerstone. This is the day the LORD has made!**

## 📚 From Luther

Psalm 118 was Luther's favorite psalm. He wrote verse 17 on the wall of his room during a hard time: "I will not die, but I will live and proclaim what the LORD has done."†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It sings Psalm 118 on Palm Sunday and Easter Day in all three years, and on the Second Sunday of Easter in Year C.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Give thanks to the LORD, for his love endures forever. The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone. This is the day the LORD has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it.**

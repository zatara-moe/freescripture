# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God.
- **A widow:** A mother whose husband has already died. Now her only son has died too.
- **Her son:** A young man being carried out to be buried.
- **Two crowds:** One following Jesus, one walking with the grieving mother.

**Setting**

- The gate of Nain, a small town in Galilee, south of Nazareth.†

**When**

- About 2,000 years ago, during Jesus's work in Galilee.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus had just healed a Roman officer's servant.** He did it without even going to the house (7:1-10).

**A widow was in a hard place.** Without a husband or son, a woman often had no one to provide for her or protect her.†

**Here's what you know:** In Nazareth, Jesus reminded people how the prophet Elijah helped a widow long ago (4:25-26). Elijah once raised a widow's son from the dead (1 Kings 17:17-24).

## 🗺️ Map of the Story

| Scene | Verses | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Two crowds meet | 7:11-12 | Jesus meets a funeral at the town gate |
| 2. "Do not weep" | 7:13 | Jesus has compassion on the mother |
| 3. "Get up!" | 7:14-15 | Jesus raises her son |
| 4. "God has visited His people!" | 7:16-17 | Everyone praises God |

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Two Crowds Meet

Jesus went to a town called Nain. His disciples and a large crowd went with him. {v: 7:11}

As he came near the town gate, a funeral was coming out. {v: 7:12}

A young man had died. He was his mother's only son, and she was a widow. {v: 7:12}

A large crowd from the town was walking with her. {v: 7:12}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture two crowds on one narrow road. One is full of people excited to see Jesus. The other is full of weeping. In the middle walks a mother who has lost everyone.

### Scene 2: "Do Not Weep"

When the Lord saw her, he had compassion on her. Compassion means feeling someone's pain and wanting to help. {v: 7:13}

**"Do not weep,"** he said (7:13). {v: 7:13}

No one asked Jesus for help. He saw her and came to her. {v: 7:13}

### Scene 3: "Get Up!"

Jesus went up and touched the open coffin. The men carrying it stopped. {v: 7:14}

**"Young man,"** he said, **"I tell you, get up!"** (7:14). {v: 7:14}

The dead man sat up and began to talk! {v: 7:15}

**"Then Jesus gave him back to his mother"** (7:15). {v: 7:15}

### Scene 4: "God Has Visited His People!"

Everyone was filled with awe. They praised God. {v: 7:16}

**"A great prophet has appeared among us!"** they said. **"God has visited His people!"** (7:16). {v: 7:16}

The news about Jesus spread everywhere. {v: 7:17}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Luke 7:11-17 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"The only son of his mother, and she was a widow"** (7:12) | She had lost everyone, and was alone. |
| **"He had compassion on her"** (7:13) | Jesus felt her grief and acted. |
| **"Do not weep"** (7:13) | Jesus was about to end the reason for her tears. |
| **"Touched the coffin"** (7:14) | Jesus wasn't afraid to come close to death. |
| **"Gave him back to his mother"** (7:15) | Jesus cared about the mother as much as the son. |
| **"God has visited His people!"** (7:16) | God himself had come to help them, in Jesus. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Burials happened quickly.** A person was usually buried the same day they died.†

**The coffin was open.** It was a stretcher or open box, carried on people's shoulders.†

**Touching a dead body made a person "unclean" under God's law** (Numbers 19:11). Jesus touched the coffin anyway. Instead of death making Jesus unclean, Jesus made the dead man alive.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does Jesus not want us to cry?** Jesus himself wept when his friend Lazarus died (John 11:35). Crying is okay. Jesus told this mother not to weep because he was about to give her son back.

**Will Jesus bring back everyone who dies right away?** Not right away. Jesus raised a few people during his life, as signs. He promises that everyone who trusts him will rise on the last day.†

**Did the young man die again later?** Yes. Like Lazarus, he lived a normal life and later died. Jesus's own resurrection is different. He rose never to die again.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The widow | Grieving | Her only son had died (7:12) |
| Jesus | Compassionate | He saw her and felt her pain (7:13) |
| The crowds | Amazed | A dead man sat up and talked (7:16) |
| The crowds | Thankful | They praised God (7:16) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God is far away and doesn't notice when people are hurting."*

**What's true:** Sometimes it can feel like God is far away.

**What the story is mainly about:** No one asked Jesus to help. He saw a grieving mother, felt compassion, and came to her. The crowd said, **"God has visited His people!"** (7:16).

**Where Jesus is:** At the gate of a small town, stopping a funeral, giving a son back to his mother.

## ✝️ Good News

Death had taken everything from this mother. Then Jesus came.

**"Young man,"** he said, **"I tell you, get up!"** (7:14).

Jesus is stronger than death. He sees people who grieve. One day he will say "Get up!" to everyone who sleeps in him.

**Jesus sees people who grieve, and he is stronger than death. God has visited his people.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther often spoke of death as a sleep for Christians. He taught that Christ will wake the dead as easily as we wake someone from sleep.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads Luke 7:11-17 in early June (Lectionary 10).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Jesus saw a grieving widow, felt compassion, and raised her only son. He is stronger than death.**

# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**What Proverbs is.** A book of wise sayings, mostly connected with King Solomon (Proverbs 1:1). Many are short. But chapters 1 to 9 are longer poems.

**Who "Wisdom" is.** In these chapters, wisdom is pictured as a woman who calls out to everyone. This is a way of making wisdom come alive. Wisdom means knowing how to live well, in God's way.

**What this passage is.** Three pieces: Wisdom calls to everyone (8:1-4), Wisdom was there when God made the world (8:22-31), and Wisdom throws a feast (9:1-6).

**How to read it.** Christians have long heard Jesus in these words.

## 🗺️ Map of the Message

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Wisdom Calls Out | 8:1-4 | Wisdom invites everyone |
| 2. Before the World Began | 8:22-26 | Wisdom was there first |
| 3. At God's Side | 8:27-31 | Wisdom delighted in creation |
| 4. Wisdom's Feast | 9:1-6 | Wisdom invites people to her table |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Wisdom Calls Out

**"Does not wisdom call out, and understanding raise her voice?"** (8:1). {v: 8:1}

She stands where everyone passes: on the hills, at the crossroads, by the city gates. {v: 8:2, 8:3}

**"To you, O men, I call out, and my cry is to the sons of men"** (8:4). {v: 8:4}

Wisdom isn't hidden. She calls to everyone. {v: 8:4}

### Section 2: Before the World Began

Wisdom says, **"The LORD created me as His first course, before His works of old"** (8:22). {v: 8:22}

Before the oceans, before springs of water, before the mountains, Wisdom was there. {v: 8:24, 8:25, 8:26}

### Section 3: At God's Side

**"I was there when He established the heavens"** (8:27). {v: 8:27}

When God set the clouds in place and gave the sea its boundaries, Wisdom was beside him. {v: 8:28, 8:29}

**"Then I was a skilled craftsman at His side, and His delight day by day, rejoicing always in His presence"** (8:30). {v: 8:30}

Wisdom delighted in the world, and in people. {v: 8:31}

> 🧵 **Follow the thread**
> John wrote about Jesus: **"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God... Through Him all things were made"** (John 1:1-3).

### Section 4: Wisdom's Feast

**"Wisdom has built her house; she has carved out her seven pillars"** (9:1). {v: 9:1}

She has prepared meat and wine and set the table. {v: 9:2}

She sends out her servants to call: **"Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!"** (9:4). Simple means inexperienced, easily led astray. {v: 9:3, 9:4}

**"Come, eat my bread and drink the wine I have mixed"** (9:5). {v: 9:5}

**"Leave your folly behind, and you will live; walk in the way of understanding"** (9:6). Folly means foolishness. {v: 9:6}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Proverbs 8:1-4, 8:22-31, and 9:1-6 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Does not wisdom call out"** (8:1) | God's wisdom is offered to everyone. |
| **"At the crossroads she takes her stand"** (8:2) | Wisdom meets us where we make choices. |
| **"I was there when He established the heavens"** (8:27) | God made the world wisely. |
| **"His delight day by day"** (8:30) | Creation was full of joy. |
| **"Delighting together in the sons of men"** (8:31) | God delights in people. |
| **"Come, eat my bread"** (9:5) | Wisdom invites us to a feast, not a test. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Proverbs was used to teach young people.** It's like a parent's advice to a son or daughter (1:8).†

**Seven pillars** suggests a complete, perfect house.†

**There is also "Folly,"** another woman in Proverbs 9 who calls out to foolish people. The two invitations are compared (9:13-18).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Is Wisdom a real woman?** No. This is poetry. Wisdom is pictured as a person to help us hear her call.

**Was Wisdom "created"?** Early Christians debated this verse a lot. Most read it as a picture of God's wisdom being present from before creation. The church confesses that the Son, God's eternal Word, was never created.†

**Is this about Jesus?** The New Testament calls Christ **"the wisdom of God"** (1 Corinthians 1:24). Christians see Wisdom's call fulfilled in him.

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Wisdom | Eager | She calls out to everyone (8:4) |
| Wisdom | Delighted | She rejoiced in creation and in people (8:30-31) |
| The simple | Invited | Wisdom calls them to her feast (9:4) |

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**Where do you need wisdom?** Tap one.

- A choice :: Wisdom stands at the crossroads. Ask God for help.
- Friends :: Proverbs says walking with the wise makes you wise.
- I feel foolish :: Wisdom invites the "simple." You're welcome.
- Wonder :: God made the world with wisdom and delight.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Wisdom is only for smart or old people."*

**What's true:** Experience can teach wisdom.

**What the passage is mainly about:** Wisdom calls out to everyone, especially the inexperienced. She invites them to a feast. God's wisdom is a gift, offered freely.

**Where Jesus is:** Jesus is God's wisdom, present at creation. He invites us to his table: "Come, eat my bread."

## ✝️ Good News

Wisdom doesn't hide. She stands at the crossroads and calls out.

**"Come, eat my bread and drink the wine I have mixed"** (9:5).

Jesus, the wisdom of God, invites everyone to his table. In the Lord's Supper, he gives himself.

**Wisdom calls out to everyone. She was with God at creation, delighting in the world, and she invites us to her feast.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that true wisdom starts with knowing God through Christ. **"The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom"** (Proverbs 9:10).†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads Proverbs 8:1-4 and 22-31 on Holy Trinity Sunday. In Year B, it reads Proverbs 9:1-6 in August (Lectionary 20), next to Jesus saying, "I am the living bread."†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Wisdom calls out to everyone. She was with God when he made the world, delighting in it, and she invites us: "Come, eat my bread."**

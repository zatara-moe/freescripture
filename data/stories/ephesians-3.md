# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** Paul, writing from prison. He calls himself **"the prisoner of Christ Jesus for the sake of you Gentiles"** (3:1).

**What came before.** In chapter 2, Paul said Christ tore down the wall between Jews and Gentiles and made them one household (2:14, 2:19).

**What this chapter is.** Paul explains the "mystery" God has now revealed. Then he prays one of the most beautiful prayers in the Bible.

**A word to know.** A "mystery" in the Bible isn't a puzzle. It's something God kept hidden for a long time and has now made known.

## 🗺️ Map of the Letter

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Mystery Revealed | 3:1-6 | Gentiles share fully in God's promise |
| 2. Less Than the Least | 3:7-13 | Paul's job is to preach Christ's riches |
| 3. Paul's Prayer | 3:14-19 | That they may know Christ's love |
| 4. Immeasurably More | 3:20-21 | Glory to God, who can do more than we imagine |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: The Mystery Revealed

Paul is in prison because he preached to Gentiles, people who aren't Jewish. {v: 3:1}

God showed him a mystery, hidden for generations. {v: 3:3, 3:4, 3:5}

**"This mystery is that through the gospel the Gentiles are fellow heirs, fellow members of the body, and fellow partakers of the promise in Christ Jesus"** (3:6). {v: 3:6}

Everyone who trusts Jesus shares fully in God's family and his promises. {v: 3:6}

### Section 2: Less Than the Least

Paul became a servant of this good news by God's grace. {v: 3:7}

**"Though I am less than the least of all the saints, this grace was given me: to preach to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ"** (3:8). {v: 3:8}

Unsearchable means too great to measure. {v: 3:8}

Through the church, God shows his wisdom even to the powers in heaven. {v: 3:9, 3:10, 3:11}

**"In Him and through faith in Him we may enter God's presence with boldness and confidence"** (3:12). {v: 3:12}

### Section 3: Paul's Prayer

**"For this reason I bow my knees before the Father"** (3:14). {v: 3:14, 3:15}

Paul prays that God will strengthen them by his Spirit, **"so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith"** (3:17). {v: 3:16, 3:17}

He prays that they will be **"rooted and grounded in love"** (3:17), like a tree with deep roots. {v: 3:17}

He prays they will understand **"the length and width and height and depth of the love of Christ"** (3:18-19). {v: 3:18, 3:19}

And to **"know this love that surpasses knowledge"** (3:19). Christ's love is bigger than anyone can fully understand. {v: 3:19}

### Section 4: Immeasurably More

Paul ends with praise. {v: 3:20}

**"Now to Him who is able to do immeasurably more than all we ask or imagine, according to His power that is at work within us"** (3:20). {v: 3:20}

**"To Him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus throughout all generations, forever and ever. Amen"** (3:21). {v: 3:21}

> 🧵 **Follow the thread**
> Many churches close worship with Ephesians 3:20-21 as a blessing.

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Ephesians 3 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"This mystery"** (3:6) | God's plan, now revealed: everyone is welcome in Christ. |
| **"Fellow heirs"** (3:6) | Gentiles share everything God promised. |
| **"Less than the least"** (3:8) | Paul knew he didn't deserve this calling. |
| **"Boldness and confidence"** (3:12) | We can come to God without fear. |
| **"Rooted and grounded in love"** (3:17) | God's love is the soil we grow in. |
| **"Love that surpasses knowledge"** (3:19) | Christ's love is bigger than we can understand. |
| **"Immeasurably more"** (3:20) | God can do more than we can ask or imagine. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Most of the earliest Christians were Jewish.** Including Gentiles as full members was a big change. Some believers struggled with it (Acts 11:2-3, 15:1-2).

**Paul was arrested in Jerusalem** after being accused of bringing Gentiles into the temple (Acts 21:28).

**Bowing the knees** to pray was unusual. Most people stood to pray. Kneeling showed deep humility.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does God do whatever I ask?** Paul says God can do more than we ask. That doesn't mean he gives us everything we want. It means his power and love are bigger than our prayers.

**What are the "rulers and authorities in the heavenly realms"?** Spiritual powers. God's plan shows even them his wisdom.†

**Can love be measured?** Paul uses the words length, width, height, and depth to say Christ's love has no limits in any direction.

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**What part of Paul's prayer do you need?** Tap one.

- Strength inside :: Paul prays God will strengthen you with power through his Spirit.
- Deep roots :: Rooted and grounded in love, you can stand.
- How much God loves me :: It's wider, longer, higher, and deeper than you know.
- Big things :: God can do immeasurably more than you ask or imagine.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God's love is for good church people."*

**What's true:** God's love gathers people into the church.

**What the passage is mainly about:** God's mystery is that outsiders are fellow heirs. Christ's love has no limits. Paul called himself less than the least, and still God's grace was for him.

**Where Jesus is:** Dwelling in our hearts through faith, his love beyond measuring.

## ✝️ Good News

Paul prayed for something bigger than anyone could understand.

**"To know this love that surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled with all the fullness of God"** (3:19).

Christ's love has no limits. It reaches every person, and it reaches you.

**God's mystery is revealed: everyone in Christ is a fellow heir. Paul prays we will know Christ's love, which is wider and deeper than we can imagine.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that Christ dwells in the hearts of believers by faith. He said faith grabs hold of Christ and makes him present, so that Christ and the believer belong to each other.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It reads Ephesians 3:1-12 on the Epiphany of Our Lord in all three years. In Year B, it reads Ephesians 3:14-21 in late July (Lectionary 17).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**God's mystery is revealed: all people are welcome in Christ. Paul prays that we will know Christ's love, which surpasses knowledge, and praises God, who can do immeasurably more than we ask.**

# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God.
- **A man with demons:** A man from the town, controlled by many evil spirits. He lives among the tombs.
- **The disciples:** Jesus's closest followers, who crossed the lake with him.
- **The people of the region:** Mostly not Jewish. Some of them keep pigs.

**Setting**

- The region of the Gerasenes, on the east side of the Sea of Galilee.

**When**

- About 2,000 years ago, during Jesus's work in Galilee.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus had just calmed a storm.** The disciples asked, **"Who is this? He commands even the winds and the water, and they obey Him!"** (8:25).

**They landed across the lake.** It was an area where many people were not Jewish.†

**Here's what you know:** The wind and waves obeyed Jesus. Now he will meet something even more frightening than a storm.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: A Man Among the Tombs

Jesus and his disciples sailed across the lake. When Jesus stepped onto the shore, a man from the town came to meet him. {v: 8:26, 8:27}

The man had demons. Demons are evil spirits. {v: 8:27}

For a long time he had worn no clothes and had not lived in a house. He lived among the tombs, where the dead were buried. {v: 8:27}

People had tried to chain him, but he broke the chains. {v: 8:29}

When he saw Jesus, he fell down and shouted, **"What do You want with me, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg You not to torture me!"** (8:28). {v: 8:28}

The demons knew who Jesus was. {v: 8:28}

### Scene 2: "Legion"

**"What is your name?"** Jesus asked (8:30). {v: 8:30}

**"Legion,"** he replied (8:30). A legion was a group of thousands of Roman soldiers. Many demons had gone into him. {v: 8:30}

The demons begged Jesus not to send them into the Abyss, a place of punishment. {v: 8:31}

A large herd of pigs was feeding on the hillside. The demons begged to go into the pigs. Jesus let them. {v: 8:32}

The demons came out of the man and went into the pigs. The whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and drowned. {v: 8:33}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture the hillside suddenly full of noise and dust as hundreds of pigs stampede toward the water. Then silence. And a man sitting quietly on the ground.

### Scene 3: Clothed and in His Right Mind

The men tending the pigs ran to the town and told everyone. {v: 8:34}

People came out to see. They found the man sitting at Jesus's feet, dressed, and in his right mind. {v: 8:35}

**"And they were afraid"** (8:35). {v: 8:35}

They asked Jesus to leave. So Jesus got back into the boat. {v: 8:37}

### Scene 4: "Return Home"

The man begged to go with Jesus. {v: 8:38}

But Jesus sent him home. **"Return home and describe how much God has done for you"** (8:39). {v: 8:39}

So the man went all over the town telling how much Jesus had done for him. {v: 8:39}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Luke 8:26-39 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"He stayed in the tombs"** (8:27) | He was cut off from everyone, living among the dead. |
| **"Son of the Most High God"** (8:28) | The demons knew who Jesus was, and were afraid. |
| **"Legion"** (8:30) | He was controlled by many evil spirits. |
| **"Sitting at Jesus' feet, clothed and in his right mind"** (8:35) | Jesus gave the man his life back. |
| **"They were afraid"** (8:35) | Jesus's power scared the townspeople more than the man had. |
| **"Describe how much God has done for you"** (8:39) | Jesus made the man the first messenger to his people. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Jewish people didn't keep pigs.** God's law called pigs unclean (Leviticus 11:7). A pig farm tells us this was mostly non-Jewish land.†

**Tombs were often caves cut into hillsides.** Someone could take shelter in one.†

**Mark and Matthew tell this story too** (Mark 5:1-20, Matthew 8:28-34). Mark says the man went through a whole region of ten towns telling his story.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Is mental illness the same as having a demon?** No. Mental illness is a sickness, like other sicknesses. Doctors and counselors can help, and getting help is good. The Bible describes evil spirits as something different.†

**Why did Jesus let the pigs die?** The Bible doesn't fully say. The pigs showed everyone that the demons were really gone. And it showed how much one man was worth to Jesus.†

**Why didn't Jesus let the man come with him?** Jesus gave him a job. He was sent home to tell his own people. Jesus doesn't just rescue people. He gives them a purpose.

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The man | Tormented | He lived alone among the tombs (8:27) |
| The demons | Terrified | They begged Jesus not to send them away (8:31) |
| The townspeople | Afraid | They saw the man healed (8:35) |
| The man | Grateful | He begged to go with Jesus (8:38) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Some people are too far gone to be helped."*

**What's true:** Some people are in very dark places.

**What the story is mainly about:** This man was cut off from everyone. No chains could hold him. Jesus crossed a lake to reach him, set him free, and sent him home with a story to tell.

**Where Jesus is:** On a lonely shore, among the tombs, giving a man his life back.

## ✝️ Good News

No one could help this man. Jesus could.

**"Return home and describe how much God has done for you"** (8:39).

Jesus is stronger than every evil power. He seeks out people who are alone and in darkness. He sets them free.

**Jesus crossed a lake to set one man free. He is stronger than every evil, and he gives people their lives back.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther's hymn "A Mighty Fortress" says the devil is strong, but "one little word can fell him." Luther taught that Christ has already won the battle.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads Luke 8:26-39 in June (Lectionary 12).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Jesus set a man free from many evil spirits, gave him his life back, and sent him home to tell how much God had done for him.**

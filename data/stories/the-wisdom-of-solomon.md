# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Solomon:** King David's son. He has just become king of Israel. He is young.
- **The LORD:** God. LORD in capital letters stands for God's personal name. Jewish readers traditionally don't say it aloud, out of respect.
- **Two women:** Two mothers who live in the same house. Each says a baby is hers.

**Setting**

- Gibeon, a place of worship near Jerusalem. Then the king's court in Jerusalem.

**When**

- About 3,000 years ago, at the start of Solomon's reign.†

## 🧭 Before the Story

**King David had died.** Before he died, he made his son Solomon king (1:39, 2:10-12).

**David told Solomon to follow God.** **"Keep the charge of the LORD your God to walk in His ways"** (2:3).

**Solomon was young, and the job was huge.** He was to rule a whole nation.

## 🗺️ Map of the Story

| Scene | Verses | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. "Ask!" | 3:3-9 | God tells Solomon to ask for anything |
| 2. More than he asked | 3:10-15 | God gives Solomon wisdom and more |
| 3. Two mothers | 3:16-22 | A hard case comes to the king |
| 4. "Bring me a sword" | 3:23-28 | Solomon finds the real mother |

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: "Ask!"

Solomon loved the LORD. He went to Gibeon to worship. {v: 3:3, 3:4}

One night, the LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream. God said, **"Ask, and I will give it to you!"** (3:5). {v: 3:5}

Anything at all. {v: 3:5}

Solomon thanked God for his kindness to his father David. {v: 3:6}

**"But I am only a little child, not knowing how to go out or come in"** (3:7). He meant he didn't know how to lead. {v: 3:7}

**"Therefore give Your servant an understanding heart to judge Your people and to discern between good and evil"** (3:9). To discern means to see clearly and tell things apart. {v: 3:9}

### Scene 2: More Than He Asked

God was pleased with Solomon's request. {v: 3:10}

Solomon didn't ask for a long life, or riches, or for his enemies to die. He asked for wisdom to do what is right. {v: 3:11}

So God said, **"I will give you a wise and discerning heart"** (3:12). {v: 3:12}

**"Moreover, I will give you what you did not request—both riches and honor"** (3:13). {v: 3:13}

Then Solomon woke up. It had been a dream. He went to Jerusalem and worshiped. {v: 3:15}

### Scene 3: Two Mothers

Soon after, two women came to the king. {v: 3:16}

One woman said they lived in the same house. Each of them had a baby, a few days apart. {v: 3:17, 3:18}

One night, the other woman's baby died. {v: 3:19}

**"So she got up in the middle of the night and took my son from my side while I was asleep"** (3:20). She put her dead baby next to me, the first woman said. {v: 3:20}

In the morning, she saw the dead baby wasn't hers. {v: 3:21}

**"No,"** said the other woman, **"the living one is my son and the dead one is your son"** (3:22). {v: 3:22}

They argued in front of the king. There were no witnesses. How could anyone know the truth? {v: 3:22}

### Scene 4: "Bring Me a Sword"

The king said, **"Bring me a sword"** (3:24). {v: 3:24}

Then he said, "Cut the living child in two. Give half to each woman." {v: 3:25}

The real mother cried out. She loved her baby. **"Please, my lord,"** she said, **"give her the living baby. Do not kill him!"** (3:26). {v: 3:26}

But the other woman said, "Go ahead. Then neither of us will have him." {v: 3:26}

Now Solomon knew. **"Give the living baby to the first woman. By no means should you kill him; she is his mother"** (3:27). {v: 3:27}

Solomon never meant to hurt the baby. The sword was a test to show who truly loved him.† {v: 3:27}

All Israel heard about it. They saw that God had given Solomon wisdom to do justice. {v: 3:28}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture the mother sobbing, holding out her arms. The soldier lowers the sword. Solomon nods, and the baby is placed in her arms.

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

1 Kings 3:3-28 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Ask, and I will give it to you!"** (3:5) | God offered Solomon anything. |
| **"I am only a little child"** (3:7) | Solomon was humble. He knew he needed help. |
| **"An understanding heart"** (3:9) | Wisdom starts in the heart, not just the head. |
| **"What you did not request"** (3:13) | God gave far more than Solomon asked. |
| **"She yearned with compassion for her son"** (3:26) | Real love would give up anything to protect the child. |
| **"The wisdom of God was in him"** (3:28) | Solomon's wisdom was God's gift. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Kings were judges.** People could bring hard cases directly to the king.†

**Solomon became famous for wisdom.** Much of the book of Proverbs is credited to him (Proverbs 1:1).

**The Queen of Sheba came to test him.** She traveled a long way and was amazed by his wisdom (10:1-7).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Was Solomon really going to cut the baby?** No. It was a test to find out who loved the baby. The baby was never hurt.

**Did Solomon stay wise?** Not always. Later he turned to other gods (11:4). Even great gifts don't keep us from sin. That's why we need God's grace.

**Does God give whatever we ask?** God invites us to ask. Solomon asked for what would help others. God loves to give good gifts.

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Solomon | Humble | He felt like a little child (3:7) |
| The first mother | Desperate | Her baby had been taken (3:20-21) |
| The first mother | Terrified | The king called for a sword (3:26) |
| The people | Amazed | They saw God's wisdom in Solomon (3:28) |

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**If God said, "Ask," what would you ask for?** Tap one.

- Wisdom :: Like Solomon. God loves to give it (James 1:5).
- Help for someone :: Solomon asked for wisdom to help others.
- I don't know :: Tell God that. He knows what you need.
- Something big :: God gave Solomon more than he asked. You can ask him.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Being smart is the same as being wise."*

**What's true:** Knowing things is good. Solomon knew a lot.

**What the story is mainly about:** Solomon's wisdom started with humility: "I am only a little child." He asked for an understanding heart. Wisdom is a gift from God, used to love and protect people.

**Where Jesus is:** Jesus said, **"One greater than Solomon is here"** (Matthew 12:42). He is the wisdom of God. He didn't only discover who loved the child. He gave his own life for God's children.

## ✝️ Good News

Solomon asked for wisdom. God gave it, and more.

**"I will give you a wise and discerning heart"** (3:12).

God loves to give good gifts to his children. He gave us Jesus, who is God's wisdom, and who gave himself for us.

**God gave young Solomon wisdom to do justice. Jesus is greater than Solomon, and he gives himself for us.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that rulers need wisdom from God more than anything. He praised Solomon's prayer and said every leader should pray it.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year A, it reads 1 Kings 3:5-12 in late July (Lectionary 17), next to Jesus's parables of the treasure and the pearl.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Solomon asked God for an understanding heart, and God gave him wisdom. Jesus, God's wisdom, is greater than Solomon.**

# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Where these words come from.** The later chapters of Isaiah, spoken to God's people during and after their exile in Babylon, in the 500s BC.†

**What was happening.** The people had lost everything: their temple, their city, their land. Some had come home to ruins. They wondered if God was still theirs. Some outsiders who had joined God's people wondered if they would be welcome.

**What this passage is.** Six short messages from Isaiah 44 to 66. Together they say: there is no God but the LORD, he uses even foreign kings for his plan, and his house is for everyone.

**How to read it.** Notice who God welcomes: foreigners, outsiders, people who never asked for him.

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: There Is No Other Rock

**"I am the first and I am the last, and there is no God but Me"** (44:6). {v: 44:6, 44:7}

**"Do not tremble or fear"** (44:8). {v: 44:8}

**"There is no other Rock; I know not one"** (44:8). {v: 44:8}

### Section 2: Cyrus, God's Tool

Cyrus was the king of Persia. He didn't worship the LORD. {v: 45:1}

Yet God calls him **"His anointed"** (45:1), chosen for a job: to conquer Babylon and let God's people go home. {v: 45:1, 45:2, 45:3}

**"For the sake of Jacob My servant and Israel My chosen one, I call you by name"** (45:4). {v: 45:4}

**"I am the LORD, and there is no other; there is no God but Me"** (45:5). {v: 45:5, 45:6}

God is in charge of all of history, the light and the dark. {v: 45:7}

### Section 3: Look to the Rock

**"Look to the rock from which you were cut, and to the quarry from which you were hewn"** (51:1). {v: 51:1}

Remember Abraham and Sarah. When God called Abraham, he was just one man. God made him a great people. {v: 51:2}

**"For the LORD will comfort Zion and will look with compassion on all her ruins; He will make her wilderness like Eden"** (51:3). {v: 51:3}

God's justice will be a light to the nations. {v: 51:4, 51:5}

The sky and earth will wear out like old clothes. **"But My salvation will last forever, and My righteousness will never fail"** (51:6). {v: 51:6}

### Section 4: A House of Prayer

**"Maintain justice and do what is right, for My salvation is coming soon"** (56:1). {v: 56:1, 56:2}

Some foreigners who followed God feared they would be shut out. Some men who could never have children felt like dry, dead trees. {v: 56:3}

God says to them: you will have a place in my house, and a name better than sons and daughters. {v: 56:4, 56:5}

God will bring foreigners who love him to his holy mountain. {v: 56:6, 56:7}

**"For My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations"** (56:7). {v: 56:7}

**"I will gather to them still others besides those already gathered"** (56:8). {v: 56:8}

> 🧵 **Follow the thread**
> Jesus quoted this verse when he cleared the money changers out of the temple (Mark 11:17).

### Section 5: Here I Am

**"I revealed Myself to those who did not ask for Me; I was found by those who did not seek Me"** (65:1). {v: 65:1}

To people who never called his name, God said, "Here I am! Here I am!" {v: 65:1}

**"All day long I have held out My hands to an obstinate people"** (65:2). {v: 65:2}

Some people thought they were holier than others. God says their pride is like smoke in his nose. {v: 65:3, 65:4, 65:5, 65:6, 65:7}

But God won't destroy everyone. Like a farmer who saves a cluster with good grapes, he will save his servants. {v: 65:8, 65:9}

### Section 6: As a Mother Comforts

**"Be glad for Jerusalem and rejoice over her, all who love her"** (66:10). {v: 66:10, 66:11}

God will give peace like a river. {v: 66:12}

His people will be carried and bounced on her knees, like a happy child. {v: 66:12}

**"As a mother comforts her son, so will I comfort you"** (66:13). {v: 66:13}

**"When you see, you will rejoice, and you will flourish like grass"** (66:14). {v: 66:14}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Isaiah 44:6-8; 45:1-7; 51:1-6; 56:1-8; 65:1-9; 66:10-14 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"I am the first and I am the last"** (44:6) | God was before everything and will be after everything. |
| **"There is no other Rock"** (44:8) | Only God is solid enough to trust. |
| **"His anointed"** (45:1) | God chose Cyrus for a special job. |
| **"Look to the rock from which you were cut"** (51:1) | Remember where you came from and what God did. |
| **"A house of prayer for all the nations"** (56:7) | God welcomes people from everywhere. |
| **"I was found by those who did not seek Me"** (65:1) | God reaches out first. |
| **"As a mother comforts her son"** (66:13) | God's love is tender and close. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Cyrus the Great** conquered Babylon in 539 BC and let the exiles return home and rebuild the temple (Ezra 1:1-4).

**Eunuchs** were men who had been castrated, often to serve in royal courts. They couldn't have children and weren't allowed into parts of the temple (Deuteronomy 23:1). Isaiah 56 promises them a place.

**Paul quotes Isaiah 65:1** to show that God reached out to the Gentiles (Romans 10:20).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does God cause evil?** When God says he forms light and darkness, prosperity and calamity, he means nothing is outside his control, even hard times. It doesn't mean he does evil.

**Is God a mother?** The Bible calls God Father, and also uses mother pictures to show his tender care (Isaiah 49:15, Matthew 23:37).

**Why would God use a king who didn't know him?** God can work through anyone to carry out his plans, even people who don't believe in him.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Church is for people who already fit in."*

**What's true:** God gathers his people together.

**What the passage is mainly about:** God promised foreigners and outsiders a place in his house, and a name better than sons and daughters. **"My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations"** (56:7).

**Where Jesus is:** He quoted these words, welcomed outsiders, and gathered "still others" into God's family.

## ✝️ Good News

God doesn't wait for you to find him.

**"I revealed Myself to those who did not ask for Me; I was found by those who did not seek Me"** (65:1).

He welcomes outsiders, comforts like a mother, and his salvation lasts forever.

**There is no God but the LORD. He rules history, reaches out to people who weren't looking for him, and makes his house a house of prayer for all nations.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that God comes to us before we come to him. In the Small Catechism, he says the Holy Spirit calls us by the gospel, because we cannot come to Christ by our own strength.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It reads these passages on Sundays in the season after Pentecost: Isaiah 56:1, 6-8 and 51:1-6 in Year A (Lectionary 20 and 21), 45:1-7 in Year A (Lectionary 29), and 65:1-9 and 66:10-14 in Year C (Lectionary 12 and 14).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**There is no God but the LORD, and there is no other Rock. He reaches out to people who never looked for him, comforts like a mother, and makes his house a house of prayer for all nations.**

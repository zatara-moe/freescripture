# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** Micah was a prophet from a small country town called Moresheth, about 740 to 700 BC, the same time as Isaiah (Micah 1:1).

**What was happening.** Micah spoke against leaders who cheated the poor. He warned that Jerusalem would fall. Enemies were coming (5:1).†

**What this passage is.** A promise. God's King won't come from the big capital city. He will come from a little town called Bethlehem.

**Why it matters.** When the wise men asked where the Messiah would be born, the priests and scribes quoted these words (Matthew 2:5-6).

## 🗺️ Map of the Message

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Small Bethlehem | 5:2 | A ruler will come from a small town |
| 2. Until She Gives Birth | 5:3 | God's people will wait for a birth |
| 3. He Will Shepherd | 5:4 | The ruler will care for his flock |
| 4. He Will Be Our Peace | 5:5 | He will protect his people |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Small Bethlehem

**"But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, who are small among the clans of Judah, out of you will come forth for Me One to be ruler over Israel"** (5:2). {v: 5:2}

Bethlehem was a little village. Ephrathah was the name of its area. King David was born there, long before. {v: 5:2}

And this ruler is no ordinary king. His **"origins are of old, from the days of eternity"** (5:2). {v: 5:2}

### Section 2: Until She Gives Birth

God's people would be abandoned for a time, **"until she who is in labor has given birth"** (5:3). {v: 5:3}

Then God's scattered people would come home. {v: 5:3}

### Section 3: He Will Shepherd

**"He will stand and shepherd His flock in the strength of the LORD"** (5:4). {v: 5:4}

King David was a shepherd before he was king. This King will be a shepherd too, caring for God's people. {v: 5:4}

His people will live safely, and **"His greatness will extend to the ends of the earth"** (5:4). {v: 5:4}

> 🧵 **Follow the thread**
> On the night Jesus was born in Bethlehem, the first people to hear the news were shepherds in the fields (Luke 2:8-11).

### Section 4: He Will Be Our Peace

**"And He will be our peace"** (5:5). {v: 5:5}

Not just someone who brings peace. He himself *is* peace. {v: 5:5}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Micah 5:2-5 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Small among the clans of Judah"** (5:2) | God chose a little town for the greatest King. |
| **"From the days of eternity"** (5:2) | This King existed before he was born. |
| **"She who is in labor"** (5:3) | A mother would give birth to this King. |
| **"Shepherd His flock"** (5:4) | He will lead and protect his people with care. |
| **"To the ends of the earth"** (5:4) | His greatness will reach everywhere. |
| **"He will be our peace"** (5:5) | He himself is our peace. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Bethlehem means "house of bread."** It was about six miles south of Jerusalem.†

**David was anointed in Bethlehem.** The prophet Samuel found him there with the sheep (1 Samuel 16:1, 16:13).

**Herod's scribes knew this verse.** When the wise men came looking for the newborn King, they pointed to Micah (Matthew 2:4-6).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**How can someone born in Bethlehem be "from the days of eternity"?** Christians believe Jesus, God's Son, always existed. He was born as a human in Bethlehem (John 1:1, 1:14).

**Who is "she who is in labor"?** Micah doesn't name her. Christians see Mary, the mother of Jesus.†

**Is Bethlehem today the same place?** Yes. It is a town in the West Bank, and the Church of the Nativity stands over the place long remembered as Jesus's birthplace.†

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Important things only happen in important places."*

**What's true:** Big cities matter. Jerusalem was important to God's plan.

**What the passage is mainly about:** God chose little Bethlehem for the most important birth in history. God loves to work through small, overlooked places and people.

**Where Jesus is:** Born in Bethlehem, laid in a manger, visited first by shepherds.

## ✝️ Good News

God didn't choose a palace. He chose a small town.

**"But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, who are small among the clans of Judah, out of you will come forth for Me One to be ruler over Israel"** (5:2).

Jesus was born there. He is the eternal King, the good shepherd, and he is our peace.

**From little Bethlehem, God promised an eternal King who would shepherd his people. Jesus is our peace.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther marveled that the Lord of all was born in such a small, poor place. He said God hides his greatness in lowly things, so that humble people can find him.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year C, it reads Micah 5:2-5a on the Fourth Sunday of Advent, next to Mary visiting Elizabeth.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Out of little Bethlehem, God promised a King from eternity who would shepherd his flock. He will be our peace.**

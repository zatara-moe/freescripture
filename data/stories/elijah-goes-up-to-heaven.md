# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Elijah:** A great prophet of the LORD. His work on earth is almost done.
- **Elisha:** Elijah's student and helper. He will take Elijah's place.
- **The sons of the prophets:** Groups of prophets in training who lived in different towns.
- **The LORD:** God. LORD in capital letters stands for God's personal name. Jewish readers traditionally don't say it aloud, out of respect.

**Setting**

- A journey from Gilgal to Bethel, to Jericho, and across the Jordan River.

**When**

- About 2,850 years ago.†

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Elijah had faced the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel.** God answered with fire (1 Kings 18:38).

**Then Elijah wanted to give up.** God met him on a mountain in a gentle whisper (1 Kings 19:12).

**God told Elijah to choose Elisha.** Elijah threw his cloak over Elisha, and Elisha left everything to follow him (1 Kings 19:19-21).

**Here's what everyone seems to know:** Today is the day God will take Elijah away.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: "I Will Not Leave You"

The LORD was about to take Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind. Elijah and Elisha were walking from Gilgal. {v: 2:1}

Elijah said, "Stay here. The LORD has sent me to Bethel." {v: 2:2}

Elisha replied, **"As surely as the LORD lives and as you yourself live, I will not leave you"** (2:2). {v: 2:2}

At Bethel, prophets asked Elisha, "Do you know the LORD will take your master today?" **"Yes, I know,"** he said. **"Do not speak of it"** (2:3). {v: 2:3}

Elijah tried again: "Stay here. The LORD has sent me to Jericho." Again Elisha said, "I will not leave you." {v: 2:4}

And a third time, at the Jordan River. Elisha would not leave. {v: 2:6}

### Scene 2: Across the Jordan

Fifty prophets stood at a distance, watching. {v: 2:7}

Elijah rolled up his cloak and struck the water. The river parted, and the two of them walked across on dry ground. {v: 2:8}

On the other side, Elijah asked, **"Tell me, what can I do for you before I am taken away from you?"** (2:9). {v: 2:9}

**"Please, let me inherit a double portion of your spirit,"** Elisha replied (2:9). {v: 2:9}

A double portion was what the oldest son inherited from his father. Elisha was asking to carry on Elijah's work as his true heir.† {v: 2:9}

**"You have requested a difficult thing,"** Elijah said (2:10). But if Elisha saw him taken, it would be his. {v: 2:10}

### Scene 3: Chariot of Fire

As they walked and talked, suddenly a chariot of fire and horses of fire came between them. {v: 2:11}

**"And Elijah went up into heaven in a whirlwind"** (2:11). {v: 2:11}

Elisha saw it and cried out, **"My father, my father, the chariots and horsemen of Israel!"** (2:12). {v: 2:12}

Then Elijah was gone. Elisha tore his clothes in grief. {v: 2:12}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a roaring wind and a flash of fire across the sky. Then nothing. Just Elisha standing alone by the river, and a cloak lying on the ground.

### Scene 4: The Cloak

Elisha picked up Elijah's cloak, which had fallen. He went back to the Jordan. {v: 2:13}

He struck the water with the cloak. **"Where now is the LORD, the God of Elijah?"** he asked (2:14). {v: 2:14}

The water parted, and Elisha crossed over. {v: 2:14}

The prophets watching said, **"The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha"** (2:15). {v: 2:15}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

2 Kings 2:1-15 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"I will not leave you"** (2:2) | Elisha was loyal to the very end. |
| **"Do not speak of it"** (2:3) | Elisha didn't want to talk about losing his teacher. |
| **"A double portion of your spirit"** (2:9) | Elisha wanted to carry on Elijah's work. |
| **"A chariot of fire with horses of fire"** (2:11) | God's own power came to take Elijah. |
| **"My father, my father"** (2:12) | Elisha loved Elijah like a father. |
| **"Where now is the LORD, the God of Elijah?"** (2:14) | Elijah was gone, but God was still there. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Tearing your clothes was a sign of grief.** People did it when someone died or something terrible happened.

**The Jordan parted before.** When Joshua led Israel into the promised land, the Jordan stopped flowing so they could cross (Joshua 3:16).†

**Only two people in the Bible are described as taken to God without dying.** Enoch (Genesis 5:24) and Elijah.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Did Elijah go to heaven in the chariot?** The Bible says he went up in a whirlwind, and the chariot of fire separated him from Elisha. Either way, God took him.

**Does Elijah come back?** The prophet Malachi said God would send Elijah before the day of the LORD (Malachi 4:5). Jesus said John the Baptist was that Elijah (Matthew 11:14). And Elijah appeared with Jesus on the mountain where he shone in glory (Mark 9:4).

**Did Elisha get a double portion?** The Bible records about twice as many miracles for Elisha as for Elijah.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Elisha | Loyal | He would not leave Elijah (2:2) |
| Elisha | Sad | He didn't want to talk about losing him (2:3) |
| Elisha | Grieving | He tore his clothes (2:12) |
| The prophets | Respectful | They bowed before Elisha (2:15) |

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**What does this story say to you?** Tap one.

- I lost someone :: Elisha grieved. God was still with him, and he is with you.
- Loyalty :: "I will not leave you." Who could you be loyal to?
- I feel unready :: Elisha picked up the cloak. God gives what you need.
- Where is God? :: Elisha asked. The water parted. God was still there.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"When a great leader is gone, God's work stops."*

**What's true:** Losing a teacher or leader is a real loss.

**What the story is mainly about:** Elijah was gone, but the God of Elijah was still there. Elisha picked up the cloak, and the river parted again. God's work goes on.

**Where Jesus is:** Elijah appeared with Jesus on the mountain where Jesus shone with glory (Mark 9:4). Like Elijah, Jesus was taken up into heaven (Acts 1:9). Before he left, he promised to send the Holy Spirit, so his followers could carry on his work.

## ✝️ Good News

Elijah was taken up, but God didn't leave Elisha alone.

**"Where now is the LORD, the God of Elijah?"** (2:14).

The answer: right here. When Jesus went up to heaven, he didn't leave his people alone either. He sent the Holy Spirit, and he says, **"I am with you always"** (Matthew 28:20).

**God took Elijah up to heaven and gave his spirit to Elisha. God is still with his people when a leader is gone.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that God keeps his church going from generation to generation, through his word. Leaders come and go, but the word of the Lord stands forever.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads 2 Kings 2:1-12 on Transfiguration Sunday, when Elijah appears with Jesus on the mountain. In Year C, it reads 2 Kings 2:1-2 and 6-14 in late June (Lectionary 13).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Elisha would not leave Elijah. God took Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind, and his spirit rested on Elisha. God's work goes on.**

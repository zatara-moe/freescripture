# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**What it is.** A song of praise calling the whole world, and even the trees and the sea, to sing to God.

**Who wrote it.** It doesn't say. A version of it was sung when David brought the ark of the covenant to Jerusalem (1 Chronicles 16:23-33).

**When the church sings it.** On Christmas Eve. The world sings because the King has come.

**How to read it.** Notice how the circle of praise keeps getting bigger: from God's people, to the nations, to all creation.

## 🗺️ Map of the Psalm

| Section | Verses | What it says |

| 1. A New Song | 96:1-3 | Sing and tell the nations |
| 2. Great Is the LORD | 96:4-6 | Other gods are idols |
| 3. All Families of the Nations | 96:7-10 | Come and worship |
| 4. Let the Trees Sing | 96:11-13 | All creation rejoices, for he is coming |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: A New Song

**"Sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth"** (96:1). {v: 96:1}

A new song is for something new God has done. {v: 96:1}

**"Proclaim His salvation day after day"** (96:2). {v: 96:2}

**"Declare His glory among the nations, His wonderful deeds among all peoples"** (96:3). {v: 96:3}

### Section 2: Great Is the LORD

**"For great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised"** (96:4). {v: 96:4}

The gods of the nations are just idols. **"But it is the LORD who made the heavens"** (96:5). {v: 96:5}

**"Splendor and majesty are before Him; strength and beauty fill His sanctuary"** (96:6). {v: 96:6}

### Section 3: All Families of the Nations

**"Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the nations, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength"** (96:7). {v: 96:7, 96:8}

**"Worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness; tremble before Him, all the earth"** (96:9). {v: 96:9}

Tell the nations: **"The LORD reigns!"** (96:10). {v: 96:10}

### Section 4: Let the Trees Sing

**"Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice; let the sea resound, and all that fills it"** (96:11). {v: 96:11}

The fields celebrate, and **"all the trees of the forest will sing for joy"** (96:12). {v: 96:12}

Why? **"For He is coming"** (96:13). {v: 96:13}

He will judge the world fairly and faithfully. {v: 96:13}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Psalm 96 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"A new song"** (96:1) | Fresh praise for what God has done. |
| **"All the earth"** (96:1) | Praise isn't only for one nation. |
| **"Proclaim His salvation day after day"** (96:2) | Keep telling the good news. |
| **"The LORD reigns!"** (96:10) | God is King, now and always. |
| **"All the trees of the forest will sing"** (96:12) | All creation celebrates God's coming. |
| **"For He is coming"** (96:13) | God is on his way to set the world right. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Israel's neighbors** worshiped many gods. This psalm says they are idols, and only the LORD made the heavens.

**Judging** in the Bible is often good news. It means setting wrongs right and protecting the weak.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Do trees really sing?** It's poetry. All of creation is pictured celebrating its King.

**Why is God's coming to "judge" a reason to sing?** Because he judges **"in righteousness"** and **"in His faithfulness"** (96:13). He comes to make everything right.

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| God's people | Joyful | They sing a new song (96:1) |
| All creation | Glad | The LORD is coming (96:11-13) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Faith is a private thing."*

**What's true:** Faith is personal.

**What the passage is mainly about:** **"Declare His glory among the nations"** (96:3). God's good news is meant to be sung out loud, to the whole world.

**Where Jesus is:** He is the King who came at Christmas and is coming again.

## ✝️ Good News

The King is coming, and the whole world will sing.

**"Let the heavens be glad and the earth rejoice"** (96:11).

He comes to set the world right, in righteousness and faithfulness.

**Sing to the LORD a new song, all the earth! The LORD reigns, and he is coming to judge the world in righteousness.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther loved singing. He wrote that next to theology, he gave music the highest place and honor, because it drives away the devil and makes people glad.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It sings Psalm 96 on Christmas Eve in all three years, and on several other Sundays.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Sing to the LORD a new song, all the earth! Tell the nations, "The LORD reigns!" Let all creation rejoice, for he is coming.**

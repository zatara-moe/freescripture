# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God. Here he is a baby, about six weeks old.
- **Mary and Joseph:** Jesus's mother, and Joseph, her husband, who raised Jesus as his son.
- **Simeon:** An old man in Jerusalem who loves God and has been waiting a long time.
- **Anna:** A very old widow and prophetess who spends her days in the temple. A prophetess is a woman who speaks for God.

**Setting**

- The temple courts in Jerusalem. The temple was the center of worship for God's people.

**When**

- About 2,000 years ago, forty days after Jesus was born.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus was born in Bethlehem.** Mary laid him in a manger, a feeding box for animals (2:7). Shepherds came to see him (2:16).

**On his eighth day, he was named.** Mary and Joseph named him Jesus, the name the angel had given (2:21).

**God's law had two rules for a new baby.** A mother brought an offering forty days after a son was born (Leviticus 12:2-6). And every firstborn son belonged to God in a special way (Exodus 13:2).

**God's people had been waiting a long time.** The prophet Isaiah had written God's words, **"Comfort, comfort My people"** (Isaiah 40:1). Many people were still waiting for that comfort.

**Here's what you know that most people in the temple don't:** This baby is the Christ, the Savior God promised. An angel told the shepherds so (2:11).

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: A Trip to the Temple

Forty days after Jesus was born, it was time to keep God's law about a new baby. {v: 2:22}

Mary and Joseph carried Jesus to Jerusalem. They came to present him to the Lord. To present means to bring someone and show them. {v: 2:22}

The law said, **"Every firstborn male shall be consecrated to the Lord"** (2:23). Consecrated means set apart for God. {v: 2:23}

They also brought an offering. The law said, **"A pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons"** (2:24). Turtledoves are small, gentle birds. {v: 2:24}

The law let families who could not afford a lamb bring two birds instead (Leviticus 12:8). Mary and Joseph brought birds. {v: 2:24}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a young couple in a crowded courtyard. Mary holds the baby close. Joseph carries a small cage with two birds inside.

### Scene 2: A Man Who Was Waiting

A man named Simeon lived in Jerusalem. He was righteous and devout. That means he loved God and tried to live God's way. {v: 2:25}

Simeon was waiting for **"the consolation of Israel"** (2:25). Consolation means comfort. He was waiting for God to keep his promise to comfort his people. {v: 2:25}

The Holy Spirit was upon Simeon. The Spirit had told him something special: he would not die before he saw the Lord's Christ. The Christ is the one God promised to send. {v: 2:25, 2:26}

That day, the Spirit led Simeon into the temple courts. He arrived just as Mary and Joseph brought Jesus in. {v: 2:27}

### Scene 3: Simeon's Song

Simeon took the baby Jesus in his arms. He praised God. {v: 2:28}

**"Sovereign Lord, as You have promised, You now dismiss Your servant in peace"** (2:29). Sovereign means the one who rules over everything. To dismiss means to let someone go. Simeon was ready to die in peace. {v: 2:29}

**"For my eyes have seen Your salvation"** (2:30). Salvation means God's rescue. Simeon was holding it in his arms. {v: 2:30}

God's salvation was for everyone. Simeon called it **"a light for revelation to the Gentiles, and for glory to Your people Israel"** (2:32). Gentiles are people who are not Jewish. {v: 2:31, 2:32}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture an old man with tears in his eyes, gently rocking a sleeping baby. People nearby stop to watch.

### Scene 4: Words for Mary

Mary and Joseph were amazed at what Simeon said about their child. {v: 2:33}

Then Simeon blessed them. He spoke to Mary. {v: 2:34}

He said this child would cause many in Israel to fall and to rise. He would be **"a sign that will be spoken against"** (2:34). Many people would argue against Jesus. {v: 2:34}

Then Simeon said, **"a sword will pierce your soul as well"** (2:35). {v: 2:35}

This is a comparison. No one would stab Mary with a real sword. It means Mary would feel deep pain. Many years later, Mary stood near the cross when Jesus died (John 19:25). {v: 2:35}

### Scene 5: Anna

There was also a prophetess named Anna. She was the daughter of Phanuel, from the tribe of Asher. {v: 2:36}

Anna was very old. She had been married for seven years. Then her husband died, and she was a widow until she was eighty-four. A widow is a woman whose husband has died. {v: 2:36, 2:37}

Anna never left the temple. She worshiped God night and day. She fasted and prayed. To fast means to go without food for a time so you can pray. {v: 2:37}

At that very moment, Anna came up to them. She gave thanks to God. {v: 2:38}

Then she told everyone about the child. She told all the people who were waiting for God to rescue Jerusalem. {v: 2:38}

### Scene 6: Home to Nazareth

Mary and Joseph did everything God's law asked. Then they went home to their own town, Nazareth, in Galilee. {v: 2:39}

The child grew and became strong. He was filled with wisdom. And the grace of God was upon him. Grace means God's kindness and favor. {v: 2:40}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Luke 2:22-40 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"To present Him to the Lord"** (2:22) | Mary and Joseph kept God's law. Jesus was born under the law, to keep it for us (Galatians 4:4-5). |
| **"A pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons"** (2:24) | This was the offering for families who could not afford a lamb. Jesus was born into a family that did not have much.† |
| **"Waiting for the consolation of Israel"** (2:25) | Simeon trusted God's promise to comfort his people. He waited his whole life. |
| **"You now dismiss Your servant in peace"** (2:29) | Simeon was ready to die. He had seen what God promised. |
| **"A light for revelation to the Gentiles"** (2:32) | Jesus came for every nation, not only for one people. God had promised this through Isaiah (Isaiah 49:6). |
| **"A sword will pierce your soul"** (2:35) | Mary would suffer deep sorrow when Jesus suffered. |
| **"She gave thanks to God and spoke about the Child"** (2:38) | Anna was one of the first people to tell others about Jesus. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**The temple had large open courts.** Many people came there each day to pray and bring offerings. Simeon and Anna met the family in these busy courts.

**Forty days is how the law counted.** A mother waited seven days, and then thirty-three more, before bringing her offering (Leviticus 12:2-4). So Jesus was about six weeks old.

**Asher was one of the twelve tribes of Israel.** Each tribe came from one of Jacob's sons. Asher's land was in the far north (Easton's Bible Dictionary).

**Simeon's song has been sung for centuries.** In Latin it is called the Nunc Dimittis. Those are its first two words: "now you dismiss."

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Was Anna eighty-four, or a widow for eighty-four years?** The Greek words can be read both ways. The BSB says she was a widow **"to the age of eighty-four"** (2:37). Some other Bibles say she had been a widow for eighty-four years. Either way, she was very old, and she had waited a long time.

**Did Anna live in the temple?** Luke says she **"never left the temple"** (2:37). Many readers think it means she was there every day, from early morning until night. Luke doesn't say where she slept.

**Did Jesus need to be made clean?** No. The offering was about the law for a mother after giving birth. Jesus was born under the law, and his family kept it.

**Why was Simeon happy to die?** He wasn't sad to leave. He had waited all his life to see God's promise. Now he had held it in his arms. He could go in peace.

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Simeon | Peaceful | He had seen God's salvation (2:29-30) |
| Mary and Joseph | Amazed | Simeon said wonderful things about their child (2:33) |
| Mary | Troubled | Simeon said a sword would pierce her soul (2:35) |
| Anna | Thankful | She gave thanks to God when she saw Jesus (2:38) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God is for young, busy, important people."*

**What's true:** God uses all kinds of people.

**What the story is mainly about:** The first people in the temple to welcome Jesus were not priests or leaders. They were an old man and a very old widow, people who had waited a long time. God saw them. God brought Jesus to them.

**Where Jesus is:** In Simeon's arms. God's salvation came as a small baby, carried in by a family that could only afford two birds.

## ✝️ Good News

Simeon waited his whole life for God's promise. Then he held it in his arms.

**"For my eyes have seen Your salvation"** (2:30).

Many Lutheran churches sing Simeon's song after Holy Communion. People who have just received Jesus's body and blood sing what Simeon sang.†

**God keeps his promises. Jesus is his salvation, and he came for you.**

## 📚 From Luther

In 1524, Luther wrote a hymn based on Simeon's song. In English it is often called "In Peace and Joy I Now Depart." In it, Luther sings that Christ makes death like a sleep for those who trust him.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It reads Luke 2:22-40 on the First Sunday of Christmas in Year B. Many churches also keep February 2 as the Presentation of Our Lord, and read this story every year.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Simeon and Anna waited, and God kept his promise. Jesus is God's salvation, a light for every nation.**

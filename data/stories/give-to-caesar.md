# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God. It is a few days before he dies.
- **Spies:** Men sent by the religious leaders. They pretend to be honest so they can trap Jesus.
- **The Sadducees:** A group of leaders, many of them priests. They do not believe the dead will rise.
- **Some scribes:** Teachers of God's law, listening in.

**Setting**

- The temple courts in Jerusalem.

**When**

- Holy Week, about 2,000 years ago, a few days before Jesus was crucified.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Jesus had ridden into Jerusalem.** Crowds praised him as King (19:38).

**Jesus taught every day in the temple.** The leaders were looking for a way to kill him, but the people hung on his every word (19:47-48).

**The leaders had to be careful.** If Jesus said something against Rome, they could hand him over to the Roman governor. If he said something people didn't like, the crowds might turn on him.†

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: A Trap

The leaders watched Jesus closely. They sent spies who pretended to be sincere. {v: 20:20}

They hoped to catch him saying something wrong, so they could hand him over to the Roman governor. {v: 20:20}

The spies started with flattery. **"Teacher,"** they said, **"we know that You speak and teach correctly"** (20:21). {v: 20:21}

Then came the trap. **"Is it lawful for us to pay taxes to Caesar or not?"** (20:22). Caesar was the Roman emperor. {v: 20:22}

If Jesus said yes, the people would be angry, because they hated paying Rome. If he said no, the Romans could arrest him.† {v: 20:22}

### Scene 2: Whose Picture?

But Jesus saw through their trick. {v: 20:23}

**"Show Me a denarius. Whose image and inscription are on it?"** (20:24). A denarius was a silver Roman coin. {v: 20:24}

**"Caesar's,"** they answered (20:24). {v: 20:24}

**"Give to Caesar what is Caesar's, and to God what is God's"** (20:25). {v: 20:25}

They couldn't trap him. They were amazed at his answer, and they went quiet. {v: 20:26}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture Jesus holding up a small silver coin between two fingers, turning it so everyone can see the emperor's face. The spies say nothing.

### Scene 3: Seven Brothers

Next came some Sadducees. They did not believe that the dead would rise. {v: 20:27}

They told Jesus a story. God's law said that if a man died without children, his brother should marry the widow. {v: 20:28}

There were seven brothers. The first married a woman and died. Then the second married her, and the third, and all seven died. Then the woman died too. {v: 20:29, 20:30, 20:31, 20:32}

**"So then, in the resurrection, whose wife will she be? For all seven were married to her"** (20:33). {v: 20:33}

They thought the question made the resurrection sound silly. {v: 20:33}

### Scene 4: The God of the Living

Jesus said that life in this age includes marriage. But life after the resurrection is different. {v: 20:34, 20:35}

**"In fact, they can no longer die, because they are like the angels"** (20:36). {v: 20:36}

Then Jesus pointed to Moses, whom the Sadducees trusted. At the burning bush, God called himself the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. They had died long before. {v: 20:37}

**"He is not the God of the dead, but of the living, for to Him all are alive"** (20:38). {v: 20:38}

Some scribes said, **"Teacher, You have spoken well!"** (20:39). {v: 20:39}

After that, nobody dared to ask him any more questions. {v: 20:40}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Luke 20:20-40 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Spies who pretended to be sincere"** (20:20) | They weren't really asking. They wanted to trap Jesus. |
| **"Whose image and inscription are on it?"** (20:24) | The coin carried Caesar's picture, so it belonged to Caesar. |
| **"Give to Caesar what is Caesar's"** (20:25) | It is right to pay taxes and respect the government. |
| **"And to God what is God's"** (20:25) | People carry God's image. We belong to God. |
| **"They can no longer die"** (20:36) | The resurrection is a new kind of life that never ends. |
| **"He is not the God of the dead, but of the living"** (20:38) | People who die trusting God are alive to him. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**The Roman coin had the emperor's face.** The inscription called Tiberius Caesar the son of a god. Many Jewish people found that offensive.†

**The Sadducees only accepted the first five books of the Bible as their authority.** That is why Jesus answered them from Moses.†

**God's law about a brother's widow is in Deuteronomy 25:5-6.** It protected a widow and kept the family name alive.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does this mean God and the government are equal?** No. Jesus says to give the government what it is owed, like taxes. But everything, even Caesar, belongs to God.

**Will people be married in heaven?** Jesus said life after the resurrection is different. That doesn't mean people forget the ones they loved. Jesus didn't say more, and neither should we.†

**Do people become angels when they die?** No. Jesus said people will be *like* the angels in one way: they will never die again. People stay people, raised to new life.†

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**What do you belong to?** Tap one.

- God :: You carry God's image, like a coin carries a picture. You belong to him.
- My country :: You can be a good citizen. Jesus said give Caesar what is Caesar's.
- Not sure :: Jesus says you are God's. He made you and loves you.
- Someone died :: Jesus says God is the God of the living. To him, all are alive.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"When you die, that's the end."*

**What's true:** Death is real, and it is sad. Jesus wept when his friend died.

**What the story is mainly about:** Jesus said the dead will rise. God is **"not the God of the dead, but of the living"** (20:38). A few days later, Jesus himself rose from the dead.

**Where Jesus is:** In the temple courts, a few days before his own death, saying that death is not the end.

## ✝️ Good News

People tried to trap Jesus. He told them the truth: we belong to God, and God is the God of the living.

**"He is not the God of the dead, but of the living, for to Him all are alive"** (20:38).

Jesus died and rose again. Because he lives, everyone who belongs to him will live too.

**You belong to God. And because Jesus rose, death does not have the last word.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that God rules the world in two ways. Through governments, he keeps order. Through the gospel, he forgives and saves. Christians honor the government, but they belong to God first.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year A, it reads Matthew's version of the coin question, Matthew 22:15-22 (Lectionary 29). In Year C, it reads Luke 20:27-38 in November (Lectionary 32).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Give to Caesar what is Caesar's, and to God what is God's. You belong to God, and he is the God of the living.**

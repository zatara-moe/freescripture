# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **John the Baptist:** The prophet who prepared the way for Jesus and baptized him.
- **King Herod:** Herod Antipas, ruler of Galilee under Rome. He is a son of the Herod who ruled when Jesus was born.
- **Herodias:** Herod's wife. She had been married to Herod's brother Philip.
- **Herodias's daughter:** A girl who dances at Herod's party.
- **John's disciples:** John's followers.

**Setting**

- Herod's palace, and the prison where John is held.

**When**

- About 2,000 years ago, while Jesus was teaching in Galilee.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**John told people to turn back to God.** He told everyone the truth, even powerful people (1:4-5).

**John said someone greater was coming.** That someone was Jesus (1:7-8).

**Jesus had just sent out his twelve apostles.** They were preaching and healing in village after village (6:7-13). Jesus's name was becoming famous everywhere.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: Who Is Jesus?

King Herod heard about Jesus. Everyone was talking about him. {v: 6:14}

Some people said John the Baptist had come back from the dead. Others said Jesus was Elijah, or another prophet. {v: 6:14, 6:15}

But Herod said, **"John, whom I beheaded, has risen from the dead!"** (6:16). {v: 6:16}

Herod was afraid. He remembered what he had done to John. {v: 6:16}

### Scene 2: John Speaks the Truth

Here is what had happened. {v: 6:17}

Herod had married Herodias, the wife of his brother Philip. {v: 6:17}

John told Herod, **"It is not lawful for you to have your brother's wife!"** (6:18). It was wrong under God's law. {v: 6:18}

So Herod had John arrested and locked in prison. {v: 6:17}

Herodias held a grudge against John. She wanted him dead. But Herod protected John. {v: 6:19, 6:20}

Herod knew John was a good and holy man. He was confused by what John said, but he liked to listen to him. {v: 6:20}

### Scene 3: A Birthday Party

Then came Herod's birthday. He threw a big party for important officials, army commanders, and leaders of Galilee. {v: 6:21}

Herodias's daughter came in and danced. Herod and his guests were pleased. {v: 6:22}

Herod made a foolish promise. **"Whatever you ask of me, I will give you, up to half my kingdom!"** (6:23). {v: 6:22, 6:23}

The girl went out and asked her mother what to ask for. {v: 6:24}

Herodias saw her chance. She told her daughter to ask for John's death. {v: 6:24}

The girl hurried back to the king and asked for it. {v: 6:25}

### Scene 4: A Terrible Order

Herod was very sad. {v: 6:26}

But he had made a promise in front of all his guests. He didn't want to look weak, so he didn't refuse. {v: 6:26}

He sent an executioner to the prison, and John was killed. {v: 6:27, 6:28}

When John's disciples heard, they came and took his body and laid it in a tomb. {v: 6:29}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture a small group of men walking quietly away from the prison, carrying their teacher's body. No one at the party notices. The music is still playing.

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Mark 6:14-29 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"John, whom I beheaded, has risen from the dead!"** (6:16) | Herod's guilty conscience troubled him. |
| **"It is not lawful for you"** (6:18) | John told the truth, even to a king. |
| **"Herod feared John"** (6:20) | Herod knew John was a holy man. |
| **"Up to half my kingdom"** (6:23) | Herod made a showy promise to impress his guests. |
| **"Because of his oaths and his guests"** (6:26) | Herod cared more about looking strong than doing right. |
| **"Placed it in a tomb"** (6:29) | John's friends honored him to the end. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Herod Antipas was not really a king.** Rome called him a tetrarch, a ruler of part of a country. People called him king anyway.†

**The historian Josephus also wrote about John's death.** He said Herod killed John at the fortress of Machaerus, because he feared John's influence over the people.†

**Herodias's daughter was named Salome.** Mark doesn't give her name, but Josephus does.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Why did God let John die?** The Bible doesn't fully answer that. John finished the work God gave him: he prepared the way for Jesus. Jesus called him the greatest of the prophets (Matthew 11:11). Death was not the end for John.†

**Do you have to keep a promise to do something wrong?** No. Herod should have broken a foolish promise rather than do evil. If anyone ever makes you promise to keep a secret that is wrong or unsafe, tell a trusted adult.

**Was the girl to blame?** She went along with her mother's plan. Mark puts the most blame on Herodias for planning it and on Herod for giving the order.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Herod | Afraid | He thought John had come back (6:16) |
| Herodias | Bitter | She held a grudge against John (6:19) |
| Herod | Sad | He didn't want to kill John (6:26) |
| John's disciples | Grieving | Their teacher had been killed (6:29) |

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**What part of this story stays with you?** Tap one.

- John was brave :: John told the truth to a king. God can make you brave too.
- It's unfair :: It was unfair. Jesus was killed unfairly too, and God raised him.
- Peer pressure :: Herod gave in to save face. You can say no, even in front of people.
- I'm sad :: That's okay. John's friends were sad too. Jesus knows grief.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"If you follow God, nothing bad will happen to you."*

**What's true:** God is with his people always.

**What the story is mainly about:** John was faithful, and he still suffered and died. Following God doesn't mean nothing bad happens. It means God holds you, and death is not the end. Jesus would suffer too, and rise.

**Where Jesus is:** Teaching nearby. Soon he would walk the same road John walked: arrested, killed, and laid in a tomb. Then he would rise.

## ✝️ Good News

Herod thought John's death was the end. It wasn't.

**"Truly I tell you, among those born of women there has risen no one greater than John the Baptist"** (Matthew 11:11).

John prepared the way for Jesus. Jesus died too, and rose again. Because he lives, John lives, and everyone who trusts Jesus will live.

**John was faithful to the end. Jesus, the one John pointed to, defeated death for him and for us.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther praised John for telling the truth to a ruler. He taught that pastors must speak God's word plainly, even to powerful people, and leave the rest to God.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Mark 6:14-29 in July (Lectionary 15).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**John the Baptist told a king the truth, and was killed for it. Death was not the end, because Jesus, the one John pointed to, rose again.**

# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Abram (soon Abraham):** A man God called to leave his home. He is ninety-nine years old.
- **Sarai (soon Sarah):** Abram's wife. She is eighty-nine and has never had a child.
- **Ishmael:** Abram's thirteen-year-old son, born to Sarai's servant Hagar.
- **The LORD:** God. LORD in capital letters stands for God's personal name. Jewish readers traditionally don't say it aloud, out of respect.

**Setting**

- The land of Canaan, where Abram lived in tents.

**When**

- About 4,000 years ago.†

## 🧭 Before the Story

**God promised Abram a great family.** **"I will make you into a great nation"** (12:2).

**God counted Abram's faith as righteousness.** Abram looked at the stars and believed God's promise (15:5-6).

**But Sarai had no child.** So she gave her servant Hagar to Abram, and Hagar had Ishmael (16:1-4, 16:15). Thirteen years passed.

## 🗺️ Map of the Story

| Scene | Verses | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. "I am God Almighty" | 17:1-2 | God appears to ninety-nine-year-old Abram |
| 2. Abraham | 17:3-8 | God gives Abram a new name and an everlasting promise |
| 3. A sign | 17:9-14 | God gives a sign of the covenant |
| 4. Sarah | 17:15-16 | God gives Sarai a new name too |
| 5. Abraham laughs | 17:17-22 | Abraham can't believe it, and God names the son Isaac |

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: "I Am God Almighty"

When Abram was ninety-nine, the LORD appeared to him. {v: 17:1}

**"I am God Almighty. Walk before Me and be blameless"** (17:1). {v: 17:1}

God Almighty means God who can do anything. Blameless means living faithfully with God. {v: 17:1}

**"I will establish My covenant between Me and you"** (17:2). A covenant is a binding promise. {v: 17:2}

### Scene 2: Abraham

Abram fell facedown. {v: 17:3}

God said, **"No longer will you be called Abram, but your name will be Abraham, for I have made you a father of many nations"** (17:5). {v: 17:4, 17:5}

Abram means "exalted father." Abraham means "father of many." He had only one son, and God was already calling him the father of many.† {v: 17:5}

God promised kings would come from him. And God promised, **"to be your God and the God of your descendants after you"** (17:7). {v: 17:6, 17:7}

### Scene 3: A Sign

God gave a sign of the covenant, to mark his people. {v: 17:9, 17:10}

Every boy in Abraham's family would be circumcised when he was eight days old. Circumcision is a small cut on the male body. {v: 17:11, 17:12}

It was a sign, carried in their bodies, that they belonged to God's promise. {v: 17:13}

### Scene 4: Sarah

Then God said Sarai would have a new name too: Sarah. Both names mean "princess."† {v: 17:15}

**"And I will bless her and will surely give you a son by her"** (17:16). {v: 17:16}

**"She will be the mother of nations"** (17:16). {v: 17:16}

### Scene 5: Abraham Laughs

Abraham fell facedown again. And he laughed. {v: 17:17}

**"Can a child be born to a man who is a hundred years old? Can Sarah give birth at the age of ninety?"** (17:17). {v: 17:17}

He asked God to bless Ishmael instead. {v: 17:18}

God said, **"Your wife Sarah will indeed bear you a son, and you are to name him Isaac"** (17:19). {v: 17:19}

Isaac means "he laughs." {v: 17:19}

God promised to bless Ishmael too, and make him a great nation. But the covenant would go through Isaac, born next year. {v: 17:20, 17:21}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture an old man face down in the dirt, shoulders shaking. Is he laughing or crying? Maybe both. God has just told him to name a son who isn't born yet.

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Genesis 17:1-22 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"I am God Almighty"** (17:1) | God can do what seems impossible. |
| **"Your name will be Abraham"** (17:5) | God gave him a name that matched the promise. |
| **"An everlasting covenant"** (17:7) | God's promise would never end. |
| **"A sign of the covenant"** (17:11) | God marked his people as his own. |
| **"She will be the mother of nations"** (17:16) | Sarah was part of the promise too. |
| **"Then he laughed"** (17:17) | Even faithful Abraham found it hard to believe. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Names showed who you were.** A new name meant a new identity.

**Ishmael became the father of a great people.** Many Arab peoples trace their family line back to him.†

**Sarah laughed too.** When God repeated the promise, she laughed to herself (18:12). When Isaac was born, she said, "God has made me laugh" (21:6).

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Do Christians still practice circumcision as a sign?** No. The New Testament teaches that baptism is the sign of God's covenant now, for boys and girls alike (Colossians 2:11-12).†

**Did God forget Ishmael?** No. God heard Abraham's prayer and promised to bless Ishmael (17:20).

**Was it wrong for Abraham to laugh?** God didn't scold him. God answered by naming the child "he laughs."

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Abraham | Awed | He fell facedown before God (17:3) |
| Abraham | Doubtful | He laughed at the idea of a son at 100 (17:17) |
| Abraham | Worried | He asked God to bless Ishmael (17:18) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God only works with young, strong people."*

**What's true:** God gives energy and strength to young people.

**What the story is mainly about:** God promised a son to a ninety-nine-year-old man and an eighty-nine-year-old woman. He gave them new names to match. God Almighty does what seems impossible.

**Where Jesus is:** Paul wrote that Abraham **"believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness"** (Romans 4:3). Through Abraham's family, Jesus came to bless every nation.

## ✝️ Good News

God gave Abraham and Sarah new names before the baby was even born. His promise was that sure.

**"I will establish My covenant as an everlasting covenant between Me and you and your descendants after you, to be your God"** (17:7).

In baptism, God gives us a new name too: his beloved child. His covenant is everlasting.

**God Almighty gave Abraham and Sarah new names and promised them a son. His covenant to be their God would last forever.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that God's promise comes first, and faith simply trusts it. Abraham didn't earn the promise. He received it.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Genesis 17:1-7 and 15-16 on the Second Sunday in Lent.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**When Abram was ninety-nine, God Almighty gave him and Sarai new names, Abraham and Sarah, and promised them a son. God's covenant lasts forever.**

# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**What it is.** The longest chapter in the Bible: 176 verses, all about God's word.

**How it's built.** It has 22 sections, one for each letter of the Hebrew alphabet. Every verse in each section begins with that letter. It's a love song to God's word from A to Z.

**Who wrote it.** It doesn't say.

**How to read it.** This page walks through the sections read on Sundays. Notice how many different words the writer uses for God's word: law, testimonies, precepts, statutes, commandments, judgments, word, promise.

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Psalm 119:1-16, 33-40, 97-112, 129-136 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Who walk in the Law of the LORD"** (119:1) | Who live by God's teaching. |
| **"I have hidden Your word in my heart"** (119:11) | I know it by heart. |
| **"Revive me with Your word"** (119:37) | God's word gives new life. |
| **"Sweeter than honey"** (119:103) | God's word is a delight, not a burden. |
| **"A lamp to my feet"** (119:105) | God's word shows the next step. |
| **"It informs the simple"** (119:130) | Anyone can understand God's word. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**"Law"** in Hebrew is *Torah*. It means teaching or instruction, not just rules.

**Oil lamps** were small clay bowls with a wick. They gave a little light, enough for the next step.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Does the writer think he's perfect?** No. He asks God not to forsake him (119:8), says he is afflicted (119:107), and at the very end admits he has strayed like a lost sheep (119:176).

**Do I have to memorize the Bible?** "Hiding God's word in your heart" starts small: one verse at a time.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"The Bible is a book of rules."*

**What's true:** God's word teaches us how to live.

**What the passage is mainly about:** The writer calls God's word sweeter than honey, the joy of his heart, and a light for his path. **"Oh, how I love Your law!"** (119:97). It's a gift, not a burden.

**Where Jesus is:** He is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the light of the world.

## ✝️ Good News

You don't have to find your way in the dark.

**"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path"** (119:105).

God's word gives light, revives the tired, and shows the next step.

**Psalm 119 is a love song to God's word. It is a lamp to our feet, sweeter than honey, and it revives us and lights our path.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther said Psalm 119 teaches three things: prayer, meditation on God's word, and the struggle that comes with faith. He called them the way to become a true theologian.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It sings sections of Psalm 119 on several Sundays, including the Sixth and Seventh Sundays after the Epiphany in Year A and Sundays in the fall.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Psalm 119 is a love song to God's word from A to Z. Hide it in your heart: it is sweeter than honey, it revives us, and it is a lamp to our feet and a light to our path.**

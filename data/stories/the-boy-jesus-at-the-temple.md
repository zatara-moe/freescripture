# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God. Here he is twelve years old.
- **Mary and Joseph:** Jesus's mother, and Joseph, her husband, who raised Jesus as his son.
- **The teachers:** Experts in God's law who taught in the temple courts.

**Setting**

- Jerusalem, during the Passover feast. Most of the story happens in the temple courts.

**When**

- About 2,000 years ago, when Jesus was twelve years old.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**When Jesus was a baby, Mary and Joseph brought him to the temple.** An old man named Simeon held him and praised God (2:28).

**Then the family went home to Nazareth.** Jesus grew strong and wise, and God's grace was on him (2:40).

**Passover was a great yearly feast.** Families traveled to Jerusalem to remember how God rescued his people from Egypt.

**Here's what you know that most people don't:** Jesus is God's Son. Mary and Joseph know he is special. They don't understand everything yet.

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: The Trip to Jerusalem

Every year, Jesus's parents went to Jerusalem for the Passover feast. {v: 2:41}

When Jesus was twelve years old, they went up as usual. {v: 2:42}

### Scene 2: Missing

When the feast was over, the family started home. But the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem. His parents didn't know. {v: 2:43}

They thought he was somewhere in the big group traveling together. They walked a whole day. Then they looked for him among their relatives and friends. {v: 2:44}

He wasn't there. So they went back to Jerusalem to search for him. {v: 2:45}

> 🎥 **Imagine the Scene** *(imagination, not Scripture)*
> Picture Mary and Joseph hurrying back along the dusty road, asking everyone they pass, "Have you seen a twelve-year-old boy?"

### Scene 3: Three Days Later

After three days, they found him in the temple courts. {v: 2:46}

He was sitting with the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions. {v: 2:46}

Everyone who heard him was amazed at how much he understood, and at his answers. {v: 2:47}

### Scene 4: "My Father's House"

When his parents saw him, they were astonished. {v: 2:48}

**"Child, why have You done this to us?"** his mother asked. **"Your father and I have been anxiously searching for You"** (2:48). Anxiously means with worry and fear. {v: 2:48}

**"Why were you looking for Me?"** Jesus asked. **"Did you not know that I had to be in My Father's house?"** (2:49). {v: 2:49}

Mary had said "your father," meaning Joseph. Jesus said "My Father," meaning God. The temple was his Father's house. {v: 2:48, 2:49}

They didn't understand what he meant. {v: 2:50}

### Scene 5: Growing Up

Then Jesus went home to Nazareth with them. He obeyed them. {v: 2:51}

Mary treasured all these things in her heart. She kept thinking about them. {v: 2:51}

**"And Jesus grew in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and man"** (2:52). Stature means height. Jesus grew up like any boy, in mind and body. {v: 2:52}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Luke 2:41-52 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Every year His parents went to Jerusalem"** (2:41) | Jesus grew up in a family that kept God's feasts. |
| **"Listening to them and asking them questions"** (2:46) | Jesus learned like a real boy. He listened and asked. |
| **"Astounded at His understanding"** (2:47) | Even at twelve, Jesus understood God's word deeply. |
| **"I had to be in My Father's house"** (2:49) | Jesus knew God was his Father in a special way. |
| **"Was obedient to them"** (2:51) | Jesus honored his parents. He kept the commandment for us. |
| **"Jesus grew in wisdom and stature"** (2:52) | Jesus was fully human. He really grew up. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Families traveled in big groups.** People from the same town walked to Jerusalem together, often with relatives. Children could easily spend the day with cousins or friends in the group.†

**Teachers in the temple courts sat and discussed God's law.** Students listened and asked questions.†

**Twelve was close to adulthood.** At about thirteen, a Jewish boy began taking on the duties of keeping God's law as a grown man.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Did Jesus run away?** Luke doesn't say Jesus ran away. He stayed behind, and his parents didn't know. Then he went home and obeyed them (2:51).

**Were Mary and Joseph bad parents for losing him?** No. In a big traveling group, they thought he was with relatives. When they found out, they searched hard. Any parent would be worried.

**Did Jesus sin by worrying his parents?** No. The Bible says Jesus was without sin (Hebrews 4:15). He knew he belonged in his Father's house. His parents didn't understand that yet.

**If I'm ever lost or separated from my family, what should I do?** Stay where you are, or find a safe grown-up, like a police officer, a store worker, or a parent with kids. Tell them your name and your family's phone number if you know it.

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

**What do you like most about this story?** Tap one.

- Jesus asked questions :: Questions are good. Jesus listened and asked. You can too.
- My Father's house :: Jesus felt at home with God. You can too, in God's house and in his word.
- Jesus obeyed :: Jesus honored his parents. He kept God's commandments perfectly, for us.
- Jesus grew up :: Jesus was a real kid who grew taller and wiser. He knows what growing up is like.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Jesus never had to learn anything."*

**What's true:** Jesus is God's Son. Even at twelve, he understood God's word deeply.

**What the story is mainly about:** Jesus also really grew up. He listened, asked questions, and grew in wisdom (2:46, 2:52). He was fully human. He knows what it is like to be a kid, a teenager, and a grown-up.

**Where Jesus is:** In his Father's house, and then back home in Nazareth, obeying his parents and growing up.

## ✝️ Good News

Jesus grew up like any boy. He obeyed his parents. He kept God's commandments perfectly, every day.

**"And Jesus grew in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and man"** (2:52).

He lived the perfect life we could not live, and he gives it to us. He knows what it is like to grow up, because he did.

**Jesus grew up as a real human being, at home in his Father's house. He knows our lives, and he lived them perfectly for us.**

## 📚 From Luther

In the Small Catechism, Luther explains the Fourth Commandment: "Honor your father and your mother." He teaches that we should not despise or anger our parents and others in authority, but honor, serve, obey, love, and respect them. Jesus did this perfectly.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It reads Luke 2:41-52 on the First Sunday of Christmas in Year C.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**At twelve, Jesus was at home in his Father's house. He grew up as a real human being, and he kept God's law perfectly for us.**

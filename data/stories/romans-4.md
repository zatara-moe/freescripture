# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** Paul wrote this letter to the Christians in Rome around AD 57.

**What came before.** In chapter 3, Paul said everyone has sinned and falls short of God's glory. But people are made right with God freely, by his grace, through faith in Jesus (3:23-24). **"For we maintain that a man is justified by faith apart from works of the law"** (3:28).

**What this chapter is.** Proof from Scripture. Paul goes back to Abraham, the father of God's people, to show that it was always this way.

**A word to know.** "Credited" is a bookkeeping word. It means put into someone's account.

## 🗺️ Map of the Letter

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Abraham Believed God | 4:1-8 | Righteousness is a gift, not wages |
| 2. Before the Sign | 4:9-12 | Abraham was counted righteous before circumcision |
| 3. The Promise Comes by Faith | 4:13-17 | Abraham is the father of all who believe |
| 4. Against All Hope | 4:18-25 | Abraham trusted the God who gives life to the dead |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Abraham Believed God

Was Abraham made right with God by what he did? Then he could brag. But not before God. {v: 4:1, 4:2}

**"For what does the Scripture say?"** It says, **"Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness"** (4:3). {v: 4:3}

When you work, your pay isn't a gift. You earned it. {v: 4:4}

**"However, to the one who does not work, but believes in Him who justifies the wicked, his faith is credited as righteousness"** (4:5). {v: 4:5}

God declares the ungodly righteous. That's grace. {v: 4:5}

King David said the same thing: **"Blessed are they whose lawless acts are forgiven, whose sins are covered"** (4:7). {v: 4:6, 4:7, 4:8}

### Section 2: Before the Sign

When was Abraham counted righteous? Before or after he was circumcised? {v: 4:9, 4:10}

Before. Circumcision was a sign of the righteousness he already had by faith. {v: 4:10, 4:11}

So Abraham is the father of everyone who believes, Jewish or not. {v: 4:11, 4:12}

### Section 3: The Promise Comes by Faith

God's promise to Abraham didn't come through the law. It came through faith. {v: 4:13}

**"Therefore, the promise comes by faith, so that it may rest on grace and may be guaranteed to all Abraham's offspring"** (4:16). {v: 4:16}

If it depended on us, it wouldn't be guaranteed. Because it rests on grace, it's sure. {v: 4:16}

Abraham believed **"the God who gives life to the dead and calls into being what does not yet exist"** (4:17). {v: 4:17}

### Section 4: Against All Hope

**"Against all hope, Abraham in hope believed"** (4:18). {v: 4:18}

He was about a hundred years old. Sarah couldn't have children. He knew it. {v: 4:19}

**"Yet he did not waver through disbelief in the promise of God, but was strengthened in his faith and gave glory to God"** (4:20). {v: 4:20}

He was **"fully persuaded that God was able to do what He had promised"** (4:21). {v: 4:21}

These words weren't written just for Abraham. They are for us, who believe in God who raised Jesus from the dead. {v: 4:23, 4:24}

**"He was delivered over to death for our trespasses and was raised to life for our justification"** (4:25). {v: 4:25}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Romans 4 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Abraham believed God"** (4:3) | Abraham trusted God's promise. |
| **"Credited to him as righteousness"** (4:3) | God counted Abraham as right with him. |
| **"Him who justifies the wicked"** (4:5) | God makes sinners right with him as a gift. |
| **"So that it may rest on grace"** (4:16) | God's promise depends on him, not us. |
| **"Against all hope... in hope believed"** (4:18) | Abraham trusted God when it looked impossible. |
| **"Raised to life for our justification"** (4:25) | Jesus's resurrection seals our right standing with God. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Paul quotes Genesis 15:6.** God promised Abraham descendants like the stars, and Abraham believed.

**David's words come from Psalm 32:1-2**, a psalm about the joy of being forgiven.

**Abraham was honored by Jews.** Some teachers said he was counted righteous because he obeyed. Paul says Scripture shows it was by faith first.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Doesn't God care what we do?** He does. But we don't earn his acceptance by doing. Faith receives it as a gift. Good works follow, the way fruit grows on a tree.

**Did Abraham never doubt?** Abraham laughed at God's promise once (Genesis 17:17). Paul says he didn't give up on the promise. Faith isn't never having questions.

**What does "justify" mean?** To declare someone righteous, like a judge declaring "not guilty."

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"God helps those who help themselves."*

**What's true:** God gives us strength to work and serve.

**What the passage is mainly about:** God counted Abraham righteous because he trusted God's promise, not because he earned it. God **"justifies the wicked"** (4:5). That's the good news.

**Where Jesus is:** Delivered to death for our sins, raised to life to make us right with God (4:25).

## ✝️ Good News

Abraham didn't earn God's favor. He trusted God's promise.

**"Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness"** (4:3).

The same is true for you. When you trust Jesus, God counts you righteous. It rests on grace, so it's guaranteed.

**Abraham believed God, and God counted him righteous. God still justifies sinners as a gift, through faith in Jesus.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther called this teaching, being made right with God by grace through faith, the article on which the church stands or falls. He said Romans 4 shows it plainly in Abraham.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It reads Romans 4:1-5 and 13-17 on the Second Sunday in Lent in Year A, and Romans 4:13-25 on the Second Sunday in Lent in Year B. In Year A, it also reads Romans 4:13-25 in June (Lectionary 10).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness. God's promise rests on grace and is received by faith.**

# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**What it is.** A long, sweeping hymn of creation — from the beginning of light to the last breath of every creature. It follows the order of creation from Genesis.

**What it's about.** God clothes himself in light. He made mountains, springs, ravens, grass, wine, lions, and the sea creature Leviathan. Everything is fed by his open hand. When he takes away their breath, they die; when he sends his Spirit, they are created again.

**When the church sings it.** At Pentecost, when the Holy Spirit is celebrated, and on other Sundays in Ordinary Time.

**How to read it.** Let the images pile up. The psalm is a tour of creation — wonder at every stop.

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Clothed in Majesty

**"He wraps Himself in light as with a garment; He stretches out the heavens like a tent"** (104:2). {v: 104:2, 104:3}

God's clothing is light itself. His home is stretched across the sky. {v: 104:2}

**"He set the earth on its foundations, never to be moved"** (104:5). {v: 104:4, 104:5}

### Section 2: Springs and Grass

Waters covered the earth; at God's command they fled to their place. He sends springs into the valleys. {v: 104:6, 104:7, 104:8, 104:9, 104:10}

**"He makes the grass grow for the livestock and provides crops for man to cultivate, bringing forth food from the earth:"** (104:14) **"wine that gladdens the heart of man, oil that makes his face to shine, and bread that sustains his heart"** (104:15). {v: 104:11, 104:12, 104:13, 104:14, 104:15}

Three gifts from the earth: wine for gladness, oil for health, bread for strength. {v: 104:15}

Trees, birds, and wild goats find their homes in what God made. {v: 104:16, 104:17, 104:18}

### Section 3: The Sea and All Its Creatures

Moon and sun mark the seasons and the day. Darkness comes and the lions prowl. Dawn comes and people go to work. {v: 104:19, 104:20, 104:21, 104:22, 104:23}

**"How many are Your works, O LORD! In wisdom You have made them all; the earth is full of Your creatures"** (104:24). {v: 104:24}

**"Here is the sea, vast and wide, teeming with creatures beyond number, living things both great and small"** (104:25). {v: 104:25, 104:26}

Even Leviathan — the great sea creature — is made to frolic in it. {v: 104:26}

### Section 4: He Opens His Hand

**"All creatures look to You to give them their food in due season"** (104:27). {v: 104:27}

**"When You give it to them, they gather it up; when You open Your hand, they are satisfied with good things"** (104:28). {v: 104:28}

**"When You hide Your face, they are terrified; when You take away their breath, they die and return to dust"** (104:29). {v: 104:29}

**"When You send Your Spirit, they are created, and You renew the face of the earth"** (104:30). {v: 104:30}

The same Spirit who brooded over the waters at creation (Genesis 1:2) still renews the earth. {v: 104:30}

**"I will sing to the LORD all my life; I will sing praise to my God while I have my being"** (104:33). {v: 104:31, 104:32, 104:33}

**"Bless the LORD, O my soul. Hallelujah!"** (104:35). {v: 104:34, 104:35}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Psalm 104 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Clothed with splendor and majesty"** (104:1) | God's character is like a garment — visible in what He wears. |
| **"Stretches out the heavens like a tent"** (104:2) | The sky is God's dwelling place, spread wide. |
| **"Wine that gladdens the heart of man"** (104:15) | Joy is part of God's provision — not an extra. |
| **"When You open Your hand, they are satisfied"** (104:28) | All life depends on God's generosity. |
| **"When You send Your Spirit, they are created"** (104:30) | The Holy Spirit is the life-giver of creation. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**"Leviathan"** (104:26) was a great sea creature, sometimes pictured as a monster in ancient Near Eastern mythology. Here it is just another creature God made — made to frolic, no less.

**The structure** follows the days of Genesis 1: light, sky, land, water, sun and moon, creatures. Creation praise echoes creation itself.

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**"When You take away their breath, they die"** — is this about God killing people? The psalm is about the natural order. All created things have life on loan from God.

**Does God still "send His Spirit" today?** Christians say yes: the Spirit of Pentecost is the same Creator Spirit of Genesis and Psalm 104, still bringing new life.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Nature takes care of itself."*

**What's true:** Creation has its own rhythms and patterns.

**What the passage is mainly about:** **"When You open Your hand, they are satisfied with good things"** (104:28). Every meal, every breath, every spring of water comes from God. Creation is not self-sustaining; it is sustained by God's continuous generosity.

**Where Jesus is:** He is the Word through whom all things were made (John 1:3), and he sends the Spirit who renews the face of the earth (104:30). At Pentecost, the Spirit descended — the Creator Spirit came to dwell among people.

## ✝️ Good News

God opens His hand.

**"When You give it to them, they gather it up; when You open Your hand, they are satisfied with good things"** (104:28).

Every good thing you have — food, breath, light, life — is God's hand open toward you.

**Psalm 104 is a tour of creation: light, water, grass, bread, wine, lions, the sea. Everything lives because God opens His hand. When He sends His Spirit, they are created and the earth is renewed.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther said that in the Lord's Prayer, "give us this day our daily bread" means everything that sustains life — food, home, health, work, weather. All of it is God's open hand.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It sings Psalm 104 on Pentecost Sunday in Years A, B, and C, and on other Sundays in Ordinary Time.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Psalm 104 sings of creation from light to Leviathan. Everything lives by God's open hand. When He sends His Spirit, the earth is renewed — and at Pentecost, the Creator Spirit descended again.**

# The Story

## 🧭 Before You Read

**Who wrote it.** David (29:1).

**What it is.** A psalm about a thunderstorm. David watches a storm roll in from the sea, crash over the mountains, and shake the desert. In every crack of thunder, he hears God's voice.

**When the church sings it.** On the Baptism of Our Lord, when the Father's voice spoke from heaven over the waters of the Jordan.

**How to read it.** Count how many times David says "the voice of the LORD."

## 🗺️ Map of the Psalm

| Section | Verses | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Give God Glory | 29:1-2 | Even heavenly beings worship him |
| 2. The Voice of the LORD | 29:3-9 | A storm sweeps across the land |
| 3. Strength and Peace | 29:10-11 | God reigns over the flood and blesses his people |

## 📖 Section by Section

### Section 1: Give God Glory

**"Ascribe to the LORD, O heavenly beings, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength"** (29:1). {v: 29:1}

Ascribe means to give credit where it's due. {v: 29:1}

**"Worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness"** (29:2). {v: 29:2}

### Section 2: The Voice of the LORD

**"The voice of the LORD is over the waters; the God of glory thunders"** (29:3). {v: 29:3}

**"The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is majestic"** (29:4). {v: 29:4}

The storm moves inland. It snaps the giant cedars of Lebanon. {v: 29:5}

It makes the mountains jump like calves. {v: 29:6}

Lightning flashes. The storm shakes the desert and strips the forest bare. {v: 29:7, 29:8, 29:9}

**"And in His temple all cry, "Glory!""** (29:9). {v: 29:9}

### Section 3: Strength and Peace

**"The LORD sits enthroned over the flood; the LORD is enthroned as King forever"** (29:10). {v: 29:10}

The storm doesn't rule. God does. {v: 29:10}

**"The LORD gives His people strength; the LORD blesses His people with peace"** (29:11). {v: 29:11}

A psalm that starts with thunder ends with peace. {v: 29:11}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Psalm 29 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"Ascribe to the LORD glory and strength"** (29:1) | Give God the honor he deserves. |
| **"The voice of the LORD is over the waters"** (29:3) | God's word rules even the wildest sea. |
| **"Breaks the cedars"** (29:5) | Nothing is too strong for God. |
| **"Enthroned over the flood"** (29:10) | God is king over every chaos. |
| **"Blesses His people with peace"** (29:11) | God's power is used to protect his people. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**The cedars of Lebanon** were the biggest, strongest trees anyone knew.

**Sirion** is another name for Mount Hermon, a tall mountain in the north.

**Neighboring peoples** believed Baal was the storm god. David says the thunder belongs to the LORD.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**Is thunder literally God's voice?** David uses the storm as a picture. God's word is even more powerful than the loudest storm.

**Why read this at Jesus's baptism?** At the Jordan, God's voice spoke over the waters: "This is My beloved Son" (Matthew 3:17).

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| David | In awe | God's voice is powerful (29:4) |
| God's people | At peace | God blesses them with peace (29:11) |

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"Storms show that nobody is in control."*

**What's true:** Storms can be frightening and destructive.

**What the passage is mainly about:** **"The LORD sits enthroned over the flood"** (29:10). Even the storm answers to God, and he uses his power to give his people strength and peace.

**Where Jesus is:** He calmed the wind and waves with a word (Mark 4:39), and at his baptism, the Father's voice spoke over the waters.

## ✝️ Good News

The God of the thunder is the God of peace.

**"The LORD gives His people strength; the LORD blesses His people with peace"** (29:11).

His voice is mighty, and he speaks it for you.

**God's voice thunders over the waters and shakes the earth. He reigns over every flood, and he gives his people strength and peace.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that God's word is never weak or empty. When God speaks, things happen, as when he spoke light into being.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. It sings Psalm 29 on the Baptism of Our Lord in all three years, and on the Holy Trinity in Year B.†

## 💡 Big Idea

**The voice of the LORD thunders over the waters and shakes the earth. He reigns over the flood, and he gives his people strength and peace.**

# The Story

## 🎬 Scene Card

**Cast**

- **Jesus:** The one the Gospels present as the promised Messiah and the Son of God.
- **Jesus's family:** His mother Mary and his brothers. They are worried about him.
- **Scribes from Jerusalem:** Teachers of God's law who have come to check on Jesus.
- **The crowd:** So many people that there is no room to eat.

**Setting**

- A house in Galilee, probably in Capernaum.†

**When**

- About 2,000 years ago, early in Jesus's work in Galilee.

## 🧭 Before the Story

**Crowds were following Jesus everywhere.** He healed many people and drove out evil spirits (3:10-11).

**Jesus chose twelve apostles.** He gave them authority to preach and drive out demons (3:13-15).

**Leaders were starting to plot against him.** After Jesus healed a man on the Sabbath, some leaders made plans to kill him (3:6).

## 🗺️ Map of the Story

| Scene | Verses | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. "Out of his mind" | 3:20-21 | Jesus's family comes to take him home |
| 2. A divided house | 3:22-27 | The scribes accuse Jesus, and he answers |
| 3. A serious warning | 3:28-30 | Jesus speaks about forgiveness |
| 4. "Here are my mother and my brothers" | 3:31-35 | Jesus says who his family is |

## 📝 Plain Retelling

### Scene 1: "Out of His Mind"

Jesus went home. Again a crowd gathered, so many that Jesus and his disciples couldn't even eat. {v: 3:20}

His family heard about it. They went to take him home. {v: 3:21}

**"He is out of His mind"** (3:21), they said. {v: 3:21}

### Scene 2: A Divided House

Some scribes had come down from Jerusalem. They said Jesus had the devil in him. They said that was how he drove out demons. {v: 3:22}

Jesus called them over and spoke to them in pictures. **"How can Satan drive out Satan?"** (3:23). {v: 3:23}

**"If a house is divided against itself, it cannot stand"** (3:25). {v: 3:24, 3:25}

If Satan were fighting himself, his kingdom would fall apart. {v: 3:26}

Then Jesus gave another picture. To rob a strong man's house, you first have to tie up the strong man. {v: 3:27}

This is a comparison. The strong man is Satan. Jesus is stronger. He came to tie up the devil and set people free. {v: 3:27}

### Scene 3: A Serious Warning

**"Truly I tell you, the sons of men will be forgiven all sins and blasphemies, as many as they utter"** (3:28). Blasphemy means speaking against God. {v: 3:28}

But Jesus warned that anyone who blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will never be forgiven. {v: 3:29}

Jesus said this because they were calling the Holy Spirit's work in him evil. {v: 3:30}

### Scene 4: "Here Are My Mother and My Brothers"

Then Jesus's mother and brothers arrived. They stood outside and sent someone in to call him. {v: 3:31}

The crowd sitting around Jesus told him, **"Your mother and brothers are outside, asking for You"** (3:32). {v: 3:32}

**"Who are My mother and My brothers?"** Jesus asked (3:33). {v: 3:33}

He looked at the people sitting in a circle around him. **"Here are My mother and My brothers!"** (3:34). {v: 3:34}

**"For whoever does the will of God is My brother and sister and mother"** (3:35). {v: 3:35}

# Understand It

## 📜 Read It

Mark 3:20-35 (BSB)

## 🔍 What Does This Mean?

| The text says | It means |
|---|---|
| **"He is out of His mind"** (3:21) | Even Jesus's family didn't understand him yet. |
| **"How can Satan drive out Satan?"** (3:23) | The scribes' accusation made no sense. |
| **"Ties up the strong man"** (3:27) | Jesus is stronger than the devil. He came to set people free. |
| **"Will be forgiven all sins"** (3:28) | God's forgiveness is huge. |
| **"Blasphemes against the Holy Spirit"** (3:29) | Calling God's saving work evil and refusing it. |
| **"Whoever does the will of God is My brother and sister and mother"** (3:35) | Jesus welcomes people into God's family. |

## 🌍 Back Then...

**Beelzebul was a name for the devil.** It may come from the name of an old Philistine god.†

**Family honor mattered a lot.** A son acting strangely brought shame on the whole family. That may be why they came to take him home.†

**Later, Jesus's family believed.** After the resurrection, his mother and brothers prayed with the disciples (Acts 1:14). His brother James became a leader in the church.†

## 🚨 Literal Reader Alert: "Wait, what?"

**What is the "unforgivable sin"? Have I done it?** If you are worried that you have, you haven't. The scribes saw God's Spirit at work in Jesus and called it evil. They were pushing God away. Anyone who wants God's forgiveness can have it. Jesus said **"all sins"** will be forgiven (3:28).†

**Was Jesus rejecting his mother?** No. Later, on the cross, Jesus made sure his mother would be cared for (John 19:26-27). Here he is opening his family to everyone.

**Did Jesus have brothers?** The Bible mentions his brothers. Some Christians believe they were Joseph's sons from before, or cousins. Others believe they were Mary's younger sons.†

## 💭 How People Feel

| Who | Feeling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jesus's family | Worried | They thought he was out of his mind (3:21) |
| The scribes | Hostile | They said he had the devil in him (3:22) |
| The crowd | Welcomed | Jesus called them his family (3:34) |

# Why It Matters

## 🙋 Your Turn

- My family doesn't get me :: Jesus's family didn't understand him either. He understands you.

## 🤔 You Might Have Heard...

*"I've done something God can never forgive."*

**What's true:** Sin is serious. Jesus warned the scribes seriously.

**What the story is mainly about:** Jesus is stronger than the devil, and God's forgiveness is huge. If you are worried about being forgiven, that worry shows God's Spirit is still at work in you. And Jesus calls people into his family.

**Where Jesus is:** In a crowded house, looking around at ordinary people and calling them his family.

## ✝️ Good News

Jesus is stronger than the strong man. He came to set captives free.

**"Here are My mother and My brothers! For whoever does the will of God is My brother and sister and mother"** (3:34-35).

Jesus opens his family to everyone. You don't have to earn your place. He calls you his.

**Jesus is stronger than the devil. He forgives, sets people free, and welcomes them into his family.**

## 📚 From Luther

Luther taught that the Christian church is like a mother. Through it, the Holy Spirit gives birth to believers and feeds them with God's word. He called it a family where everyone is brother and sister.†

## 📅 In Church

Many churches follow the Revised Common Lectionary. In Year B, it reads Mark 3:20-35 in early June (Lectionary 10).†

## 💡 Big Idea

**Jesus is stronger than the devil. He forgives, and he calls everyone who follows him his brother, sister, and mother.**
